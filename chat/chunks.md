# Chunk Bank

Natural multi-word expressions worth owning, taken from the learner's own
chat sessions. `Tried` counts how many times a chunk was seeded into a topic;
`Used` counts unprompted productions in a new context.

`Scene` is the situation a chunk lives in, and it is load-bearing: a chunk is
only reachable when the day's topic sits in its scene. Ten sessions of mostly
work topics left every reading chunk untouched - `couldn't put it down` was
seeded 5 times and produced 0 - and then landed on the first reading topic it
ever met. `chunks-due` therefore recommends a scene before it lists chunks.
For genuine discourse markers (`for what it's worth`, `the thing is`) the scene
is `general`: they fit any topic, so scheduling never blocks them.

Status runs `open` -> `pending` -> `owned`. Two unprompted uses make a chunk
`pending`, not `owned`, because both may have happened on home ground in a
topic built to pull for it. `owned` needs one further unprompted use in a
DIFFERENT scene, recorded with `confirm`. A false `owned` is worse than a slow
one: it removes the chunk from circulation while it is still recognition-only.
Maintained by scripts/chat_log.py - do not hand-edit.
