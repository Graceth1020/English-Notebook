# Chunk Repair 01 - Fixed-Phrase Integrity

Date: 2026-10-09 | Track: chunk repair (one-off targeted integrity drill - NOT a form drill and NOT a pattern drill; see form-drills.md boundary note)
Target: C032 make no difference / C037 how big a deal it is / C035 sit on / C053 my parents'' place

## Why this drill

Day 27-28 showed a new failure mode: chunks fully recalled but breaking on
one small word under production - "made no differences" (x3), "how big the
deal it is" (x4), "have sit on" (x2), "my parent''s place" (x3). Seeding
cannot fix this; the ideas are present, the phrase disassembles at the last
phoneme. Material drawn entirely from the learner''s own Day 27-28 sentences.

PASS. The repair chain ran to completion for the apostrophe: broke in
gap-fill (round 1) -> correct with a Chinese cue (round 2) -> correct in
free production (round 3). Per the rules, drill production does not move
chunk state - chat is the exam. The four phrases are now "repaired pending
verification": C032, C037, C035, C053 must survive their next live chat
appearance to count as fixed.
