# Day 59 - A month with nothing scheduled

Date: 2026-11-17 | Category: hypothetical | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 17 | Chunks owned: 104/128 | Chunks credited: 2 | Patterns owned: 7/13

## What We Talked About

A month off with nothing on the calendar turned into one trip: Beijing and Harbin,
four days in Beijing, and an argument about whether a detailed plan saves a trip
or ruins it. He took the pro-plan side and defended it, which was the first
genuine disagreement in three sessions.

Two rows that had been stuck for a week both moved: `hang out` came out in the
first exchange and `in a way that sticks` in the third, in a travel conversation
with no books or lessons anywhere near it.

## The Conversation

---

**1. Me:** You get a month off starting tomorrow - nobody expects anything from you, nothing scheduled. What does the first morning look like, and who's with you?

**You said:**
> If you ask me who's with me on a off day, it'd be my wife. Since nothing scheduled, we can hang out. We've made a plan to travel. We've travelled a lot of places in China so far.

**中文：**如果你问休息日谁陪我，那是我太太。既然没安排，我们可以一起待着。我们计划了一次旅行。到现在我们在中国去过很多地方了。

**Say it like this:**
> If you ask me who's with me on a day off, it'd be my wife. Since nothing's scheduled, we can hang out. We've planned a trip, and we've been to a lot of places in China so far.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | on a off day | on a day off | wrong | 休息日 is `a day off`; `an off day` means a bad day | 休息日是 a day off；an off day 是状态不好的一天 |
| 2 | Since nothing scheduled | Since nothing's scheduled | wrong | The clause needs its verb | 从句缺 be 动词 |
| 3 | We've travelled a lot of places | We've been to a lot of places | wrong | `travel` does not take a place as its object | travel 后面不能直接接地点 |
| 4 | We've made a plan to travel | We've planned a trip | optional | Yours is fine, this is shorter | 你原句没问题，这样更短 |

**Native:** awkward

---

**2. Me:** So the month off is really one trip. Where would you go, and would you plan it day by day or leave the days open?

**You said:**
> We'd go to Beijing and Ha'erbin. Last year, we went to Xinjiang province and didn't make any plan, so the experience was bad. Because of our bad trip, the clearest change is that we should make a detailed plan before travelling. The flights are going fast, so we bought them around 13:00 last Sunday.

**中文：**我们会去北京和哈尔滨。去年去了新疆，完全没做计划，所以体验很差。因为那次糟糕的旅行，最明显的变化是我们出发前要做详细计划。机票卖得很快，所以我们上周日 13 点左右就买了。

**Say it like this:**
> We'd go to Beijing and Harbin. Last year we went to Xinjiang and didn't make any plan, so the trip was rough. Because of that bad trip, the clearest change is that we should make a detailed plan before travelling. The tickets are going fast, so we bought them around 13:00 last Sunday.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | around 13:00 last Sunday | around 13:00 last Sunday | good | Time then day, no extra preposition - `P013` | 时间在前、日期在后，不需要额外介词 |
| 2 | The flights are going fast | The tickets are going fast | wrong | What sells out is the tickets, not the flights | 卖得快的是票，不是航班 |
| 3 | Ha'erbin | Harbin | optional | The English name drops the apostrophe | 英文拼写就是 Harbin |
| 4 | Xinjiang province | Xinjiang | optional | Province names stand alone | 省名通常不加 province |
| 5 | so the experience was bad | so the trip was rough | optional | Yours is fine; this is what people say | 你原句没问题，这是更地道的说法 |

**Native:** slightly off

---

**3. Me:** I'd argue the opposite: a detailed plan is what makes a trip forgettable. Tell me where I'm wrong.

**You said:**
> I'm not with you, I'd say. A detailed plan makes a trip more reasonable. You can go to everywhere you want to go. Mapping out the whole trip can save time. It's a good idea to present attractive places in a way that sticks. On top of that, the tickets of some scenic spots are sold out easily.

**中文：**我不太同意你。详细计划让旅行更合理。你能去到每个想去的地方。把整趟行程梳理清楚能省时间。把有意思的地方用能记住的方式整理出来是个好主意。另外，一些景点的票很容易卖光。

**Say it like this:**
> I'm not with you, I'd say. A detailed plan makes a trip more sensible. You can get to every place you want to go. Mapping out the whole trip can save time. It's a good idea to put the good spots down in a way that sticks. On top of that, the tickets for some sights sell out fast.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | in a way that sticks | in a way that sticks | good | The phrase arrived unprompted - `C058` | 这个表达自己就出来了 |
| 2 | You can go to everywhere you want to go | You can get to every place you want to go | wrong | `everywhere` takes no preposition; `go to` needs a noun | everywhere 前不加介词；go to 后面要名词 |
| 3 | present attractive places | put the good spots down | awkward | `present` takes a person as its object, not a list | present 的对象是人，不是清单 |
| 4 | the tickets of some scenic spots | the tickets for some sights | wrong | A ticket is FOR something, not OF it | 票是 for 某处，不是 of 某处 |
| 5 | are sold out easily | sell out fast | optional | Yours is fine; this is the usual collocation | 你原句没问题，这是常见搭配 |
| 6 | makes a trip more reasonable | makes a trip more sensible | optional | 合理 about a plan is `sensible` | 计划"合理"用 sensible |

**Native:** awkward

---

**4. Me:** Then give me the Beijing part of it: what you do on each of the four days, and how long each of those takes.

**You said:**
> I'll spend four days on Beijing. We'll arrive at Beijing DaXing airport at around 19:30 on the first day. Then we'll go to a hotel. The second day is for Tiananmen Square and the Forbidden city. The third day is for National museum. And the final day is for Universal Studios.

**中文：**我在北京待四天。第一天我们大约 19:30 到大兴机场，然后去酒店。第二天是天安门广场和故宫，第三天是国家博物馆，最后一天是环球影城。

**Say it like this:**
> I'll spend four days in Beijing. We'll arrive at Beijing Daxing Airport at around 19:30 on the first day, and then head to the hotel. Day two is Tiananmen Square and the Forbidden City. The third day is for the National Museum, and the final day is for Universal Studios.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | spend four days on Beijing | spend four days in Beijing | wrong | Time is spent IN a place | 在某地待时间是 in |
| 2 | National museum | the National Museum | wrong | The article and capitals are part of the name | 名字本身带 the，也要大写 |
| 3 | The second day is for Tiananmen Square | Day two is Tiananmen Square | optional | Yours is fine, this is quicker | 你原句没问题，这样更利落 |
| 4 | Then we'll go to a hotel | then head to the hotel | optional | `head to` is the spoken verb for this | 口语里常说 head to |

**Native:** slightly off

---

**5. Me:** Last one: give me the museum day in a single answer - how many hours it runs, and what you cut if it runs long.

**You said:**
> About five hours inside, and if it runs long I'd cut the shopping afterwards and move it to the last day.

**中文：**在里面待大约五个小时，如果时间拖长了，我就把后面的购物砍掉，挪到最后一天。

**Say it like this:**
> About five hours inside, and if it runs long I'd cut the shopping afterwards and move it to the last day.

**Native:** clean
