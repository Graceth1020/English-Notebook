Date: 2026-09-11 | Category: work | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 24 | Chunks owned: 2/69 | Chunks credited: 4 | Patterns used: 1

## What We Talked About

Wikis and documentation culture. Your team turned out to be the opposite of my
friend's: docs are mandatory, maintained, and valued over the tech itself - and
the reason is a production incident last year where a stale doc meant nobody
noticed the API's refresh flow had changed until users could not pay. The habit
started on your team and spread until it became company culture.

## Worth Stealing

- **"be big on (something)"** - *our team has been big on docs*. Yours,
  produced unprompted, and exactly how natives say "特别重视". Not banked -
  the freeze holds.
- **"up to date" vs "newest/latest"** - 最新 is one word in Chinese but two in
  English. Docs and information are `up to date`; versions and products are
  `the latest` / `the newest one`. And the `up` in `up to date` cannot be
  dropped - that happened twice today.
