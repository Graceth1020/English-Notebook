# Day 20 - Do you need to be passionate about your job?

Date: 2026-09-18 | Category: opinion | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 22 | Chunks owned: 9/76 | Chunks credited: 9 | Patterns owned: 2/7

## What We Talked About

A light debate about whether you need to love your job. You argued you do - passion is where the drive comes from, and it pays - then built the real position: a self-reinforcing cycle of passion, learning and salary, which under the current economy resolves into "a steady job beats a high one." The closing answer, after a concession: you want to be up to the job so you can leave work early, and switching off should be the norm, not a luxury.
