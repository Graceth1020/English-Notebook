## What We Talked About

The technical interview you still remember from just before graduation: a young interviewer with no hair and a pair of glasses, clearly an expert in his field, an hour of Spring and MyBatis questions. The one question that stuck was whether you had run into serious problems in production - you told the story of a backed-up message queue that you ran past your classmates, tracked down over half an hour, and finally got to the bottom of (network congestion). After the technical part came the hobby question: reading, because you wanted to be up to the job, and people who know a lot are in demand in the job market. You had just bought Clean Code, were halfway through, and hoped to get through it by the end of the quarter. The offer arrived a month later - but replaying the interview in your head, you realised it could have gone better. Your advice to a junior: build up enough technical knowledge first, then keep a clear head.
