## Worth Stealing

- `C001` **"couldn't put it down"** - *the* idiom for a book too good to stop. "I started it Friday night and couldn't put it down."
- `C002` **"be really into (something)"** - the everyday way to say you're passionate about it. "I was really into suspense novels."
- `C003` **"flush out your system"** - the fixed phrase for the health idea. "A big glass of water flushes out your system."
- `C004` **"get through (a book/task)"** - finish it at pace. "I got through it in three nights."
- `C005` **"a luxury"** - something you can rarely afford the time for. "Reading for two hours straight is a luxury now."
- `C006` **"it comes down to (X)"** - name the real cause, casually. "Honestly, it just comes down to time."

Also visible across the rewrites: **"take up most of my time"** beats "occupy much
time", **"big on health"** beats "pay attention to our health", and **"a whole
stack of"** beats "a great number of". Same move each time - a person's words
instead of a textbook's.
