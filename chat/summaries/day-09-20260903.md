  and four consecutive sessions (`chat:E031`). Next review pushed to 2026-10-03 by the
  scheduler, which is too far out given the streak; consider drilling it directly
  rather than waiting for a topic to catch it.
- **Two words where English has one** - *the form style* for `format`. Same move as
  *the change of that ticket* (Day 05) and *a set of advanced devices* (Day 07): when
  the exact word is missing, the gap gets filled with a noun phrase built from parts.
  This is the recall-vs-recognition problem in its purest form.
- **`good for you` as a verdict** - back after eight days (Day 01: *water is good for
  our health*). The frame is "X is good", where English wants "X is what decides it".
- **Register was low all session** - no report-register lines at all, which is a first.
  Across nine sessions: 27 of 50 exchanges. The turns were short and concrete, and
