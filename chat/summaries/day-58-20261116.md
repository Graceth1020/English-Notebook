# Day 58 - Teaching the new hire

Date: 2026-11-16 | Category: work | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 9 | Chunks owned: 104/128 | Chunks credited: 0 | Patterns owned: 7/13

## What We Talked About

Eight minutes, one-line answers: a new hire with one hour of your time before
they are on their own. He defended the docs (the company keeps every page
current), chose pairing over reading, put the timeline at "a week" and the path
at "environment on day one, then a ticket".

The format changed the shape of the day rather than its value: five exchanges
with no room for narration means no post-noun hangers and no stacked time, but
the decision question and the condition question both landed exactly where the
seeded error rows needed them.

## The Conversation

---

**1. Me:** New hire starts tomorrow and you have one hour with them before they're on their own. What do you actually do in that hour?

**You said:**
> I'll give them a wiki URL and tell them which docs are important to read. Besides, there are some flowcharts that can help them sort out the business process.

**中文：**我会给他们一个 wiki 链接，告诉他们哪些文档重要需要读。另外还有一些流程图，能帮他们理清业务流程。

**Say it like this:**
> I'll give them a wiki URL and tell them which docs are important to read. Besides, there are some flowcharts that can help them sort out the business process.

**Native:** clean

---

**2. Me:** A wiki URL is where knowledge goes to die - most new hires skim it and ask the same three questions for a month. What actually works on them in that hour?

**You said:**
> Actually, our company culture is keeping every doc up to date, so it's still reading the doc. And don't read too much to my answer. If there is something unclear, I'll tell it.

**中文：**其实我们公司的文化是让每份文档都保持最新，所以还是要读文档。别对我这个回答想太多。如果有不清楚的地方，我会说。

**Say it like this:**
> Actually, our company culture is keeping every doc up to date, so reading them is still the way. And don't read too much into my answer. If something's unclear, I'll say so.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | don't read too much to my answer | don't read too much into my answer | wrong | The preposition is `into`, never `to` | 介词是 into，不是 to |
| 2 | If there is something unclear, I'll tell it | If something's unclear, I'll say so | wrong | `tell` takes a person, not a thing | tell 后面跟人，不跟事 |
| 3 | so it's still reading the doc | so reading them is still the way | awkward | Singular `doc` for all of them, and the clause hangs | doc 是单数，且句子悬空 |
| 4 | our company culture is keeping every doc up to date | our culture is keeping every doc up to date | optional | `company` is implied | 你原句没问题，company 可省 |

**Native:** awkward

---

**3. Me:** Fine - so give me your call: in that one hour, which one carries the weight, the docs or sitting next to somebody? Your call, not a preference.

**You said:**
> It's better to sit next to somebody, I'd say. In this way, we can do things efficient.

**中文：**我觉得最好是坐在别人旁边。这样我们做事情效率更高。

**Say it like this:**
> It's better to sit next to somebody, I'd say. That way we can do things efficiently.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | In this way | That way | bookish | `in this way` is written register; speech says `that way` | in this way 偏书面，口语说 that way |

He caught the adjective himself - `efficient` became `efficiently` in the same
turn, so there is no row for it.

**Native:** bookish

**中文：**看这事有多大。如果业务复杂，一周就够了。

**5. Me:** You gave me the clock but not the path. What happens inside that week - what do they touch on day one, and what do you let them own by day five?

**中文：**新人第一天都是装环境，等他熟悉了业务流程我就给他派 ticket。

**Say it like this:**

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
