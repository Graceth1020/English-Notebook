## Worth Stealing

- **"got pulled into a meeting"** — the spoken alternative to `attended` or `went to`. It carries the real meaning: you did not choose to be there.
- **"keep something running"** — the casual version of `maintain`. A server, a system, a habit. Maintain is what the docs say; keep running is what the engineer says.
- **"a new joiner"** — the noun for someone who started recently. Not `a newer`.
