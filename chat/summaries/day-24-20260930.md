## What We Talked About

The coupon bug from three years ago - amounts stored in cents, displayed in yuan, ten times the actual value on the page. You tracked it down from the logs, told your leader straight away, and shipped a fix the same evening. The lasting part is what it changed: a docs-first discipline that became company culture, and a wiki that is actually kept up to date. Three years on, no bug has beaten it - precisely because the lesson worked.
