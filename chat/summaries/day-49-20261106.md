# Day 49 - The tool you'd refuse to give up

Date: 2026-11-06 | Category: work | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 16 | Chunks owned: 74/128 | Chunks credited: 4 | Patterns owned: 6/13

## What We Talked About

One laptop, five years, no plan to replace it, and then the shop where he would
replace it. The site turned out to be laid out by requirement rather than by
brand, with sellers who stay on the hook after the parcel arrives. It ended on
the tablet he bought there for video editing and the sold-out problem he cannot
do anything about.

## The Conversation

---

**1. Me:** Our team's been shopping for new tools and everyone's pushing a different favourite. What's the one you'd refuse to give up, and how did you end up on it?

**You said:**
> I refuse to give up my laptop which is still high-spec though. I've used it for nearly five years, and I'm familiar with the system. The only situation I'd give it up is it's broken.

**中文：**我不愿意放弃我的笔记本电脑，它现在还是高配的。我用了快五年了，也熟悉这套系统。唯一会放弃它的情形就是它坏了。

**Say it like this:**
> I refuse to give up my laptop - it's still high-spec. I've had it for nearly five years and I know the system inside out. The only way I'd give it up is if it broke.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | my laptop which is still high-spec though | my laptop - it's still high-spec | awkward | "though" is a sentence-level contrast word; it cannot close a relative clause. | though 是整句的转折词，不能挂在定语从句后面。 |
| 2 | The only situation I'd give it up is it's broken | The only way I'd give it up is if it broke | wrong | The clause after "is" needs if, and "the only way... is if" is the frame people actually use. | is 后面要接 if 从句，固定说法是 the only way ... is if。 |

**Native:** slightly off

**2. Me:** Five years is a long run. When you bought it, did you try anything else out first, or did you just go with the one you'd already read about?

**You said:**
> Honestly, I'd try out something else. I had a Lenovo computer before, and when I've got to buy a new one, I will get something off a platform called Computer country. Many people recommend their computers there, and you can get all sorts.

**中文：**说实话，我会试试别的。我之前用的是联想，到要买新的时候，我会在一个叫 Computer Country 的平台上买。很多人在那里推荐电脑，各种都有。

**Say it like this:**
> Honestly, I'd try out something else. I had a Lenovo before, and when it's time to buy a new one I'd get something off a platform called Computer Country. Many people recommend laptops on there, and you can get all sorts.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | I will get something | I'd get something | wrong | The answer started with "I'd try out" - the earlier would has to carry through the whole reply. | 前半句用了 I'd try out，后面所有动作都要跟着用 would。 |
| 2 | when I've got to buy a new one | when it's time to buy a new one | awkward | "have got to" reports an obligation you have now, so it cannot set up a future occasion. | have got to 是"现在不得不"，不能当将来的时间状语。 |
| 3 | recommend their computers there | recommend laptops on there | awkward | You recommend something ON a site or ON there, and "their" leaves it unclear whose computers. | 推荐东西用 on a site / on there；their 指代不清。 |
| 4 | I had a Lenovo computer before | I had a Lenovo before | optional | Both are fine - dropping "computer" is what most people say once the brand has said it for them. | 两种都对，但说了牌子之后大多数人不再说 computer。 |

**Native:** slightly off

**3. Me:** So it sounds like a community rather than a shop. Is it the same setup as a normal marketplace, or does someone actually vet the sellers?

**You said:**
> It's the same as a normal market. A buyer finds what he needs and pays for an order. Then the seller sends the product. Sellers get involved in all process. You have to answer the buyer's questions, send the product he buys and do a good job in after-sales service.

**中文：**它跟普通市场一样。买家找到自己需要的东西，下单付款，然后卖家发货。卖家要参与整个过程：回答买家的问题、发货、售后也要做好。

**Say it like this:**
> It's the same as a normal market. A buyer finds what he needs, places an order and pays for it. Then the seller sends the product. Sellers get involved in the whole process - you have to answer the buyer's questions, send what he ordered and do a good job on after-sales.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | pays for an order | places an order and pays for it | awkward | You place an order; paying comes after, and it is paid for, not paid for an order. | 下单是 place an order，付款是后面的事。 |
| 2 | in all process | in the whole process | wrong | all with a singular noun is ungrammatical; one process takes the whole. | all 后面要接复数名词；单数一个流程用 the whole。 |
| 3 | do a good job in after-sales service | do a good job on after-sales | awkward | You do a good job ON a piece of the work, not in it. | 把某一块工作做好用 on，不是 in。 |

**Native:** slightly off

**4. Me:** Simpler version: is that site mostly about ordinary laptops for everyday use, or does it also cover the heavy machines people buy for work?

**You said:**
> It includes all kinds of laptops. You can write your requirements down and ask the customer service stuff. On top of that, there are many different sections for specific requirements. It's easy to find what you need.

**中文：**各种笔记本都有。你可以把需求写下来问客服。除此之外还有很多按具体需求分的板块，很容易找到自己要的。

**Say it like this:**
> It includes all kinds of laptops. You can write your requirements down and send them to customer service. On top of that, there are many sections for specific requirements, so it's easy to find what you need.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | the customer service stuff | the customer service staff | wrong | staff is the people who work there; stuff is things. | staff 是人，stuff 是东西。 |
| 2 | write your requirements down and ask the customer service staff | write your requirements down and send them to customer service | optional | Both work - but ask leaves it unclear what you are asking them for, and send is what you actually do. | 两种都行，但 ask 没说清问什么，send 更贴实际动作。 |

**Native:** good

**5. Me:** Would you point a colleague at that site too, even someone who needs the machine for serious work all day, or would you send them somewhere else?

**You said:**
> I'd recommend this site to everyone who wants to buy a new laptop. As for me, I had a good shopping experience at this site. I bought a high-spec pad to edit videos before, and it really reached my demand. I couldn't put it down whenever I take new videos. And if you'd refund something, the customer service staff would deal with it at once. The only bad thing is popular products are always sold out.

**中文：**我会把这个网站推荐给所有想买新笔记本的人。就我来说，在那里购物体验很好。我以前买过一台高配的平板用来剪视频，确实满足了我的需求。每次拍新视频我都离不开它。如果要退款，客服会马上处理。唯一的缺点就是热门产品总是卖光。

**Say it like this:**
> I'd recommend this site to everyone who wants to buy a new laptop. As for me, I had a good shopping experience there. I bought a high-spec tablet to edit videos, and it really meets my needs. I couldn't put it down whenever I shoot a new video. And if you want a refund, the customer service staff would deal with it right away. The only bad thing is popular products are always sold out.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | a high-spec pad | a high-spec tablet | wrong | pad is not the word for it; the device is a tablet. | 说平板用 tablet，pad 不是这个词。 |
| 2 | it really reached my demand | it really meets my needs | calque | 需求 splits two ways: demand is what a market wants, needs is what a person has. | 需求分两种：demand 是市场需求，个人的需求是 needs。 |
| 3 | whenever I take new videos | whenever I shoot a new video | calque | You shoot video, take photos. | 拍视频是 shoot，拍照片才是 take。 |
| 4 | if you'd refund something | if you want a refund | wrong | You do not refund something - the shop refunds you; you return it or ask for the money back. | refund 是商家退钱给你，你不能"退还某物"。 |
| 5 | would deal with it at once | would deal with it right away | optional | Your at once is correct and it is banked as C115 - the "immediately" sense is only a notch more formal than right away, so keep it. | 你的 at once 本身是对的（C115），"立刻"这个意思只比 right away 正式一点，继续用就行。 |
