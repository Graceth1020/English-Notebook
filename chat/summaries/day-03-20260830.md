## Worth Stealing

- `C014` **"never get round to it"** - keep meaning to do something and never do. "I've always meant to play it, I just never get round to it."
- `C015` **"I've always meant to (do X)"** - a standing intention never acted on. "I've always meant to pick that game back up."
- `C016` **"get something off (a platform)"** - find content on an app. "I got this recipe off Rednote."
- `C017` **"all sorts"** - the spoken replacement for "various". "People post all sorts on there."
- `C018` **"sold out"** - always with "be", and plural for shorts/jeans/glasses. "I went back for them but they were sold out."
