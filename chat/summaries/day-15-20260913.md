## What We Talked About

Meetings, and whether they earn their time. Your team turned out to be the
disciplined one again: agendas before every meeting, meetings that end on
time, and a weekly all-hands you killed and replaced with per-project

## Worth Stealing

- **"set an agenda"** - the meeting version of a todo list. *Before beginning a meeting, we always set an agenda.*

- **"Sorry, I wasn't clear. Let me put it differently."** - yours now. The repair move for when the listener has misunderstood you, produced unprompted today.

## What This Session Proved

Three stalled chunks moved on the day their scene was scheduled, including
C004, which had survived five seedings. The scene system keeps predicting
which chunks are reachable and being right.

The more interesting signal is the resolutions. E033 and E035 are the first
chat errors ever resolved, and both closed the same way: the fix from the
error row appeared unprompted, in a later session, in a different topic. That
is the exact mechanism the whole practice is built on - recognition converted
to recall with a time gap. P004 reaching `owned` the same day is the pattern
version of the same event.

The new family to watch is the **-ing subject**: `To do things effectively
is...` (exchange 2), `Every time we do what we enjoy helps...` (exchange 5),

(Drill 06 round 2) are one mechanism - the subject slot filled by something
English does not allow there. Three sightings in two days; at four it earns
an error row of its own.
