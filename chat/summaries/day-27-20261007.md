# Day 27 - The National Day break

Date: 2026-10-07 | Category: daily | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 23 | Chunks owned: 38/78 | Chunks credited: 11 | Patterns owned: 6/9

## What We Talked About

A full switch-off week - not one second on work. The drive home on October 2nd: everyone heading in the same direction, traffic brutal, a four-hour drive that took six and a half. The hours at the wheel went to music, of a very on-brand kind: points farmed from Tencent's Yuanbao app promotion, redeemed for a one-day QQ Music pass - and sometimes silence, because that way he could stay focused on long-distance driving. At his parents' place, time with them came first; talking was a luxury after working far away from home for so long, catching up on what had happened, telling them the problems he'd run into over the past few months. Their advice was the universal parent script: your health comes first; no matter what happens, nothing else really matters. The return trip beat the rush - leaving early on the 6th, six hours instead of last year's ten. That's after the fact, though: from now on, he returns early every year.
