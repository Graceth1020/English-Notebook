# Day 48 - Remote vs office, honestly

Date: 2026-11-05 | Category: opinion | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 25 | Chunks owned: 74/128 | Chunks credited: 12 | Patterns owned: 6/13

## What We Talked About

Remote versus office, from a straight no to the reason that actually mattered. The
first half was rent and commute; by the end it was friends, a board-game table, and
parents in the hometown. It closed on the practical side of work - what to do with
a client who keeps asking for the same feature.

## The Conversation

---

**1. Me:** Our team's been going back and forth about whether we should be in the office more. If your company pushed for full-time office again, would you go along with it, or is your home setup too good to give up?

**You said:**
> No, I won't agree with it. A remote work allows me to work at my parents' place. I don't need to rent another house. If the company pushed for full-time office gain, I could only get back home twice a year. On top of that, I have to switch up my routine because of the commute. So a full-time work really gets in the way of my life quality.

**中文：**我不会同意的。远程办公让我可以在我爸妈那里上班，不用再租一套房子。如果公司要求全职回办公室，那我一年只能回家两次。而且通勤会打乱我的作息，所以全职上班确实影响我的生活质量。

**Say it like this:**
> No, I wouldn't agree to it. Remote work allows me to work from my parents' place. I don't need to rent another house. If the company pushed for full-time office again, I could only get back home twice a year. On top of that, I'd have to switch up my routine because of the commute. So full-time work really gets in the way of my quality of life.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | I won't agree with it | I wouldn't agree to it | wrong | You agree TO a plan and WITH a person; and the whole answer is a hypothetical, so it wants would, not won't. | 同意某个方案用 agree to，同意某个人用 agree with；整句是假设，所以要用 would。 |
| 2 | A remote work allows me | Remote work allows me | wrong | work is uncountable here - no article, the same as "traffic" or "equipment". | work 在这个意思上不可数，不能加 a。 |
| 3 | work at my parents' place | work from my parents' place | awkward | You work FROM a place - your desk is there, the job is elsewhere. | 在某地上班用 work from，不是 work at。 |
| 4 | full-time office gain | full-time office again | wrong | gain is not the word you want; again marks the second time it happens. | gain 不是这个词，这里要 again（又一次）。 |
| 5 | I have to switch up my routine | I'd have to switch up my routine | awkward | After "if the company pushed", the consequence has to stay in the same conditional. | 前面是 if 引导的假设，后面的结果也要用 would。 |
| 6 | a full-time work | full-time work | wrong | Same rule as row 2 - work takes no article. | 和第二条一样，work 不加冠词。 |
| 7 | my life quality | my quality of life | calque | The fixed phrase is quality of life; Chinese puts the noun first. | 固定说法是 quality of life，中文语序反了。 |

**Native:** wrong

**2. Me:** Fair enough - the commute argument is hard to beat. But honestly, when there's nobody in the room, do you actually get more done at home, or does the day just drift?

**You said:**
> When I work at home, I turn off the electric devices to stay focus. I see the work as a high-priority item. At first, it's hard to concentrate when I was working at home. That's down to no supervision. So it's essential to turn off the devices. In terms of the work efficiency, full-time work is more acceptable. I can arrange my time more flexible.

**中文：**在家工作时我会关掉电子设备来保持专注。我把工作当成高优先级的事。刚开始在家工作时很难集中注意力，因为没人监督，所以关掉设备很有必要。从工作效率来说，全职工作更能接受，我可以更灵活地安排时间。

**Say it like this:**
> When I work from home, I turn off my devices to stay focused. I see the work as a high-priority item. At first, it was hard to concentrate when I was working from home. That's down to no supervision. So it's essential to turn off the devices. In terms of work efficiency, full-time work suits me better. I can arrange my time more flexibly.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | work at home (twice, same turn) | work from home | awkward | The fixed phrase is work from home - the same repair as exchange 1, row 3. | 固定说法是 work from home，和第一轮第三行同一个错误。 |
| 2 | the electric devices | my devices | calque | electric is for motors and wiring; a phone or laptop is just a device. | electric 指带电的东西（马达、线路），手机电脑就是 device。 |
| 3 | to stay focus | to stay focused | wrong | stay takes the -ed form here - it is a state, not an action. | stay 后面接形容词 focused，不是动词原形。 |
| 4 | At first, it's hard | At first, it was hard | wrong | "At first" puts you in the past, so the verb has to follow. | At first 指向过去，动词要跟着用过去时。 |
| 5 | In terms of the work efficiency | In terms of work efficiency | calque | efficiency is uncountable, so no the. | efficiency 不可数，不加 the。 |

| 7 | more flexible | more flexibly | wrong | It describes how you arrange, so it needs the adverb. | 它修饰 arrange（怎么安排），要用副词 flexibly。 |

**3. Me:** That flips the usual argument - most people say the office wins on focus, not efficiency. Whose job is it, though, when someone on the team goes quiet for a week and nothing moves: theirs or the manager's?

**You said:**
> Both two of them should be responsible for them, I'd say. As a manger, it really matters to manage the project progress. If someone on the team has been quiet for a long time, the manger has to ask the progress actively. As a employee, you should push things forward. Whenever something gets in the way of the progress, you've got to put something in place to solve it.

**中文：**我觉得两边都有责任。作为经理，管好项目进度很重要。如果有人一直很安静，经理得主动去问进度。作为员工，你应该推进事情；一旦有东西阻碍进度，就得想办法解决。

**Say it like this:**
> Both of them should be responsible for it, I'd say. As a manager, managing project progress really matters. If someone on the team has been quiet for a long time, the manager has to proactively ask about progress. As an employee, you should push things forward. Whenever something gets in the way of the progress, you've got to put something in place to solve it.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Both two of them | Both of them | wrong | both already means the two - the number is redundant. | both 本身就是"两个"，不用再加 two。 |
| 2 | responsible for them | responsible for it | wrong | One situation is at stake, so it is singular. | 指的是一件事，所以用 it。 |
| 3 | the manger (twice) | the manager | wrong | Spelling - a manger is the box animals eat from. | 拼写错误，manger 是马槽。 |
| 4 | it really matters to manage project progress | managing project progress really matters | awkward | The thing that matters is the subject; "matters to" is reserved for who it matters to. | matter 的主语是"要紧的那件事"，to 后面只能接"对谁要紧"。 |
| 5 | ask the progress | ask about progress | calque | 问进度: ask needs about before the thing you ask about. | 问进度要说 ask about，不能直接接宾语。 |
| 6 | actively | proactively | calque | actively means with energy; you mean on your own initiative. | actively 是"用力地"，这里要"主动地"proactively。 |
| 7 | As a employee | As an employee | wrong | a becomes an before a vowel sound. | 元音开头用 an。 |

**Native:** wrong

**4. Me:** Fair - responsibility on both sides. Here's a harder one: a client keeps pushing for a feature we've already turned down twice, and he won't drop it. What do you do with someone like that?

**You said:**
> If the client won't take no for an answer, we should talk it through seriously. We can't push back on a feature a client really wants. I have to write things down and hand it off to the business side to evaluate its necessity.

**中文：**如果客户死活不接受拒绝，我们就得认真谈一谈。客户真正想要的功能，我们不能直接顶回去。我会把需求写下来，交给业务方去评估必要性。

**Say it like this:**
> If the client won't take no for an answer, we should talk it through seriously. We can't push back on a feature a client really wants. I have to write things down and hand it off to the business side to work out whether it's really needed.

**5. Me:** Last one. Say the company offered to pay for a proper home setup - standing desk, second monitor, the works. Would that make staying home an easier yes for you?

**You said:**
> No, it isn't. I still prefer to work from home. I have many friends who I can round up to play board games on weekends. They are living in my hometown. Playing board games doubles as a way for me to hang out. Sometimes we eat out to catch up on what's happened recently. What's more, I can always talk to my parents. Taking with people way above my level helps me broaden my horizons. My dad is an investor who aims for steady returns over the long run. He believes that good companies will reward their investors with excess returns. It's a luxury to learn a lot from them.

**中文：**不会。我还是更喜欢在家工作。我有很多朋友可以叫来一起玩桌游，他们住在我老家。玩桌游也是我们聚在一起的方式。有时我们会出去吃饭，聊聊最近发生的事。而且我随时可以跟我爸妈聊天。跟水平比我高很多的人交流能开阔眼界。我爸是投资人，追求长期稳定回报，他相信好公司会用超额回报回报投资者。能从他们身上学到很多东西，这很难得。

**Say it like this:**
> No, it wouldn't. I still prefer to work from home. I have many friends who I can round up to play board games on weekends. They live in my hometown. Playing board games doubles as a way for me to hang out. Sometimes we eat out to catch up on what's happened recently. What's more, I can always talk to my parents. Talking with people way above my level helps me broaden my horizons. My dad is an investor who aims for steady returns over the long run. He believes that good companies will reward their investors with excess returns. It's a luxury to learn a lot from them.

**What changed:**
