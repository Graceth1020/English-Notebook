# Day 51 - Thrown in with no preparation

Date: 2026-11-08 | Category: daily | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 26 | Chunks owned: 87/128 | Chunks credited: 22 | Patterns owned: 6/13

## What We Talked About

The question was about being dropped into something with nobody to show you how,
and the answer went straight back to first-year coding: a framework that traces a
whole request across every system, built with no front end, learned by asking
people two levels above him. Then the queue that silently stopped sending SMS,
the teacher whose fix he refused, and the book that made him want to argue back.

**1. Me:** Was there a time you got dropped into something you'd never done before, with nobody around to show you how?

**You said:**
> Yeah, that was coding. When I was in colleague, I was into coding. A freshman wanted to try out everything. Since I was major in computer silence, the thing was coding. I saw it a high-priority thing in my life. As we know, coding is a matter of thinking. Sometimes you spend a long time tracking down a bug. Let me through the worst bug I'd ever shipped when I was a student. After shipping a new feature, the system failed to send SMS anymore. And it came down to the queue was backed up. Everyone in the team got involved in the process of locating the problem. From then, I've been big on code review, and the clearest change is that such things never happen ever. Review really matters to every tech team. Moreover, I have a project kicked off when I was a junior but it hasn't been done by now.

**中文：**对，就是写代码。大学时我就喜欢写代码，大一那年什么都想试一遍。因为我专业是计算机，那件事就是写代码。我把它看成生活里优先级很高的事。大家都知道，写代码本质上是思考。有时候你会花很长时间去追一个 bug。让我讲讲我学生时代发布过的最严重的 bug。上线一个新功能之后，系统突然发不出短信了。根因是队列堵住了。团队所有人都参与了定位这个问题。从那以后我很重视代码评审，最明显的变化是这种事再也没发生过。评审对每个技术团队都很重要。另外，我大三启动的一个项目到现在还没做完。

**Say it like this:**
> Yeah, that was coding. In college I was into coding - as a freshman I wanted to try everything. Since I majored in computer science, the thing was coding. I saw it as a high-priority thing in my life. As everyone knows, coding is a matter of thinking. Sometimes you spend a long time tracking down a bug. Let me talk you through the worst bug I'd ever shipped when I was a student. After shipping a new feature, the system stopped sending SMS. And it came down to the queue being backed up. **What changed:**

**Say it like this:**

**What changed:**
