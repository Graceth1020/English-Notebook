## Worth Stealing

- **"I know the feeling"** — the phrase for "same here" in English, replacing
  the calque `I have the same feeling`.
- **"get back to someone"** — the general-purpose spoken alternative to
  `reply/respond to someone`. Covers messages, calls, emails, all of it.
