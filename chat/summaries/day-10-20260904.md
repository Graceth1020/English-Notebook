# Day 10 - The incident review

Date: 2026-09-04 | Category: work | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 17 | Chunks owned: 3/65 | Patterns: P001 attempted

## What We Talked About

A roleplay post-mortem: the SMS provider migration you shipped on Friday broke
verification codes for two hours on Saturday, and your team lead wants to know why.
He pushed twice on the same thing - every explanation pointed somewhere else (the
config, the queue, the network) and none of them said what should have been different
before Friday. Exchange 3 is where that turned, and it was the only clean turn of the
session.

Then a second problem: marketing wants the same code path used on Thursday. You
estimated two days and asked for the following Monday, which do not match, and he
caught it.

Three chunks landed, one of them promoted to `owned`, and `P001` was attempted
unprompted for the second day running. Best chunk session of the ten.
