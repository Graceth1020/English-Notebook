# Day 26 - A habit you're trying to build (or break)

Date: 2026-10-03 | Category: daily | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 22 | Chunks owned: 31/78 | Chunks credited: 12 | Patterns owned: 6/9

## What We Talked About

The habit is learning itself. Two and a half years of English: a novel bought online after the bookstore sold out, already halfway through, to be finished by the end of the quarter; English articles on Reddit; these chat drills every day. The habit nearly died once in its old form - a year ago you learned ten new words presented in a way that stuck, and found it useless, because you only knew what a word meant, not how to use it. That insight rebuilt the whole approach: words and chunks in use, not in lists. A study day now: review yesterday's errors first, then about an hour of drills, podcasts on weeknights. The podcast of choice is DownieLive on YouTube - train travel, local culture - and unknown words are allowed to wash over you, because wired before bed gets in the way of sleeping. The upgrade: jot the new words down, learn them afterwards. Let's aim to do that in the next few months.
