# Day 31 - The broken lock

Date: 2026-10-11 | Category: daily | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 21 | Chunks owned: 51/101 | Chunks credited: 8 | Patterns owned: 6/9

## What We Talked About

The sick-day topic was set aside for a better story: fresh out of university, broke on 5000 a month in a rented Foshan flat, he came home from work to a broken lock. A thousand yuan to fix - a fifth of a month's pay, small change for some people. The landlord called it none of his business; there was no way to fight it, so he paid it on his own. The tuition bought a rule: paper before trust - if the split of responsibilities is just agreed on over a chat, he doesn't buy it. Money today means safety, not things: earning more than before, he feels safer. And four sentences back to that fresh graduate: talk with people outside your field (it broadens your horizons), read books that keep you up to the job, learn to invest for steady returns over the long run, and stick with things you love - everyone should have at least one hobby.
