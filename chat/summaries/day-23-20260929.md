# Day 23 - City or small town?

Date: 2026-09-29 | Category: opinion | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 22 | Chunks owned: 23/77 | Chunks credited: 17 | Patterns owned: 4/9

## What We Talked About

City or small town. You picked Foshan on commute, company and colleagues - then conceded under one question that the city was never the point: being close to your parents is. The dream version is remote work from their place, but it has stayed a dream - you have always meant to find a remote job and never get round to it. The closing answer: what makes next year real is probably a new job, because a team going remote is downstream of the work itself.
