# Day 33 - Cooking or takeout

Date: 2026-10-17 | Category: daily | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 14 | Chunks owned: 57/108 | Chunks credited: 11 | Patterns owned: 6/9

## What We Talked About

Weekday dinner reality: eating at the canteen because getting off work early never happens, meaning to cook but never getting round to it, living far from his parents' place. The canteen ran a points promotion for a one-week pass. Five years of the same dishes prompted the idea that they should switch up the menu - and even free food wouldn't save a bad canteen. The home repertoire is a beef steak and a bowl of noodles; a recipe book with hundreds of recipes has been sitting on his desk untouched. For guests he'd have to add stir-fried broccoli with garlic. He has eaten out all week but will cook Saturday anyway, because canteen food, like any restaurant's, uses too much oil.
