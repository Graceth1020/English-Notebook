## Worth Stealing

- `C037` **"how big a deal it is"** - the spoken way to gauge size or urgency. "It depends on how big a deal it is."
- `C038` **"that's down to (X)"** - attribute a cause in four short words. "That's down to how my leader plans it."
- `C033` **"company culture"** - fixed compound, no possessive.
- `C034` **"work-life balance"** - fixed compound, that order.
- `C035` **"sit on something"** - hold it without acting. "He's been sitting on my PR for three days."
- `C036` **"read something into it"** - infer a meaning that may not be there. "Don't read too much into it."
- `C039` **"be up to the job"** - from the redo. Usually negative: "Am I just not up to the job?"
- `C040` **"out of my depth"** - beyond what you can handle. "I was completely out of my depth in that meeting."

- **`reply` without `to`** (exchanges 2 and 3) - twice in one session, so logged

  answers described a system: company culture, then the team leader's planning.
