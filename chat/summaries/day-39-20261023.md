## What We Talked About

The week, graded. The spec dominated: the new project is a C#-to-Java rewrite of an existing system, and he is about halfway through the spec - drawing diagrams to get clear on business processes is a must before system design. The rewrite happened because the business side had always complained the old system was hard to use, especially the clunky UI, so the leader decided to refactor; the new project is big on user flows. To avoid a repeat, the PM designs prototypes on a platform called UI/UE, and the business side tries out the user flows as soon as prototypes are done, floating ideas while clicking through. When an idea would blow up the schedule, the tech leader says no - and if they will not take no for an answer, the team talks the feature through and finds another way to build it. His grade: approve. Whatever got in the way, he put something in place to solve it - but the A-share market dropped nearly 5% and inflicted heavy losses, which was disheartening.
