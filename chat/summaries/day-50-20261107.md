# Day 50 - A trip you keep postponing

Date: 2026-11-07 | Category: daily | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 18 | Chunks owned: 76/128 | Chunks credited: 4 | Patterns owned: 6/13

## What We Talked About

Beijing, five years after graduation, with a date finally on it and nothing booked
yet. The plan is a day-by-day list he intends to ignore, drafted by a model and
checked against other travellers' posts. It closed on the practical edge of it -
3,500 km, once a year, and a winter three thousand kilometres colder than home.

## The Conversation

**1. Me:** Is there somewhere you've been meaning to go for a while now, and it still hasn't happened?

**You said:**
> I've always meant to travel Beijing since I graduated from university, but it still hasn't happen by then. We've make a plan to travel next month. Travel doubles as a way for me to hang out. I'll switch up my routine during the journey. I think travel really matters to people who get involved in a stressful workplace for a long time. Over the holiday, you can switch off totally.

**中文：**我从大学毕业就一直想去北京，但到现在还没成。我们计划下个月去。旅行对我来说也是跟朋友聚在一起的方式。旅途中我会打乱一下自己的作息。我觉得对那些长期待在压力大的工作环境里的人来说，旅行真的很重要。假期里可以彻底放空。

**Say it like this:**
> I've always meant to travel to Beijing since I graduated, but it still hasn't happened. We've made a plan to travel next month. Travel doubles as a way for me to hang out. I'll switch up my routine while I'm away. I think travel really matters to people who work in a stressful place for a long time. Over the holiday, you can switch off totally.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | travel Beijing | travel to Beijing | wrong | travel takes to before the destination. | travel 后面接目的地要加 to。 |
| 2 | it still hasn't happen | it still hasn't happened | wrong | have takes the -ed form, not the bare verb. | have 后面要用过去分词，不能用原形。 |
| 3 | We've make a plan | We've made a plan | wrong | Same have + -ed rule. | 同样是 have + 过去分词。 |
| 4 | has happened by then | has happened yet | awkward | "by then" needs a point in time you can name; "yet" is the open-ended one. | by then 需要一个明确的时间点，泛指"至今"用 yet。 |
| 5 | people who get involved in a stressful workplace | people who work in a stressful place | awkward | You get involved in a process; you work in a place. | get involved in 是参与某个过程，在某地工作用 work in。 |
| 6 | during the journey | while I'm away | optional | Yours is fine - this is just the more spoken shape. | 你原句没问题，这里只是更口语的说法。 |
