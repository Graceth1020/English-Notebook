## What We Talked About

A full weeknight off, door to bed: water the moment he leaves the office (flushes out the system), fast food or instant noodles for dinner, half an hour of short videos to unwind, then basketball with the neighbors - the group chat rounds people up for a game, and he plays more than twice a week - then home, shower, and books before sleep. Friends are a weekend thing: lately the crew has got hooked on a board game and is supposed to play Saturday morning, followed by a local restaurant - Guangzhou rice rolls and Cantonese breakfast - and a good chat to catch up on what has been going on. Sunday nights bring the scaries, and the reason is specific: he is a software engineer who enjoys coding, but he doubles as a PM now, and coordinating every team until blockers are solved is not his thing. Given one protected hour a day, he would spend it on coding - staying focused is easy there, pushing things forward is not his thing, and he feels proud when a feature he built goes live.
