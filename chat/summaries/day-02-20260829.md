**3. Me:** Right, but that's the part managers push back on hardest - they hear "flexible hours" and worry you'll be offline when they need you. What would you say to that?

**You said:**
> Have more freedom doesn't I'll be offline. I just want to work in different places like library or coffee shop. Actually, if you need me, I'll response at once.

**中文：**更自由不意味着我会脱线。我只是想在不同的地方工作，比如图书馆或咖啡厅。其实如果你需要我，我会马上回复。

**Say it like this:**
> More freedom doesn't mean I'll be offline. I just want to be able to work from a library or a coffee shop - if you need me, I'll respond right away.

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Have more freedom doesn't I'll be offline | More freedom doesn't mean I'll be offline | wrong | The verb "mean" is missing, and the subject needs to be a noun phrase. | 漏了动词 mean，主语要用名词短语。 |
| 2 | in different places like library or coffee shop | from a library or a coffee shop | wrong (caught after the fact) | Countable singulars need articles; and it's work FROM a place. | 可数名词单数要加冠词；且是 work from。 |
| 3 | Actually, if you need me | if you need me | optional | "Actually" signals a correction of the other person. Here you're adding, not correcting. | Actually 暗示在纠正对方，这里你是补充。 |
| 4 | I'll response | I'll respond | wrong | "response" is the noun; the verb is "respond". | response 是名词，respond 是动词。 |
| 5 | at once | right away | register | Not wrong - "at once" is correct but formal, the register of an announcement rather than a chat with a colleague. Default to "right away". | 不算错。"at once" 正确但偏正式，是通知的语域，不是同事间聊天。日常默认用 "right away"。 |

**Native:** wrong

On `at once` - it has two senses, and only one of them is dated-sounding:

| Sense | Register | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "immediately" | formal / written | "All staff must evacuate at once." |
| "at the same time" | ordinary spoken English | "Don't all talk at once." / "I can't do three things at once." |

The "immediately" sense is the one to avoid in casual speech; the "at the same
time" sense is alive and worth using. Everyday alternatives for the first sense,
most neutral first: **right away** > **right now** > **straight away** (more
British) > **ASAP** (Slack/email) > **immediately** (formal). In this exchange,
`If you need me, I'll respond right away` is the natural version - or, more
casually still, `Ping me and I'll get back to you`.

Worth noting: this turn answered the pushback head-on, and separating
*flexibility* from *availability* is exactly the move that wins that argument.

## Worth Stealing

- `C007` **"That's on me"** - taking responsibility without the weight of "duty". "Keeping the boundary is on me, not my manager."
- `C008` **"stay focused"** - the fixed phrase. "I turn off my console to stay focused."
- `C009` **"I get more say over (X)"** - claiming control in natural speech. "I get more say over my own hours."
- `C010` **"work from home"** - never "at home" when it means remote work.
- `C011` **"switch off"** - stop thinking about work. "The hard part is switching off at the end of the day."
- `C012` **"my commute"** - the noun covers trip and duration on its own. "My commute is 40 minutes door to door."
- `C013` **"right away"** - the default spoken "immediately"; reach for this before "at once". "If you need me, I'll respond right away."

Also worth noticing across the rewrites: **"got more done"** beats "was more
efficient", and **"anything I might get distracted by"** beats naming a product
category. Both are the same move - a person's words instead of a report's.

## Patterns Showing Up Again

- **Condition before answer** (exchanges 1, 2): asked a direct question twice, and
  twice you led with a qualifier instead of a position. Exchange 2 never answered
  the yes/no question at all. Same shape as `coach:E047`, now at 2 hits.
- **Half a word's usage known** (exchanges 1, 3, 4, 5): `commute` as verb but not
  noun, `response`/`respond`, `at home`/`from home`, `keep focusing`/`stay
  focused`. You recognise every one of these; you just have not stored them as
  units you can reach for.
- **Report register instead of speech** (exchanges 2, 3, 4, 5): "Another reason is
  that", "at once", "more efficient", "entertainment electronics", "the COVID-19
  era". Same habit as Day 01 - confirmed across two sessions and two topics, so it
  is the dominant issue. Note how few of these are errors: most are correct
  English sitting one level too formal, which is exactly why they are hard to
  self-detect.
