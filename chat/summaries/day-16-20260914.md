## What We Talked About

The game that has been on your phone for years. Yours is Honor of Kings -
an hour a day back in the day, now a weekend-only habit run by a rule you
invented yourself: delete it Sunday evening, reinstall it Friday evening. We
compared it with your dust-gathering PC rig and Red Alert, and ended on why
two days of access does not mean two days of playing.

**Say it like this:**

**What changed:**

| # | You wrote | Natural | Label | Why | Why 中文 |

| 1 | It **calls** "王者荣耀" | **It's called** Honor of Kings | wrong | A thing's name takes the passive: `it's called X`. `It calls X` means the phone is doing the calling. The English name of 王者荣耀 is Honor of Kings. | 名字用被动 it's called;it calls 是"它在呼叫"。王者荣耀的英文名是 Honor of Kings。 |
