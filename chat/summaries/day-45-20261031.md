## What We Talked About

A Saturday social topic after two weeks of weekday continuity. The split between study nights at home and group nights out opened with the board game Legends of the Three Kingdoms, which the group had been supposed to play in a few weeks and are playing this weekend. The closing coffee invitation was declined - juice over coffee, which stays the office default rather than a weekend thing.
