## What We Talked About

The Saturday board game got swapped for the university's anniversary - the plan was made in the group chat Friday morning, and with the subway expanding so fast, everyone from different districts could get back easily. The game is postponed a few weeks. On campus: new buildings have gone up since graduation six years ago, the dormitory building was still there, and electric bikes are now allowed on campus - students ride to class instead of walking between buildings, which used to be kind of a hassle. Does he miss student days? Depends on the period: freshman and sophomore, yes; junior and senior, no - those years were about pushing many things forward, talking to people outside his own field to broaden horizons, figuring out which field to work in, and what skills were a must. He is glad he picked backend engineering - people who know a lot about computer science are in demand - and his one sentence back to himself six years ago: investing matters to everyone, start early, aim for steady returns over the long run, don't be afraid of losses. This week's 5% A-share drop? He sees it as a short-term correction and believes good companies will reward their investors with excess returns.
