# Day 29 - A design you pushed back on

Date: 2026-10-09 | Category: work | Exchanges: 5 | Fixes: 18 | Chunks owned: 44/91 | Chunks credited: 15 | Patterns owned: 6/9

## What We Talked About

Last month's review-feature design: a colleague floated one table doubling as review logs. You pushed back - on the business side, people would confuse what the table is for, and problems would pile up if the team went with it. His counter: one table or two made no difference. Yours: two tables is a symptom, not the reason - the moment someone rejects a review, there's no way to show the logs. The team went with two tables, it really made a difference, and the rejection-logs scenario actually happened once. He agreed his own idea didn't hold up; it came down to lack of experience, and the culture is big on growing people, so rejected engineers come back with new ideas. The flip side: in January you picked a low-code platform for a fund-management project while out of your depth on the business side, having set aside too little time for the domain. Your leader pushed back - the project was interaction-heavy, and that choice would have been hard to maintain in the next few months. That's on you, and you should have learned the domain before making the call.
