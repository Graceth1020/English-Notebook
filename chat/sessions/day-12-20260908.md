## Mechanism

The learner's two complaints, and what follows from them.

1. **Difficulty drifted into content, not language.** By exchange 2 the roleplay
   needed a negotiating position before it could be answered in any language,
   and the learner had to ask for a strategy hint. `topics.md` already says work
   topics stay casual; SKILL.md says "kept light", "no lesson plan". Form-drill
   02 round 3 was withdrawn mid-drill for exactly this. Not a new rule, a rule
   that was ignored - written up as a hard constraint in `topics.md`.

2. **A turn with no reachable chunk raises difficulty for nothing.** Exchanges 1
   and 2 pulled four chunks; the negotiation turn pulled none. Scene matching was
   satisfied and the turn still had no chunk in range, because the pressure had
   pushed the conversation into a register where the bank has nothing. Cost with
   no return, and the learner felt it before Codex noticed it.
