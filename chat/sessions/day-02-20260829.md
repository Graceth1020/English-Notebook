**[meta] You:** commute 不是动词吗？(re: "as long as the commute is bad") - not counted as an exchange

**[meta] Codex:** "commute" is both a verb and a noun. Verb: "I commute by subway."
Noun: "my commute is 40 minutes" / "a long commute" / "the commute". The noun is
the more common one in everyday speech, and it already means the trip plus its
duration - so "commuting time" is understandable but redundant; natives say
"my commute".
