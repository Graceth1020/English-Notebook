# Pattern Inventory

Sentence patterns the learner does not use. Separate from the chunk bank on
purpose: a chunk is a fixed expression a topic can be built to demand, while a
pattern changes how a whole sentence is assembled. No opening can force a
pattern - the same idea can always be expressed with ordinary clauses and be
entirely correct - so seeding cannot reach one and there is nothing to correct
when it is avoided.

That is why these are drilled explicitly, one at a time, on request.

`Status` is deliberately two-stage, and the second stage is not earned here:


A drill can only ever produce `drilled`. Getting ten out of ten in a drill is
recognition, which is already this learner's strength; the diagnosis being worked
on is that vocabulary is recognition-level rather than recall-level. Promotion to
`owned` therefore happens only from a daily chat session, never from a drill.
Maintained by scripts/chat_log.py - do not hand-edit.
