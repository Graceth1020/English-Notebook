# Pattern 15 - abstract prepositions by spatial metaphor (on/in/of)

Date: 2026-10-19 | Pattern: P014 | Status: open | Rounds so far: 1

Learner-raised gap, from the Day 33 QA about "leave a comment on that line":
abstract on/in/of feel random. The frame: they are projections of three
physical prototypes -

  on  = touching a SURFACE or LINE (a page, a platform, a line of text,
        a topic, a date on the timeline)
  in  = inside a CONTAINER (a file, a function, a sentence, a stretch of
        time, a state)
  of  = PART of a WHOLE (a cause of the incident, two lines of code,
        the color of the button)

Decision procedure being trained: ask "what does this thing physically
resemble?" - a surface/line picks on, a container picks in, a part-of
relation picks of. Covers ~90% of abstract uses; the rest are fixed
collocations learned one by one.

Round 1: prototype judgment - pick on/in/of, six items mixing the three
prototypes plus the time contrast (date as point vs month as container).
Round 2: embed in full sentences, distractors included (fixed collocations
that do NOT follow the metaphor). Round 3: free production, describe own
work artifacts.

## Round 1 - pick the preposition

**1.** a comment ___ line 42
**中文：** 在 42 行留一条评论

**2.** the bug ___ the save function
**中文：** save 函数里的 bug

**3.** the root cause ___ the incident
**中文：** 事故的根因

**4.** a recipe ___ Rednote
**中文：** 小红书上的一个菜谱

**5.** ___ the third paragraph
**中文：** 在第三段里

**6.** ___ October / ___ the 15th (two blanks)
**中文：** 在十月 / 在 15 号

## Round 1 outcome

5/6 first pass. The only miss: item 4, "a recipe in Rednote" -> on Rednote -
the platform-as-surface metaphor (on the dashboard / on the internet / on the
platform; same family as his known "I got this off Rednote" from Drill 15).
The container reading of platforms is the natural Chinese default, so this is
the item to retest. Minor note on item 1: specific "line 42" takes no article.

## Round 2 - embed in a sentence (fixed-collocation distractors)

**1.** 这个 bug 藏在支付模块里。
**中文：** 这个 bug 藏在支付模块里。

**2.** 我在这个 PR 上留了三条评论。
**中文：** 我在这个 PR 上留了三条评论。

**3.** 问题的根源在于配置。
**中文：** 问题的根源在于配置。

**4.** 仪表盘上出现了一个红色告警。
**中文：** 仪表盘上出现了一个红色告警。

**5.** 他依赖缓存来提速。
**中文：** 他依赖缓存来提速。

**6.** 会议定在周一早上。
**中文：** 会议定在周一早上。

## Round 2 outcome

Item 4 skipped on first pass - handed back for completion. Of the five
answered: prepositions correct on items 1 (in the pay module), 2 (on this PR
- but tense slipped: leave -> left for a completed act), 6 (scheduled for
Monday morning - the Day 32 QA feeding straight into production). Item 3:
taught collocation was "lies in", he produced "be down to" - accepted as a
correct idiomatic alternative (BrE informal); both now on record. Item 5
wrong: "by cache" - by + bare noun is transport/means only; tool takes
with the cache / by caching; and the cued fixed collocation rely on was not
reached for. The distractor design worked: metaphor-guided items clean, the
two fixed collocations (rely on, lie in) are the real gap.
Item 4 on retry: "A red alert showed up on the dashboard." - correct (on + surface), and showed up is the idiomatic verb for an alert appearing. R2 final: metaphor-guided items 4/4 clean; fixed collocations remain the gap.

## Round 3 - free production

Describe one of your own work systems or an investigation in 3-4 sentences.
Must collect all four cards: one on (surface/line/topic), one in
(container), one of (part-whole), one fixed collocation (rely on / lie in /
depend on).

## Round 3 outcome

All four cards collected, including a self-chosen depends on for the fixed
collocation: "displays on the index page of the platform / saved in the
database / depends on the role of current user." The preposition choices are
4/4 correct. The breakage is outside the pattern's scope but logged: "What
data displaying depends" - bare -ing without the auxiliary, passive needed
(what data is displayed), the E005 mechanism inside a subject clause; "the
role of current user" - empty determiner slot, E031. Verdict: drilled.
