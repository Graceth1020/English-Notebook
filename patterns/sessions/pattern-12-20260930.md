# Pattern 12 - noun pile -> embedded question

Date: 2026-09-30 | Pattern: P011 | Status: open | Rounds so far: 1

Requested by the learner after Day 24 exchange 3 produced "the unit of the
amount saved in database" - a word-by-word rendering of the Chinese de-chain
数据库里存的金额的单位. The frame that should have fired is one he already
owns: same day, exchange 2, "I checked the logs to find out what the problem
was" - a perfect embedded question, unprompted. So the structure is not new;
the de-chain simply beats it under pressure when the head noun is an
information point.

Earlier instances of the same mechanism: Day 17 "the amount showing logic on
our pages", Day 10 "the send SMS logic", Day 05 "I'll figure out the reason".

The rule, in its shortest runnable form:

  Catching yourself building "the + NOUN + of ..."? Check the noun:
  - names a piece of information (unit / name / reason / time / place / way
    / price)  ->  drop the pile, use wh-word + clause:
                  what unit it was stored in / why it failed / where the
                  config file is / when it ships
  - is a property that belongs to the thing (the end of the quarter, the
    plot of the book)  ->  keep "the X of Y" - do not unwind.
  Two how-shapes: the way to do X -> how to do X;
                  the way X works -> how X works.

Round 1 spreads three wh-forms (what / how+to / why).
Round 2 leads with the discriminating item: a sentence that must NOT unwind.
Round 3: free production from his own situations.

## Round 1 - unwind the pile

**1.** We would have known the unit of the amount saved in the database. (Day 24)
**中文：**我们当时就会知道数据库里存的金额是什么单位。

**2.** I don't know the way to refresh our API. (Day 14)
**中文：**我不知道怎么刷新我们的 API。

**3.** We need to figure out the reason for the failed alerts. (Day 21)
**中文：**我们得弄清楚告警为什么没发出去。

## Round 2 - unwind or keep?

**1.** I'll get through it by the end of the quarter. (careful - keep or unwind?)
**中文：**我会在季度结束前读完它。

**2.** Do you know the location of the config file?
**中文：**你知道配置文件在哪儿吗?

**3.** Tell me the time of the campaign SMS. (Day 21)
**中文：**告诉我营销短信什么时候发。

**4.** We updated the amount showing logic on our pages. (Day 17)
**中文：**我们更新了页面上金额的显示逻辑。

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** 你没看懂数据库里金额的单位,去问了同事。Say what you did.
**中文：**你没看懂数据库里金额的单位,去问了同事。

**2.** A new joiner doesn't know how to debug a production issue. Tell him you'll teach him.
**中文：**新同事不会排查线上问题,你说"我教你怎么查"。

**3.** 你不记得优惠券功能是哪天上线的,去查了发布记录。Say it.
**中文：**你不记得优惠券功能是哪天上线的,去查了发布记录。

## Round 1 outcome (retry history)

First pass 0/3. Shared mechanism found: the wh-word is available (item 1
grabbed "what" immediately) but the of-pile was never unfolded into a clause -
"what unit of the amount stored" kept the chain alive inside the frame. Items
2-3 kept the pile entirely. After the four-step explanation (info-noun -> wh-
question -> statement order), retry: items 2-3 landed clean; item 1 took two
more passes - first losing "was" (passive participle with no be, its third
occurrence in two days -> logged as E070), then giving the stranded "in" to
"the database" instead of the unit. Final answer "stored in in the database"
shows both locatives understood. Round 1 lesson: the wh-word is the easy
half; unfolding of-phrase -> subject + verb is the real move.

## Round 2 outcome

4/4 clean. The discriminating item (keep "by the end of the quarter") passed -
no over-application. Item 3 produced "when the campaign SMS is sent" instead
of the model's "goes out" - correct, and notably the passive be was present
one turn after E070 was logged for dropping it. Item 4 backshifted "showed"
after "updated" - legal; present "shows" also fine.

## Round 3 outcome

3/3 clean, all in free production: "Could you tell me what unit the amount
was stored in?" (was + stranded in both correct), "how to debug a production
issue", "when the coupon feature went out" (collocation note: a feature goes
live / ships). First-pass total 7/10; after retries 10/10. Pattern-drilled
logged: P011 -> drilled. Summary written: patterns/summaries/pattern-12-20260930.md.
