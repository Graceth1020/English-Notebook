# Pattern 13 - post-noun modifier hangers (de-chain unstacking)

Date: 2026-09-30 | Pattern: P012 | Status: open | Rounds so far: 1

Learner-raised gap, same day as the P011 drill (pattern-12). His question:
how do you say 那边那个正在球场打球的穿着黑色球衣的长头发的带着眼镜的男生 -
a Chinese de-chain where every modifier stacks BEFORE the noun. English says
the head noun first and hangs each modifier AFTER it on its own hook:

  with + feature (long hair, glasses)
  in + clothing (a black jersey)
  -ing + action (playing on the court)
  place phrase last (over there)

Full: the guy with long hair and glasses, in a black jersey, playing on the
court over there.

Rules being trained:
1. Head noun first - never assemble modifiers before naming the noun.
2. One de = one hook - two modifiers never share one English phrase.
3. The 2-3 hook ceiling - beyond it, natives split: "See that guy on the
   court? Long hair, glasses, black jersey - that's him."

Round 1: assemble from given pieces (all four hooks spread; the in-hook is
the discriminating item at position 2 - clothing takes "in", never "with").
Round 2 will give unsorted de-chains (choose the hook yourself).
Round 3: free production from his own life, including one 4+ chain said the
split way.

## Round 1 - build the noun phrase

**1.** Pieces: a guy / long hair / glasses
**中文：**那个长头发、戴眼镜的男生

**2.** Pieces: a guy / he is wearing a black jersey
**中文：**那个穿着黑色球衣的男生

**3.** Pieces: a boy / he is playing basketball / on the court over there
**中文：**那边那个正在球场打篮球的男生

**4.** Pieces: a guy / long hair + glasses / black jersey / playing on the court / over there
**中文：**那边那个穿着黑色球衣、正在打球的长头发戴眼镜的男生

## Round 1 outcome (retry history)

First pass 1/4 (item 1 was the worked example, echoed clean). The finding:
the with-hook fires, but 穿 and 正在做 never reached for their hooks - item 2
dodged the in-hook with a legal "wearing" (not the pattern), and items 3-4
promoted 正在做 to a full verb ("a boy IS playing"), turning the noun phrase
into a sentence. Named the test (穿 -> in; 正在做 -> chop "is", keep -ing).
Retry next day: 3/3 - all three hooks landed, item 4 the full four-hook chain
("with long hair and glasses, in the black jersey, playing basketball on the
court over there"). Residual error, one mechanism: definiteness - 那个/
那边那个 came out as "a" in all three retries (pointing someone out takes
"the"), and "playing the basketball" picked up a stray "the" (sports take no
article). Noted in the drill, no error rows - article choice by definiteness
is a separate slot from the hanger pattern.

## Round 2 - choose the hook yourself

**1.** 那个在会上一直问问题的实习生
**中文：**那个在会上一直问问题的实习生

**2.** 门口那个背红色双肩包的男生
**中文：**门口那个背红色双肩包的男生

**3.** 那个穿白衬衫、正在跟经理说话的女士
**中文：**那个穿白衬衫、正在跟经理说话的女士

**4.** 客厅那台屏幕裂了的旧手机
**中文：**客厅那台屏幕裂了的旧手机

## Round 2 outcome (retry history)

First pass 3/4 on hooks. The discriminating item passed: habitual action
(一直问问题) correctly escaped to a who-clause, and Round 1's definite "the"
landed in items 1-2. Item 4 missed: an object's state (屏幕裂了) went to a
whose-clause ("whose screen is broken in the living room") - stiff for a
thing, and it swallowed the place phrase (the screen now broke "in the
living room"). Named the new hook: state of an object -> with + noun + -ed
(with a cracked screen / with a broken button / with a missing key). Side
notes: 门口 = at the door, not behind; 跟某人说话 = talk to, not talk with.
Retry: all clean. Item 2 came back as "the body" (typo for "the boy",
flagged); item 4 dropped "old" (noted).

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Your new colleague wants to see your gaming rig. Point it out: "It's the one ___." (2-3 hooks)
**中文：**新同事想看你的游戏主机,你指给他看:"就是那台……"

**2.** A friend is coming to watch you play basketball. Tell him how to spot you: "I'm the one ___." (2-3 hooks)
**中文：**朋友来球场看你打球,你告诉他怎么认出你:"我就是那个……"

**3.** Describe the most distinctive person at your court or office - as many features as you can, the SPLIT way (short sentence + dash list).
**中文：**描述你球场或公司里最有辨识度的一个人,特征尽量多,用拆分的方式说。

## Round 3 outcome (retry history)

Items 1-2 clean on first pass. Item 1 (rig): "the red one with its screen on
sitting on the desk" - both hooks fire, comma needed between them. Item 2
(self-description): hooks right, "long black hair" adjective order right;
vocabulary notes: 皮肤白 = fair skin, 科比牌 = Kobes. Item 3 first pass: all
hooks fired but SEVEN features crammed into one sentence - legal, and exactly
the overload the split exists for (not the pattern). Word traps surfaced:
yellow for blond, white for fair, "a pair of glasses" losing 金丝, "a Nike
brand shoes" (a + plural). Retry landed the full split: "Mike is the one
coding in the office. Long blond hair, gold-rimmed glasses, fair skin, green
eyes, white shirt and blue jeans, Nikes - that's my leader." One surviving
slip: "That's" capitalized after the dash. Drill complete: 6/11 first pass,
11/11 after retries. P012 -> drilled.

Status: done.
