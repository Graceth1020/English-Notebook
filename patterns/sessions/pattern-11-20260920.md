# Pattern 11 - none of + noun (second pass)

Date: 2026-09-20 | Pattern: P007 | Status: done | Rounds so far: 3

Second drill on P007, requested by the learner. The first pass (Drill 07, 16/17)
found the negation family structurally sound except for one miss: `Neither coffee
shop closed` - a negative verb cancelling `neither`, the mirror image of Day 14s
original `all users couldnt pay`.

Three things happened since:

- Day 21, unprompted in chat: *Since our queue was backed up and no alert went
  out* - the `no + noun` sibling, produced in the wild. Logged `pattern-used`
  (1/2).
- Day 20 produced two new gaps in the wider quantifier family: *all two things
  work* (exactly two = `both`, never `all two`) and *All the team have got to be
  responsible* (`all the team` is not how it is said; `everyone on the team`).
  This drill extends the frame to cover them.
- Drill 07s one miss (`neither` + negative verb) has not been retested.

The rule extension for this drill:

  The full quantifier map:

  TOTAL      None of the users could pay.     No user could pay.
  PARTIAL    Not all users could pay.
  TWO        Both providers were expensive.    Neither provider worked.
  UNCOUNT    None of the work is done.         None of the traffic was moving.
  GROUP      Everyone on the team is responsible.   (never "all the team")

  Two hard rules:

  1. The negation lives in the subject; the verb stays affirmative.
     Neither coffee shop closed = BOTH were open.  (the Drill 07 miss)
  2. Exactly two is `both` / `neither`, never `all two` / `none of the two`.

Round 1 spreads five forms (total, partial, two-affirmative, two-negative,
uncountable) per the standing rule.
Round 2 carries the Drill 07 retest and the Day 20 items.
Round 3: free production, 4 items.

## Round 1 - rewrite with the right quantifier

**1.** After the ticket shipped, all users could not pay. (total - the Day 14 original)
**中文：**上线后，没有一个用户能支付。

**2.** Some of the team docs are up to date. Some are not. (partial)
**中文：**不是所有团队文档都是最新的。

**3.** Both coffee shops were closed. I could not get a coffee. (two, affirmative verb - careful)
**中文：**两家咖啡店都关门了，我没喝到咖啡。

**4.** I asked two teammates to review. Both have not replied. (two, negative meaning - careful)
**中文：**我请了两个同事 review，两人都没回。

**5.** The information in the wiki is never current. (uncountable)
**中文：**wiki 里的信息没有一条是最新的。

## Round 2 - mixed, choose the right shape

**1.** All the team have got to be responsible for this. (careful - Day 20)
**中文：**团队每个人都要为此负责。

**2.** All two things work. It is a virtuous cycle. (careful - Day 20)
**中文：**这两个因素都起作用，形成良性循环。

**3.** The old SMS provider was reliable. We swapped it out anyway. (say it with `neither`-family or `none`-family - your choice)
**中文：**旧短信供应商很可靠，但我们还是换掉了。

**4.** Not every engineer wants to work remote. Some prefer the office. (partial)
**中文：**不是每个工程师都想远程，有人喜欢去公司。

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Retell the Day 14 incident in one sentence: what happened to users payments?
**中文：**用一句话重述 Day 14 的事故：用户的支付怎么了？

**2.** Tell a teammate that the two candidates for the new framework both have problems.
**中文：**跟同事说，新框架的两个候选都有问题。

**3.** Someone asks if your whole team keeps docs up to date. Honest answer: some do, some do not.
**中文：**有人问你们团队是不是所有人都及时更新文档。诚实回答。

**4.** Describe the National Day drive: the traffic, the cars, and how you felt.
**中文：**描述国庆开车：车流、那些车，以及你的感受。
