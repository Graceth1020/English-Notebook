# Pattern 05 - no matter + wh- + clause (Round 2, harder)

Date: 2026-09-09 | Pattern: P005 | Status: done | Rounds so far: 3

Second drill on P005, requested by the learner. The first drill (04) found that
`how` was over-applied as a general-purpose concession word, and that the same
miss could survive ten items because a filler happened to be available. This drill
attacks that boundary directly.

Added material: end-position alternatives `..., anyway` (C069, 0 tries, 0 uses),
`..., though` (C052, 0 tries, 0 uses), and `either way` — all frame chunks the
learner has banked but never produced in a drill.

Round 1 (join two, pick the right wh-word) — no `how` items this round, so the
only available wh-words are what/who/where/when. The learner must pick the one
that fits the conceded dimension.
Round 2 (mixed) — items specify the position: front-position `no matter`, or
end-position `..., anyway` / `..., though`, or `either way`. Some items ask for
both versions.
Round 3 (situation only) — 4 items.

The rule extension for this drill:

  End-position:  The concession after the fact, said as a short tag.
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  No matter how bad the traffic is, I drive back.  (front)    │
  │  The traffic is brutal. I drive back anyway.      (end)      │
  │  The traffic is brutal, but I drive back anyway.  (end)      │
  │  The traffic is brutal, though. (end, just the concession)   │
  │  Either way, I drive back.                         (front)   │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  `anyway` = the action happens despite the obstacle.
  `though` = just the concession, no action stated.
  `either way` = the choice is irrelevant.

## Round 1 - join two sentences, pick the right wh-word

(The wh-word is NOT given — you supply it. None of these want `how`.)

**1.** Everyone says coding is not relaxing. It is how I switch off.
**中文：**不管别人怎么说，编程就是我的放松方式。

**2.** The PM assigns the ticket to me or to someone else. I need to understand the context.
**中文：**不管PM把 ticket 派给谁，我都需要理解上下文。

**3.** I am at the library or at home. I turn off my phone when I read.
**中文：**不管在图书馆还是在家，看书时我都会关掉手机。

**4.** The queue was blocked or the provider was down. The monitor caught the error.
**中文：**不管是队列堵了还是供应商挂了，监控都抓住了错误。

## Round 2 - mixed, position and structure

**1.** The traffic is brutal. I still drive back. (say it with end-position `..., anyway`)
**中文：**路况很差，但我还是会开回去。

**2.** I am tired after work. I still read at weekends. (say it BOTH ways: front-position `no matter`, and end-position `..., anyway`)
**中文：**下班后很累，但还是周末读书。用前置和后置两种说法。

**3.** The old SMS provider was reliable. We swapped it out. (say it with end-position `..., though`)
**中文：**旧的短信供应商很可靠，但我们还是换掉了。

**4.** Shipping on Tuesday or Wednesday. I need the testing time. (use `either way` at the front)
**中文：**周二上线还是周三上线，我都要测试时间。

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Someone asks how you can read on weekends when you are already worn out from work. Concede the fatigue, then state the action.
**中文：**别人问你工作那么累怎么还周末看书。先承认累，再说不影响。

**2.** A PM asks whether you can handle the ticket alone. You will figure it out regardless.
**中文：**PM 问你一个人能不能搞定这个 ticket。不管怎样你都会搞定的。

**3.** You are deciding between going home over National Day and staying in Foshan. Either way has a cost.
**中文：**你在纠结国庆回不回家。两边都有代价。

**4.** A teammate asks why you keep buying physical books when you can read digital. Say what you prefer.
**中文：**同事问你为什么还买纸质书。用 `..., anyway` 或 `..., though` 收尾。
