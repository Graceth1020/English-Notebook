# Pattern 20 - stacked time adverbials (second pass, harder)

Date: 2026-10-26 | Pattern: P013 | Status: done | Rounds so far: 3

Requested by the learner ("begin P013 pattern drill. The drill can be more
difficulty"), overriding `pattern-next` (P003). Second pass on P013;
Drill 14 went 6/6 on assembly, so the ceiling goes up:

1. **The Drill-16 miss is the discriminating item** (1.1, 2.1): `on` governs
   day+date as ONE unit - `at 6:30 pm on Friday, October 24th`, never
   `on Friday on October 24th` and never a bare hanging weekday.
2. **Four-unit stacks** (1.5, 2.4): between X and Y + day part + week
   reference, zero glue.
3. **The meridiem trap** (1.6): 12 am is midnight, 12 pm is noon; the
   avoidance strategy from Drill 14 (say noon/midnight) is the target.
4. **midnight/noon stay bare** even after by (1.2) - Drill 14 Round 2
   produced `by the midnight`.
5. **Harder frames around the stack** (Round 2): not...until, by + past
   perfect, ranges with for.

Round 1 spreads: the day+date unit / bare by midnight / the one legal of /
this-coming stripping / four-unit stack / meridiem.
Round 2 embeds in sentences with harder frames.
Round 3: free production - timeline narration, meeting scheduling, incident
report.

The rule, restated:

  at + clock time, on + day/date, in + bare part of day
  small to large, ZERO glue: at 6:30 pm on Friday, October 24th
  day+date is ONE unit under a single on
  last/this/next/yesterday strip all prepositions
  midnight and noon are always bare: by midnight, at noon
  the one legal of: on the morning of October 15th

## Round 1 - build the time phrase

**1.** Pieces: at 6:30 pm / Friday / October 24th  (the Drill-16 miss - discriminating item)
**中文：**10 月 24 号周五下午 6 点半

**2.** Pieces: by midnight / New Year''s Eve  (midnight stays bare after by)
**中文：**到除夕半夜为止

**3.** Pieces: in the morning / the 15th  (the one legal of)
**中文：**15 号早上

**4.** Pieces: around 2 / this coming Sunday afternoon  (zero preps with this coming)
**中文：**这个周日下午 2 点左右

**5.** Pieces: between 2 and 4 / Friday afternoon / last week  (four-unit stack)
**中文：**上周五下午 2 点到 4 点之间

**6.** Pieces: 12 / yesterday midday  (meridiem trap)
**中文：**昨天中午 12 点

## Round 2 - embed the stack in a sentence

**1.** 会议改到 10 月 24 号周五下午 6:30。(discriminating item, embedded)
**中文：**会议改到 10 月 24 号周五下午 6:30。

**2.** 到除夕半夜为止，所有的票都卖光了。(by + past perfect)
**中文：**到除夕半夜为止，所有的票都卖光了。

**3.** 我们把发布定在下周二早上 10 点到 11 点之间。(range + scheduled for)
**中文：**我们把发布定在下周二早上 10 点到 11 点之间。

**4.** 我昨天晚上 11 点才到家。(not...until frame)
**中文：**我昨天晚上 11 点才到家。

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Narrate last weekend with at least three stacked times, one with a part of day and one deadline (by).
**中文：**复盘你的上个周末：至少三个时间堆叠，一个带上午/下午，一个用到...为止。

**2.** Schedule a meeting for a teammate: full date, weekday, part of day, clock time.
**中文：**给同事约个会：完整日期、星期几、上午还是下午、具体钟点。

**3.** Report an incident timeline: when the alert fired, when you rolled back, by when it was fixed.
**中文：**汇报一次事故时间线：警报什么时候响的，什么时候回滚的，到什么时候修好的。
