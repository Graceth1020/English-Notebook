Drills 15 (10/13) and 16 (16/17) are done. Drill 16 cleared all three Drill-15
flags: platform-as-surface, `rely on`, `with` for tools. So this pass has two
jobs:

1. **Spaced retest of the cleared flags** - platform-as-surface (the sentinel,
   still placed first), `with` for a tool, `by -ing` for a method. A flag
   cleared at 24 hours is not the same as a flag cleared at 48.
2. **New territory the metaphor covers but no drill has visited** -
   `on` for lines (on line 42), `in` + duration (sold out in minutes),
   `by` + deadline (by Friday), and two fixed collocations never yet
   produced: `thanks to` and `lie in`.

Round 1 spreads eight distinctions (on/off/on-line/in/of/at/by/with) with the
platform sentinel at #1 per the standing rule.
Round 2 embeds in sentences, carrying both new collocations and the retests.
Round 3: free production, 4 items, his own scenes.

The rule, as restated after Drill 16:

  on = surface/line    a comment on line 42, on the dashboard, on Rednote
  in = container       in the wiki, in October, in a few minutes
  of = part-whole      the first half of the book
  off = the off-pair of on (platforms)   I got this off Rednote
  Which pair fires follows the VERB: see/read ON a platform, get OFF it.

  by + bare noun = transport/means ONLY (by bus, by hand)
  tool   -> with          fix it with a one-line change
  method -> by -ing       speed it up by caching
  deadline -> by          get it done by Friday

  Fixed collocations ignore the metaphor: rely on, depend on, lie in,
  thanks to, down to, based on - learned one by one.
