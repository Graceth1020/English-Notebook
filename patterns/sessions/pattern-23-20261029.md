# Pattern 23 - abstract prepositions (sixth pass: particles + new collocations)

Date: 2026-10-29 | Pattern: P014 | Status: done | Rounds so far: 3

Requested by the learner ("Begin today''s P014 pattern drill"), overriding
`pattern-next` (P003). Drill 19 said no sixth pass by default - but if
requested, lead with on/off as VERB PARTICLES (the `pry the lid on` flag
from Drill 19 item 2.5) and keep the all-new-items rule. Both honoured.

Spine:

1. **on/off as verb particles** (1.1, 1.2, discriminating) - on = contact,
   off = separation: shut off the phone, put on the lid, pry off, take off.
   Same metaphor, new grammatical home.
2. **New fixed collocations**: count on (指望), focus on (聚焦),
   result in (导致), full of (全是) - none ever requested before.
3. **Container retest in code** (1.3) - bugs live IN a project/service
   (flagged in Drill 22 item 3.2: `bugs of the new project`).
4. **Topic retest** (1.4) - a lecture ON behavioural economics.

Round 1 spreads: off-particle / on-particle / in-container / on-topic /
count on / focus on / result in / of-part-whole.
Round 2 embeds in sentences. Round 3: free production, 4 items.

## Round 1 - pick the preposition or particle

**1.** shut ___ the phone  (particle: off = separation - discriminating item)
**中文：**关掉手机

**2.** put ___ the lid  (particle: on = contact)
**中文：**盖上盖子

**3.** a bug ___ the payment service  (container retest, code)
**中文：**支付服务里的一个 bug

**4.** a lecture ___ behavioural economics  (topic retest)
**中文：**一场关于行为经济学的讲座

**5.** count ___ him  (new collocation: 指望)
**中文：**指望他

**6.** focus ___ the root cause  (new collocation: 聚焦)
**中文：**聚焦根因

**7.** resulted ___ a full outage  (new collocation: 导致)
**中文：**导致整个服务宕机

**8.** the first week ___ November  (part-whole)
**中文：**十一月的第一周

## Round 2 - embed in a sentence

**1.** 出门前我把电脑关了。(particle in a sentence)
**中文：**出门前我把电脑关了。

**2.** 他进门就脱掉了外套。(off = separation, clothing)
**中文：**他进门就脱掉了外套。

**3.** 代码库里全是临时代码。(new chunk: full of)
**中文：**代码库里全是临时代码。

**4.** 别指望他周末回消息。(count on in a sentence)
**中文：**别指望他周末回消息。

**5.** 这次改动导致了整个服务宕机。(result in in a sentence)
**中文：**这次改动导致了整个服务宕机。

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Describe your leaving-home routine: what you shut off, what you put on, what you take with you.
**中文：**描述你出门前的动作：关掉什么，穿上/带上什么。

**2.** Talk about a person or tool you count on - and what for.
**中文：**说说一个你依赖的人或工具，依赖它做什么。

**3.** Describe an incident where a small change caused a big problem.
**中文：**讲一次小改动引发大问题的事故。

**4.** Recommend a lecture or book - name its topic.
**中文：**推荐一个讲座或一本书，说出它的主题。
