# Pattern 04 - no matter + wh- + clause

Date: 2026-09-08 | Pattern: P005 | Status: done | Rounds so far: 3

Requested by the learner ("不管怎么样：No matter....."), logged as `P005`.
`pattern-next` had offered `P002` (object pronoun after a preposition) for the
fourth drill running; vetoed again in favour of the learner's pick.

Same absence profile as P001, P003 and P004: 0 uses in 55 exchanges over 11 days.
The concession is currently made in one of two ways, both correct, so no error row
could ever exist for it:

- a separate sentence with `but` / `though` - Day 08's *The traffic can be brutal
  though*, Day 11's *A lot of people don't think learning counts as switching off
  at the weekend. But for me...*
- or the concession is dropped and only the conclusion survives - Day 08's *since
  then I've just been driving back*, with the "however bad it gets" left implied.

Unlike the previous three, this one has a partner in the error log. `E011`
(*Whenever working from home*, Day 02, 1 hit) is the same defect one level up: a
wh- adverbial clause built without a subject. `no matter how` is where that will
bite hardest, because the `how` also drags an adjective up in front of the
subject, so two things move at once.

Round 1 (join two given sentences) - can the clause be built at all, with the
subject present.
Round 2 (mixed) - carries three traps:
  - the `how` word order: `no matter how bad the traffic is`, not `no matter how
    the traffic is bad`
  - subject-position `whatever`: *Whatever you decide is fine* is a noun clause and
    cannot take `no matter`. `No matter what you decide, it's fine.` This is the
    one real grammatical boundary of the pattern.
  - present tense for a future concession: `no matter when he ships it`, never
    `will ship`.
Round 3 (situation only) - 4 items, kept large for the same reason as Drill 03:
the bottleneck is the number of first instincts, not the amount of explanation.

The rule being taught, in the shortest runnable form:

  no matter + wh-word + a full clause , then the main clause

  no matter what   he says      ,  I'm driving back
  no matter how    bad it is    ,  I'm driving back
  no matter when   he ships it  ,  I'll test it first

  The wh-word is doing the conceding, so the clause after it is ordinary word
  order - no inversion, no `will`. Only `how` reorders anything, and only by
  pulling its adjective or adverb up next to itself.

Watch for: a missing subject (`no matter how tired`, `no matter how busy` are real
ellipses but should not be the first thing produced - insist on the full clause
first), `no matter` used where a noun clause is needed, `will` inside the clause,
and the bookish trailing shape. Also worth naming if it comes up: the spoken
end-position versions - `..., either way`, `..., whatever happens`, `..., anyway` -
which are what a native speaker often reaches for instead.

## Round 1 - join two given sentences

**1.** The traffic gets bad. I still drive back home.
**中文：**不管路况多差，我还是开车回家。

**2.** People say learning is not relaxing. It works for me.
**中文：**不管别人说学习算不算放松，对我来说有用。

**3.** Everyone books at the same time. I need to get home somehow.
**中文：**不管多少人同时抢票，我总得回家。

**4.** The ticket is complicated. I have got to start somewhere.
**中文：**不管这个 ticket 多复杂，我总得从某个地方开始。

## Round 2 - mixed, choose the right shape

**1.** He ships it on Monday or Wednesday. I test it properly first.
**中文：**不管他周一还是周三上线，我都要先好好测。

**2.** You decide. It is fine with me. (careful - what fills the subject slot?)
**中文：**你决定就好，我都行。

**3.** The commute is long. It wears me out. (a proportional link, not a concession)
**中文：**通勤越长，我越累。

**4.** The new business model is unusual. An LLM cannot design the process.
**中文：**不管新的商业模式多特别，LLM 都设计不出流程。

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** A teammate keeps pushing you to ship on Tuesday. Concede nothing on testing.
**中文：**同事一直催你周二上线，但测试这件事你不让步。

**2.** Explain why you go home over National Day even though it is eight hours in the car.
**中文：**解释你为什么国庆还是回家，即使来回八小时。

**3.** Say what you do about your phone when you are working from home.
**中文：**说说在家办公时你怎么处理手机。

**4.** Tell someone why you keep reading at weekends, even when work has worn you out.
**中文：**跟别人说，不管工作多累，你周末还是要读书。
