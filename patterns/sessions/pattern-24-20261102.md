## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Describe a place or container you know that is full of something - your
fridge, your gym bag, a group chat, your notebook. The sentence must use
`be full of`.
**中文：**描述一个你熟悉、里面全是某样东西的地方或容器（冰箱、健身包、群聊、笔记本都行），必须用到 be full of。

**You wrote:** The fridge in my house is full of delicious Chaoshan specialties.
**Answer:** The fridge at home is full of delicious Chaoshan specialties.
**Verdict:** correct
**Why:** Pinned frame `be full of` produced in free production - the Drill 23 gap closed. Not counted: in my house -> at home is more spoken.

**2.** Recommend one book, course, podcast, or video you enjoyed. Name its
topic with `a book/course/podcast/video on X`.
**中文：**推荐一本你喜欢的书、一门课、一个播客或一个视频，用 a ... on X 说出它的主题。

**You wrote:** I recommend a podcast called how to invest on investing.
**Answer:** I'd recommend a podcast on investing. / a podcast called "How to Invest" if that is the actual title.
**Verdict:** wrong form
**Why:** Same garble as Drill 23 R3.4: the topic frame and the titled-name frame fused (`called how to invest on investing`). Two legal shapes: a podcast on investing (topic surface, no called), or a podcast called "How to Invest" (title, quotes, no on). Topic-on still cannot assemble cleanly under free production.

**3.** Tell about a small decision or change that led to a good result or a
problem. Use `lead to`.
**中文：**讲一个小决定或小改动最后带来了好结果或问题的经历，用到 lead to。

**You wrote:** I decided not to climb mountains on October 25th. And thanked to my decision, it was raining heavily that day.
**Answer:** I decided not to go mountain climbing on October 25th. It poured that day, so that decision led to a much drier weekend.
**Verdict:** not the pattern
**Why:** The required `lead to` frame never appeared; the story ran on two flat clauses. Separately: thanked to -> thanks to (fixed chunk, no -ed); the rain is not the result of the decision, so lead to needs a result clause attached to the decision.

**4.** Say what you are interested in outside work, and one tool or method you
used to get started - a `with` tool or a `by -ing` method.
**中文：**说说工作之外你对什么感兴趣，以及你当初用什么工具或方法入门的（with + 工具，或 by + -ing 方法）。

**You wrote:** I enjoy cooking on weekends. At first, I could only cook easy dishes like stir-fried eggs, and I usually fried an egg for breakfast with a pot. I learn how to cook by reading posts on Rednote.
**Answer:** I enjoy cooking on weekends. At first I could only make simple dishes like scrambled eggs; I usually fried an egg for breakfast in a pan. I learned to cook by reading posts on Rednote.
**Verdict:** correct
**Why:** Both method frames reached: by reading (by + -ing) and with a pot (with + instrument); platform-on held on Rednote. Not counted: pan not pot; scrambled eggs; learned (past for how he got started).
