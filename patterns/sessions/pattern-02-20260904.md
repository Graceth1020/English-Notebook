# Pattern 02 - purpose nouns

Date: 2026-09-04 | Pattern: P003 | Status: open | Rounds so far: 0

Requested by the learner after the `about` vs stacked-noun distinction came up.
Same profile as P001: 0 uses in 45 exchanges, never produced wrongly - he reaches
for `a platform where we can get various recipes` or just a bare noun, both of
which are correct, so no error row could ever exist for it.

Chinese cues this time, at his request. Defensible here in a way it would not be
in daily chat: the target is a compression rule, and the Chinese is the same
length either way, so it cues meaning without modelling the English shape.

Material from Days 01-08 where possible. Round 2 carries the trap - `about` for
content vs stacking for purpose - and Round 3 goes to free production.

Watch for: over-application. Not every noun pair stacks (`a book about learning`
never becomes `a learning book`), and some stacked forms are simply not idiomatic
even when the logic works. Mark those honestly rather than rewarding the pattern.
