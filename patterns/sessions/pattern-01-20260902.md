# Pattern 01 - with + X + doing/done

Date: 2026-09-02 | Pattern: P001 | Status: open | Rounds so far: 0

Chosen by pattern-next: 0 drills, the only logged pattern. The gap is real and
measurable - 45 exchanges across 8 days, not one use of the structure. He builds
two flat clauses instead, or reaches for `since` / `because`, both of which are
correct, which is exactly why chat feedback can never surface this.

Material: every item is drawn from his own lines, Days 01-08. No invented
sentences.

Round 1 (join two given sentences, `with` up front) - tests that the structure
can be built at all.
Round 2 (mixed active/passive) - tests the doing/done decision, which is where
this will actually break. Item 2.3 needs `being done`, the hardest case.
Round 3 (situation only) - the round that predicts real use.

The rule being taught, deliberately avoiding the words active and passive: pull
the noun out, make a small sentence, and take whatever follows `be`.
  everyone -> everyone IS BOOKING     -> booking
  tickets  -> tickets ARE SOLD OUT    -> sold out
