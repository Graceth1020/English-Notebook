# Pattern 21 - stacked time adverbials (third pass, harder)

Date: 2026-10-27 | Pattern: P013 | Status: done | Rounds so far: 3

Requested by the learner ("begin P013 pattern drill. The drill can be more
difficulty"), overriding `pattern-next` (P003). Third pass, the day after
Drill 20 (8/13). Drill 20''s carry-forward retest list leads Round 1, all
on fresh items (Drills 14 and 20 content excluded):

1. **The day+date split with a week-reference** (1.1, discriminating) -
   Drill 20 item 3.2: `next week, November 7th` split the unit and
   double-anchored. Retested first.
2. **The one legal `of`** (1.2) - on the evening of December 31st.
3. **The meridiem trap** (1.3) - 半夜 12 点 this time (the other pole).
4. **between...and with a stripped day** (1.4) - tomorrow strips the
   preposition off the whole stack.
5. New difficulty: quarter hours (a quarter past seven), `the day after
   tomorrow` / `the day before yesterday` as stripped anchors, future
   perfect after by, imperative `not...until`.

Round 2 embeds with harder frames. Round 3: free production.

## Round 1 - build the time phrase

**1.** Pieces: at 10 am / Thursday / next week / November 6th  (the Drill-20 split - discriminating item)
**中文：**下周 11 月 6 号周四上午 10 点

**2.** Pieces: in the evening / December 31st  (the one legal of, retest)
**中文：**12 月 31 号晚上

**3.** Pieces: 12 / tomorrow night  (meridiem trap, midnight pole)
**中文：**明天半夜 12 点

**4.** Pieces: between 9 and 10 / tomorrow morning  (between...and + stripped tomorrow)
**中文：**明天早上 9 点到 10 点之间

**5.** Pieces: at a quarter past seven / Monday morning / two weeks ago  (four units + quarter hour)
**中文：**两周前周一早上 7 点一刻

**6.** Pieces: until noon / the day before yesterday  (until + stripped anchor)
**中文：**直到前天中午

## Round 2 - embed the stack in a sentence

**1.** 复盘会定在 11 月 6 号周四下午 3 点。(the split, embedded)
**中文：**复盘会定在 11 月 6 号周四下午 3 点。

**2.** 我们必须在后天半夜之前把它修好。(by + future, the day after tomorrow)
**中文：**我们必须在后天半夜之前把它修好。

**3.** 我昨天半夜 12 点还在改 bug。(meridiem in a sentence + still)
**中文：**我昨天半夜 12 点还在改 bug。

**4.** 我们前天上午把数据迁移完了。(in the morning + stripped anchor)
**中文：**我们前天上午把数据迁移完了。

**5.** 别等到周五下午才开始。(imperative not...until)
**中文：**别等到周五下午才开始。

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Schedule a release: full date, weekday, and a time window (between...and), using scheduled for.
**中文：**安排一次上线：完整日期、星期几、一个时间窗（between...and），用 scheduled for。

**2.** Describe a late night: what you were doing until a deadline (by/until), and what time you finally slept (not...until).
**中文：**描述一次熬夜：到几点为止在干什么（by/until），几点才睡（not...until）。

**3.** Tell a story that happened on the morning of a specific date.
**中文：**讲一个发生在某月某号上午的故事。
