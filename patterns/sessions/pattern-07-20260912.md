# Pattern 07 - none of + noun (total negation)

Date: 2026-09-12 | Pattern: P007 | Status: done | Rounds so far: 3

Born from a live error, not from absence. Day 14, exchange 3:

  "After the ticket shipped, all users couldn't pay their orders anymore."

The intended meaning was 所有用户都没法支付 - zero users could pay. English reads
`all ... not` as partial negation: "not all users could pay", i.e. some could.
The sentence says the opposite of what happened, which in an incident report is
the worst possible failure mode.

The negation family:

  TOTAL    None of the users could pay.        (zero could)
  PARTIAL  Not all users could pay.            (some could, some couldn't)
  TWO      Neither provider worked.            (exactly two, both failed)
  UNCOUNT  None of the work is done.           (singular verb)

The escape hatch: `all` + a verb that carries its own negation is fine -
`All the tests failed` means every test failed. The forbidden shape is
`all ... not-verb`, where the `not` scopes over `all` and flips the meaning.

P002 tie-in: pronouns after `of` are object pronouns - `none of us`,
`none of them`, never `none of we`.

Round 1 spreads the forms deliberately (see pattern-drills.md). The partial
negation item is #2 - early, discriminating, and the trap is reaching for
`none of` when the meaning is only partial.
Round 2 mixes all four decisions: total vs partial, countable vs uncountable,
group vs two, and the forbidden `all ... not` shape.
Round 3: free production, 4 items.

## Round 1 - rewrite the sentence with the right negation frame

**1.** After the ticket shipped, all users couldn't pay.
**中文：**上线后，没有一个用户能支付。

**2.** Some of the team's docs are up to date. Some are not.
**中文：**不是所有文档都是最新的。

**3.** The queue held every alert. Zero got out.
**中文：**所有告警都没发出去。

**4.** We looked at two SMS providers. Both were too expensive.
**中文：**两个短信供应商都太贵了。

## Round 2 - mixed, choose the right shape

**1.** 不是所有同事都看文档。(some do, some don't - careful)
**中文：**不是所有同事都看文档。

**2.** 没有一个用户能支付。(zero could - careful)
**中文：**没有一个用户能支付。

**3.** 这项工作一点都没做完。(uncountable)
**中文：**这项工作一点都没做完。

**4.** 我们两个人都没去过那家图书馆。(two people, pronoun after of)
**中文：**我们两个人都没去过那家图书馆。

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Retell the Day 14 incident: after the coupon ticket shipped, what happened to users' payments?
**中文：**重述 Day 14 的事故：优惠券 ticket 上线后，用户的支付发生了什么？

**2.** Tell a teammate the monitoring queue dropped every alert during the incident.
**中文：**跟同事说，事故期间监控队列把所有告警都吞了。

**3.** Someone asks whether your whole team keeps docs up to date. The honest answer is some do, some don't.
**中文：**有人问你们团队是不是所有人都及时更新文档。诚实回答：有人更新，有人不更新。

**4.** You wanted to try two coffee shops near the library. Both were closed.
**中文：**你想去图书馆附近的两家咖啡店，两家都关门了。

Expanded at the learner's request - more free production, weighted toward the
uncountable + singular verb decision and total-vs-partial discrimination:

**5.** During the National Day drive home, the traffic on the highway barely moved. (uncountable)
**中文：**国庆开车回家，高速上车流几乎一动不动。

**6.** Someone asks if everyone on your team wants to work remote. Some do, some don't. (partial)
**中文：**有人问你们团队是不是所有人都想远程。有人想，有人不想。

**7.** The test suite ran overnight. Zero tests passed. (total, plural)
**中文：**测试套件跑了一晚上，一个都没过。

**8.** You asked two teammates to review your ticket. Neither has replied. (two)
**中文：**你请了两个同事 review 你的 ticket，两人都没回。

**9.** The wiki has plenty of pages, but the information in it is never current. (uncountable)
**中文：**wiki 页面不少，但里面的信息没有一条是最新的。
