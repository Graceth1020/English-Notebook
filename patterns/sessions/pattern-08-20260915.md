# Pattern 08 - reduced relative clause

Date: 2026-09-15 | Pattern: P008 | Status: open | Rounds so far: 0

Logged on Day 17 from exchange 2:

  "The engineer who is responsible for the function ends up dealing with it."

The full clause is correct, so nothing errored - the reduced form is simply absent.
`pattern-next` offered P002 for the eighth drill running; vetoed again.

## The Gap, stated honestly

The inventory row says the reduced form "has never once appeared". Checking the
corpus before building this drill, that is not quite true, and the correction
matters for what to drill:

- Day 17, the coupon incident: *the order amount **displayed on the page** was ten
  times what they'd actually spent*. That IS a reduced relative clause - `which was
  displayed` with both words dropped. It came out unprompted.
- Day 16: *I have had a game **installed for years*** - same shape. (Codex's opening
  line had used `installed ages ago`, so it may have been primed.)

What is actually missing is the **adjective and prepositional-phrase reduction**,
where `be` is the whole verb and what survives is not a participle:

  the engineer who is responsible   ->  the engineer responsible     never used
  projects which were unrelated     ->  unrelated projects           never used
  the teammate who is in charge of  ->  the teammate in charge of    never used

So the -ed branch is already available. This drill spends its discriminating
items on the adjective/PP branch and on the trap, not on past participles.

## The Trap

`who/which + be` drops. `who/which + a lexical verb` does NOT drop to nothing - it
leaves a finite verb stranded in the middle of a noun phrase:

  a rig which can run anything        ->  a rig can run anything      finite verb
  the engineer who works on payments  ->  the engineer works on payments is on leave

**One test covers both branches: after the reduction, no finite verb may be left
standing.** `responsible` and `displayed` are not finite; `works` and `can run` are.

The middle case - a lexical verb that reduces to a participle rather than to
nothing - belongs to P001/P006 and is included in Round 2 as a boundary item.

Round 1 spreads four forms (adjective, past participle, -ing, prepositional
phrase) per the rule added after Drill 06.
Round 2 carries the two traps, one at each end, plus the object-pronoun drop.
Round 3: free production, 4 items.

## Round 1 - reduce the clause

**1.** The engineer is responsible for the feature. He ends up dealing with it.
**中文：**负责这个功能的工程师最终会来处理它。

**2.** The amount was displayed on the page. It was ten times what they had spent.
**中文：**页面上显示的金额是他们实际花费的十倍。

**3.** The cars were heading in the same direction. They made the traffic brutal.
**中文：**同向行驶的车让路况变得很糟。

**4.** The teammate is in charge of the payment module. She is on leave this week.
**中文：**负责支付模块的同事这周请假了。

## Round 2 - mixed, choose the right shape

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** At your company, who handles an API when it goes down? Describe that person.
**中文：**你们公司某个 API 挂了的时候由谁处理？描述一下这个人。

**2.** Explain the coupon incident: what was wrong with the amount on the page?
**中文：**说说优惠券那次事故：页面上显示的金额出了什么问题？

**3.** Talk about the old weekly meeting and the projects your team had at the time.
**中文：**说说以前那个周会，以及当时你们团队的项目。

**4.** Describe the drive home during the holiday and the cars on the highway.
**中文：**描述国庆开车回家时，高速上的车。
