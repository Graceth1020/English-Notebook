# Pattern 03 - the more X, the more Y

Date: 2026-09-06 | Pattern: P004 | Status: done | Rounds so far: 3

Requested by the learner ("more... more..."), logged as `P004` on Day 11.
`pattern-next` had offered `P002` (object pronoun after a preposition); vetoed in
favour of this one, which is the learner's pick.

Same profile as P001 and P003: 0 uses in 55 exchanges over 11 days, never once
produced wrongly. The proportional link is always said as two flat sentences or
an `if` / `once` clause - Day 11's *Once I've got more experience, I think I'll be
able to handle it better*, Day 03's *depends on the commute, but if it's a long
one, definitely remote*. Both correct, so no error row could ever exist for it.

Material from Days 01-11 only. Round 1 items are all rebuilds of his own two-clause
versions, so he can see the fold happen.

Round 1 (join two given sentences) - can the frame be built at all.
Round 2 (mixed) - carries the trap: `more and more` is a *steady increase with one
variable*, not a proportional link, and stacking it into `the more..., the more...`
is the over-application to watch for. Also tests the noun slot (`the more time`),
the dropped `be` (`the worse the traffic,`), and the word order - no `do`, no
`that`, subject straight after the comparative.
Round 3 (situation only) - 4 items, deliberately large. Drill 02's finding was
that his first instinct reaches for the old shape and his second switches, so the
bottleneck is the number of first instincts, not explanation.

The rule being taught, in the shortest runnable form:

  the + comparative + [subject verb] , the + comparative + [subject verb]
  the longer the commute (is)        , the less I want to go in

  Both halves front the comparative. Nothing else moves - no `do`, no `that`,
  and `is` can drop when the half is just noun + be + adjective.

Watch for: `more and more` swallowed into the frame, `the more better`, and a
trailing version (*I want to go in less the longer the commute is*) - the same
position habit that showed up in Round 1 of Drill 01.

## Round 1 - join two given sentences

**1.** The commute is long. I want to go into the office less.
**中文：**通勤越长，我越不想去公司。

**2.** I get more experience. I handle complicated tickets better.
**中文：**经验越多，处理复杂 ticket 就越顺手。

**3.** I read more guides. I unlock more hidden endings.
**中文：**攻略看得越多，解锁的隐藏结局就越多。

**4.** More people book at the same time. It gets harder to get a ticket.
**中文：**同一时间订票的人越多，票就越难抢。

## Round 2 - mixed, choose the right shape

**1.** AI is developing quickly, and it keeps speeding up. (no second variable)
**中文：**AI 发展越来越快。

**2.** I spend more time in the library. I get more done.
**中文：**在图书馆待的时间越长，做完的事越多。

**3.** The traffic is bad. The commute is brutal.
**中文：**路况越差，通勤越难熬。

**4.** I work longer hours. I sleep worse.
**中文：**工作时间越长，睡得越差。

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** A colleague asks why you would rather work remote. Link the commute to how you feel about going in.
**中文：**同事问你为什么想远程，把通勤和你想不想去公司连起来说。

**2.** Explain why you keep reading Clean Code at weekends.
**中文：**解释你为什么周末一直在读《Clean Code》。

**3.** Tell a teammate that a change touching core logic takes longer to test.
**中文：**跟同事说，改动碰到的核心逻辑越多，测试就越久。

**4.** Say what you have noticed about your own English after eleven sessions.
**中文：**说说十一次之后你对自己英语的观察。
