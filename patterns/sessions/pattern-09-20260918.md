# Pattern 09 - object pronoun after a preposition

Date: 2026-09-18 | Pattern: P002 | Status: done | Rounds so far: 3

The oldest open pattern in the inventory - logged on Day 08, offered by
`pattern-next` for eight consecutive drills, vetoed seven times. Now requested.

## The Gap

The error row is a single line from Drill 01: *with I trying to learn*. One
occurrence in the entire corpus, and that is the point - **nouns carry no case
marking, so the error can only surface when a pronoun follows a preposition**.
*With the change touching core logic* gives English nothing to mark; *with I* /
*with me* is where the case choice is exposed.

So the question this drill answers is not "is `with me` known" - it obviously is,
in isolation. It is whether the object form survives (a) coordination and
(b) the hypercorrection pressure that makes `between you and I` sound educated.
The wrong model to kill is "I is always more correct/polite than me".

## The Rule

```
after a preposition -> object form:  me / him / her / us / them

with me    for him    to her    between us    none of them
```

**The coordination test** - the only one worth keeping: when a pronoun shares the
slot with a noun (`for my wife and ___`), delete the noun and say it alone:

```
for my wife and ___   ->  for my wife   ->  delete  ->  for I?  no  ->  for me
between you and ___   ->  between you   ->  delete  ->  between I? no ->  between me
```

Whatever survives alone is what goes in the joint slot. `and` changes nothing.

**The trap runs both ways**: hypercorrection - `between you and I`, `for my
colleagues and I` - is the same error as `with I`, dressed up as carefulness.
The `I` in `between you and I` is not more formal; it is just wrong.

**Subject slots keep the subject form**: `He and I went through the logs`, never
`Him and I went`. The case test is about position, not politeness.

Round 1 spreads four prepositions and three pronoun persons, with the coordination
item at #4 - early, per the standing rule, because a wrong model produces
right-sounding answers on every uncoordinated item.

Round 2 carries the two-way trap: one hypercorrection item, one subject-position
item where the object form would be wrong.

Round 3: free production, 4 items, at least one coordinated.

## Round 1 - complete with the right pronoun form

**1.** With ___ (I) trying to learn so much, weekends are never long enough.
**中文：**我要学的东西这么多，周末根本不够用。

**2.** The PM assigned the ticket to ___ (he).
**中文：**PM 把这个 ticket 派给了他。

**3.** The decision is between ___ (we) - nobody else gets a say.
**中文：**决定权在我们之间，别人没有发言权。

**4.** My wife has already seen the film, so this ticket is for my wife and ___ (I). (careful)
**中文：**我妻子已经看过了，这张票是给我和她的。

## Round 2 - mixed, choose the right form

**1.** I went through the logs with ___ (my colleague) on Saturday. Now say it with a pronoun instead.
**中文：**我周六和同事一起查了日志。换成代词再说一遍。

**2.** Just between you and ___ (I/me), I think the deadline is impossible. (careful - which one sounds right, and which one IS right?)
**中文：**这话就咱俩知道，我觉得这个 deadline 根本不现实。

**3.** ___ (he) and I found the root cause on Saturday evening. (careful - different slot this time)
**中文：**我和他周六晚上找到了根因。

**4.** None of ___ (they) replied to my review request.
**中文：**他们谁也没回我的 review 请求。

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Say who you usually play basketball with, and what happens when more neighbours join.
**中文：**说说你平时和谁打篮球，邻居越聚越多之后是什么情形。

**2.** A teammate asks why the ticket took two days. Explain it was split between you and one other person.
**中文：**同事问为什么这个 ticket 花了两天。解释说这活是你和另一个人分的。

**3.** Tell someone learning English is hard for you personally, and why weekends help.
**中文：**跟别人说学英语对你个人来说很难，以及为什么周末有帮助。

**4.** Describe a time you and your parents disagreed about something small. (needs a coordinated pronoun)
**中文：**说一次你和父母在小事上的分歧。
