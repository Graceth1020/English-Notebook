# Pattern 20 - stacked time adverbials (second pass, harder)

Date: 2026-10-26 | Pattern: P013 | Rounds: 3 | Items: 13 | Correct: 8/13

## The Gap

Second drill on P013, requested by the learner ("begin P013 pattern drill.
The drill can be more difficulty"), overriding `pattern-next` (P003).
Drill 14 went 6/6 on assembly, so the ceiling went up: four-unit stacks,
the meridiem trap, `not...until`, `by` + past perfect, and the Drill-16
miss (`scheduled for` + day + date) as the discriminating item.

## The Rule

at + clock time, on + day/date, in + bare part of day. Small to large, zero
glue. Day+date is ONE unit under a single `on` (at 6:30 pm on Friday,
October 24th). last/this/next/yesterday strip all prepositions. midnight
and noon are always bare (by midnight). The one legal `of`: on the morning
of the 15th. am/pm already names the part of day - never add
`in the morning` after `am`.

## Round 1 - build the time phrase

**1.** Pieces: at 6:30 pm / Friday / October 24th (the Drill-16 miss - discriminating item)

**中文：**10 月 24 号周五下午 6 点半

**You wrote:**
> at 6:30 pm on Friday, October 24th.

**Answer:**
> at 6:30 pm on Friday, October 24th

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** The discriminating item, and the Drill-16 miss repaired: `on`
governs Friday + October 24th as one unit, zero glue.

---

**2.** Pieces: by midnight / New Year''s Eve

**中文：**到除夕半夜为止

**You wrote:**
> by midnight on New Year''s Eve

**Answer:**
> by midnight on New Year''s Eve

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** midnight bare after `by` (Drill 14''s `by the midnight` does not
return), and a named day takes `on`.

---

**3.** Pieces: in the morning / the 15th (the one legal of)

**中文：**15 号早上

**You wrote:**
> in the morning on the 15th.

**Answer:**
> on the morning of the 15th

**Verdict:** wrong form

**Why:** Once a part-of-day carries a specific date, the whole phrase is
day-bound: `on` claims the morning, the date hangs on `of` - the one legal
`of` in the family. `in the morning` is only for dateless mornings.

---

**4.** Pieces: around 2 / this coming Sunday afternoon

**中文：**这个周日下午 2 点左右

**You wrote:**
> around 2 this coming Sunday afternoon.

**Answer:**
> around 2 this coming Sunday afternoon

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** `this coming` strips all prepositions, correctly.

---

**5.** Pieces: between 2 and 4 / Friday afternoon / last week (four-unit stack)

**中文：**上周五下午 2 点到 4 点之间

**You wrote:**
> between 2 and 4 on Friday afternoon last week.

**Answer:**
> between 2 and 4 on Friday afternoon last week

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** Four units, perfect order, one `on` doing all the day work.

---

**6.** Pieces: 12 / yesterday midday (meridiem trap)

**中文：**昨天中午 12 点

**You wrote:**
> at 12 at yesterday midday.

**Answer:**
> at noon yesterday / at 12 pm yesterday

**Verdict:** wrong form

**Why:** Double `at`, and bare `12` is unreadable (12 am = midnight, 12 pm =
noon). The Drill-14 avoidance strategy is the answer: say noon / midnight.

## Round 2 - embed the stack in a sentence

**1.** 会议改到 10 月 24 号周五下午 6:30。

**中文：**会议改到 10 月 24 号周五下午 6 点半。

**You wrote:**
> The meeting was moved to 6:30 pm on Friday, October 24th.

**Answer:**
> The meeting was moved to 6:30 pm on Friday, October 24th.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** The Drill-16 miss repaired in a sentence.

---

**2.** 到除夕半夜为止，所有的票都卖光了。

**中文：**到除夕半夜为止，所有的票都卖光了。

**You wrote:**
> All tickets were sold out by midnight on New Year''s Eve.

**Answer:**
> By midnight on New Year''s Eve, all the tickets had sold out.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** Grammatical as written (state reading). The tighter frame for
到...为止(已经) is past perfect with the deadline fronted. Both on record.

---

**3.** 我们把发布定在下周二早上 10 点到 11 点之间。

**中文：**我们把发布定在下周二早上 10 点到 11 点之间。

**You wrote:**
> The press conference is scheduled between 10 to 11 next Tuesday morning.

**Answer:**
> The release is scheduled for between 10 and 11 on Tuesday morning next week.

**Verdict:** wrong form

**Why:** The range frame is `between X and Y`, never `between X to Y`
(`from 10 to 11` is the frame that takes `to`). Not counted: `scheduled`
wants `for`; 发布会 here is a software release - a press conference is
记者招待会.

---

**4.** 我昨天晚上 11 点才到家。(not...until frame)

**中文：**我昨天晚上 11 点才到家。

**You wrote:**
> I got home at 11 last night.

**Answer:**
> I didn''t get home until 11 last night.

**Verdict:** wrong form

**Why:** The stack is fine but 才 disappeared - `not...until` is how English
carries the lateness. That frame was the item''s point. Note: it self-
repaired in Round 3 item 3, unprompted, an hour later.

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Narrate last weekend with at least three stacked times, one with a part of day and one deadline (by).

**中文：**复盘你的上个周末：至少三个时间堆叠，一个带上午/下午，一个用到...为止。

**You wrote:**
> Last Saturday, I went to charge my car with my wife at 11 am in the morning. And I drove back to my university with my friends at 4 pm in the afternoon. I had to get home by 9 pm so I left early.

**Answer:**
> Last Saturday, I went to charge my car with my wife at 11 in the morning. Then I drove back to my university with some friends at 4 in the afternoon. I had to be home by 9 pm, so I left early.

**Verdict:** wrong form

**Why:** `Last Saturday` and `by 9 pm` are perfect, but `11 am in the
morning` doubles the part of day - `am` already means morning. Pick one:
at 11 am / at 11 in the morning. This exact redundancy was Drill 14''s
Round-2 lesson, so it counts this time.

---

**2.** Schedule a meeting for a teammate: full date, weekday, part of day, clock time.

**中文：**给同事约个会：完整日期、星期几、上午还是下午、具体钟点。

**You wrote:**
> I want to book a room for the meeting which will be scheduled for between 10 and 11 on Friday morning next week, November 7th.

**Answer:**
> I want to book a room for the meeting scheduled for between 10 and 11 on Friday morning, November 7th.

**Verdict:** wrong form

**Why:** `between 10 and 11` came out right - an hour after Round 2''s
`between...to`. The miss is the date: `next week, November 7th` splits the
day+date unit and double-anchors the week. Day+date live together under one
`on`: on Friday morning, November 7th - drop `next week`, the date already
says it.

---

**3.** Report an incident timeline: when the alert fired, when you rolled back, by when it was fixed.

**中文：**汇报一次事故时间线：警报什么时候响的，什么时候回滚的，到什么时候修好的。

**You wrote:**
> I received the alert message around 11 pm last Friday, and we rolled back the release at 11.30 pm after we had checked the logs. The fix didn''t ship until 1 am.

**Answer:**
> I received the alert around 11 pm last Friday, and we rolled back the release at 11:30 pm after we''d checked the logs. The fix didn''t ship until 1 am.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** Three stacks, all correct, and the `not...until` frame self-
repaired here, unprompted, an hour after Round 2 item 4 missed it. The
past-perfect anchor (after we had checked) is right. Not counted: English
clock times use a colon (11:30).
