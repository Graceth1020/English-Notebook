# Pattern 09 - object pronoun after a preposition

Date: 2026-09-18 | Pattern: P002 | Rounds: 3 | Items: 12 | Correct: 9/12

## The Gap

The oldest open pattern - logged on Day 08, offered by `pattern-next` for eight
consecutive drills, vetoed seven times before being requested.

The gap is a case choice that nouns never expose. One logged occurrence in the
corpus (Drill 01: *with I trying to learn*), which is exactly the point:
*with the change touching core logic* gives English nothing to mark. The error
can only surface when a pronoun follows a preposition.

## The Rule

```
after a preposition  ->  object form:  me / him / her / us / them

with me    for him    to her    between us    none of them
```

**The coordination test** - when a pronoun shares the slot with a noun, delete
the noun and say it alone:

```
for my wife and ___   ->  for I?  no  ->  for me
between you and ___   ->  between I? no ->  between me
```

Whatever survives alone goes in the joint slot. `and` changes nothing.

**The trap runs both ways.** *Between you and I* is the same error as *with I*,
dressed up as carefulness - hypercorrection, not politeness. And subject slots
keep the subject form: *He and I went through the logs*, never *Him and I went*.

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Say who you usually play basketball with, and what happens when more neighbours join.

**中文：**说说你平时和谁打篮球，邻居越聚越多之后是什么情形。

**You wrote:**
> I always go to play basketball with my neighbors. With more neighbors joining, the game is more fierce than it used to be.

**Answer:**
> I usually play with my neighbours - with more of them joining, the games are fiercer than they used to be.

**Verdict:** not the pattern

**Why:** `with my neighbors` is a noun - the case slot never opened. `With more neighbors joining` is P001 unprompted, worth noticing. Not counted: `go to play` -> `go play`, `more fierce` -> `fiercer`.

**2.** A teammate asks why the ticket took two days. Explain it was split between you and one other person.

**中文：**同事问为什么这个 ticket 花了两天。解释说这活是你和另一个人分的。

**You wrote:**
> Because Jack and I are responsible for the different part of the project.

**Verdict:** not the pattern

**Why:** `Jack and I` is the right case - for a subject. The cue wanted the preposition slot (`between Jack and me`), and the sentence was restructured so it disappeared. The case decision made was correct; the one being drilled never happened.

## What Went Wrong

- **No errors at all. The finding is avoidance.** Rounds 1-2, where the slot was
  forced: 8/8, including both traps. Round 3, given freedom: the pronoun slot was
  routed around three times out of four - with a noun (3.1), with a restructure
  (3.2), and by dropping the phrase (3.3). This is precisely the gap the inventory
  recorded: *nouns hide it, only pronouns expose it*. A drill can force the case
  choice; it cannot force the slot to exist.

- **The one pattern production in free production (3.4) came from the only cue
  that named the pronoun implicitly** - "you and your parents" primes `us`. Where
  the cue named nouns (my neighbours, one other person), nouns came out.

- **A flawed cue in Round 1** (1.4): a wife who has already seen the film cannot
  hold the ticket, and the learner's `for us` was the smooth way out of a broken
  prompt. Recorded as a drill-design error.

## The Pattern Behind The Pattern

Seventh drill, and a new shape. Every previous drill scored errors: wrong forms,
wrong words, wrong models. This one scored **absence under freedom** - the first
drill where nothing was wrong and the round was still a miss. Which confirms the
track's founding claim from a new angle: a pattern that is never wrong can be
drilled to recognition in an afternoon, and recognition changes nothing about
whether the slot opens in production. For this pattern especially, `owned` can
only come from chat - and even there, only on a turn where a pronoun happens to
follow a preposition.

## Carry Into Chat

1. Watch for pronoun-after-preposition slots specifically: `with me`, `for him`,
   `between us`, `to them`. The case choice itself is drilled; the question is
   whether the slot opens.
2. Watch for hypercorrection - `between you and I`, `for my colleagues and I` -
   especially in work-register turns where the learner is being careful.
3. A `with I` / `for I` in chat would be a genuine recurrence of the Day 08
   shape and should be logged as an error row.
4. P001 appeared unprompted in this drill (3.1); it is already `owned`, no
   logging needed.

`drilled`, not `owned`. Two unprompted uses in normal chat earn `pending`, and a
third in a different scene earns `owned`.
