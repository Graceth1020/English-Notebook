# Pattern 13 - post-noun modifier hangers

Date: 2026-09-30 | Pattern: P012 | Rounds: 3 | Items: 11 | Correct: 6/11

## The Gap

Chinese stacks every modifier before the noun (的-chains); English says the head noun first and hangs each modifier after it on its own hook. Learner-raised: he asked how to say 那边那个正在球场打球的穿着黑色球衣的长头发的带着眼镜的男生. The same family surfaced in Day 24's noun pile "the unit of the amount saved in database" (the information-noun branch, drilled separately as P011/pattern-12 the same day). What the pattern buys: pointing a person or thing out in one phrase instead of a run of "and" sentences. Drill spanned two days (Round 1 on 09-30, retry + Rounds 2-3 on 10-01).

## The Rule

Head noun first. One 的 = one hook:

- 固有特征 -> with + noun (with long hair and glasses)
- 穿着 -> in + clothing (in a black jersey)
- 正在做 -> -ing, no "is" (playing on the court)
- X坏了/裂了/缺了 (a state) -> with + noun + -ed (with a cracked screen)
- 方位 -> place phrase, last (over there)
- 习惯性/一般动作 -> no hook fits: who-clause (who keeps asking questions)

Ceiling: 2-3 hooks, then split - short sentence + dash list of bare features.

## Round 1 - build the noun phrase

**1.** Pieces: a guy / long hair / glasses

**中文：**那个长头发、戴眼镜的男生

**You wrote:**
> the guy with long hair and glasses

**Answer:**
> the guy with long hair and glasses

**Verdict:** ok

**Why:** Worked example, echoed clean - same-category features share one "with".

---

**2.** Pieces: a guy / he is wearing a black jersey

**中文：**那个穿着黑色球衣的男生

**You wrote:**
> a guy wearing a black jersey

**Answer:**
> the guy in the black jersey

**Verdict:** not the pattern

**Why:** "Wearing" is legal English but dodged the item's decision - 穿着 takes the in-hook when pointing someone out. Also 那个 = "the". Landed on retry.

---

**3.** Pieces: a boy / he is playing basketball / on the court over there

**中文：**那边那个正在球场打篮球的男生

**You wrote:**
> a boy is playing basketball on the court over there

**Answer:**
> the boy playing basketball on the court over there

**Verdict:** wrong form

**Why:** 正在做 promoted to a full verb - "a boy IS playing" is a sentence, not a phrase. Chop "is", hang the -ing. Landed on retry; "playing the basketball" lost its stray "the" (sports take no article).

---

**4.** Pieces: a guy / long hair + glasses / black jersey / playing on the court / over there

**中文：**那边那个穿着黑色球衣、正在打球的长头发戴眼镜的男生

**You wrote:**
> a guy with long hair and glasses wearing a black jersey is playing basketball on the court over there.

**Answer:**
> the guy with long hair and glasses, in the black jersey, playing basketball on the court over there

**Verdict:** wrong form

**Why:** Same promotion, doubled - "wearing" + "is playing" forced a full sentence. Landed clean on retry: the full four-hook chain.

## Round 2 - choose the hook yourself

**1.** 那个在会上一直问问题的实习生

**中文：**那个在会上一直问问题的实习生

**You wrote:**
> the intern who keeps asking questions in the meeting

**Answer:**
> the intern who keeps asking questions in the meeting

**Verdict:** ok

**Why:** The discriminating item passed - habitual action has no hook, correctly escaped to a who-clause.

---

**2.** 门口那个背红色双肩包的男生

**中文：**门口那个背红色双肩包的男生

**You wrote:**
> the boy with a red backpack behind the door

**Answer:**
> the boy with a red backpack at the door

**Verdict:** ok

**Why:** Hooks right; 门口 = at the door ("behind the door" is 在门后面). Retry came back "the body" - typo for "the boy", flagged.

---

**3.** 那个穿白衬衫、正在跟经理说话的女士

**中文：**那个穿白衬衫、正在跟经理说话的女士

**You wrote:**
> a lady in a white shirt, talking with the manager

**Answer:**
> the lady in a white shirt talking to the manager

**Verdict:** ok

**Why:** Hooks right; "the lady" (那个), and 跟某人说话 = talk to (talk with implies a two-way conversation). Both fixed on retry.

---

**4.** 客厅那台屏幕裂了的旧手机

**中文：**客厅那台屏幕裂了的旧手机

**You wrote:**
> The old phone whose screen is broken in the living room

**Answer:**
> the old phone with a cracked screen in the living room

**Verdict:** wrong form

**Why:** An object's state went to a whose-clause - stiff for a thing, and it swallowed the place phrase (the screen broke "in the living room"). New hook: with + noun + -ed. Landed on retry ("the phone..." - dropped "old", noted).

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Your new colleague wants to see your gaming rig. Point it out: "It's the one ___."

**中文：**新同事想看你的游戏主机,你指给他看:"就是那台……"

**You wrote:**
> It's the red one with its screen on sitting on the desk.

**Answer:**
> It's the red one with its screen on, sitting on the desk.

**Verdict:** ok

**Why:** Both hooks fire; a comma is needed when the first hook already contains "on".

---

**2.** A friend is coming to watch you play basketball. Tell him how to spot you: "I'm the one ___."

**中文：**朋友来球场看你打球,你告诉他怎么认出你:"我就是那个……"

**You wrote:**
> I'm the one with long black hair and white skin, in a black basketball outfit and Kobe brand basketball shoes.

**Answer:**
> I'm the one with long black hair and fair skin, in a black basketball outfit and Kobes.

**Verdict:** ok

**Why:** Assembly clean, adjective order right (long black hair). Vocabulary: 皮肤白 = fair skin; 科比牌球鞋 = Kobes.

---

**3.** Describe the most distinctive person at your court or office - as many features as you can, the SPLIT way.

**中文：**描述你球场或公司里最有辨识度的一个人,特征尽量多,用拆分的方式说。

**You wrote:**
> Mike, with long yellow hair, green eyes, white skin and a pair of glasses, in a Nike brand shoes, white shirt and blue jeans, coding in the office, is my leader.

**Answer:**
> Mike's the one coding in the office. Long blond hair, gold-rimmed glasses, green eyes, fair skin, white shirt and blue jeans, Nikes - that's my leader.

**Verdict:** not the pattern

**Why:** Every hook fired, but seven features in one sentence is exactly the overload the split exists for. Word traps inside: 黄头发 = blond (not yellow), 金丝眼镜 = gold-rimmed, "a Nike brand shoes" (a + plural; 牌 is just the brand name), "white shirt" missing "a". Retry landed the full split, all vocabulary fixed - "That's" after the dash should be lowercase, the one surviving slip.

## What Went Wrong

- The recurring mechanism across all three rounds: **when no hook is known, the modifier gets promoted to a full verb and the phrase becomes a sentence** ("a boy IS playing", "wearing a black jersey is playing", "whose screen is broken"). The drill added the two missing hooks (in for clothing; with + noun + -ed for states) plus the who-clause escape hatch, and the promotion stopped.
- The second finding: **the split is a separate skill from the hooks.** Item 3's first pass had every hook right and still failed the item - seven features in one sentence is listener overload regardless of correctness.
- Word-level traps banked along the way: blond (not yellow) hair, fair (not white) skin, gold-rimmed glasses, Kobes/Nikes (no "a", no "brand").

## Carry Into Chat

1. `P012` is drilled. Two unprompted hanger assemblies in daily chat earn `owned` - watch for "the guy with...", "the one in...", "the X playing/sitting/talking...", especially when he describes a person or points a thing out.
2. The 4+ hook split (short sentence + dash list) is the capstone - watch for any long description staying split instead of chaining.
3. Vocabulary landed inside the drill: blond / fair / gold-rimmed / Kobes - recognition-level at minimum; an unprompted production of any of them in chat is worth a good-line.
