Date: 2026-10-23 | Pattern: P014 | Rounds: 3 | Items: 17 | Correct: 15/17

## The Gap

questions should never appear before." Every item in Drills 15/16/17 was

## The Rule

on = surface/line/topic, in = container, of = part-whole. Platforms are
surfaces (on Stack Overflow / on Bilibili); the off-pair follows the verb
(get OFF, see ON). Texts are containers: a typo IN the message, buried IN
the logs. Topics are surfaces: an article ON spaced repetition.

by + bare noun = transport/means only. Tool -> with. Method -> by + -ing.
Deadline -> by + time. Fixed collocations: rely on, depend on, lie in,
thanks to, down to, based on.

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Tell a teammate how you made the interface faster: what tool you used, what method.

**中文：**跟同事说你怎么把接口提速的：用了什么工具，用了什么方法。

**You wrote:**

**Answer:**
> We sped the interface up with a cache - by caching the service metadata.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** Both slots exercised (rely on + by caching), both prepositions
correct. Not counted: the sentence is circular - the cache and the caching
are the same thing said twice; and `metadata` is one word.
