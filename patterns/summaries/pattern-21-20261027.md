Date: 2026-10-27 | Pattern: P013 | Rounds: 3 | Items: 14 | Correct: 8/14

## The Gap

"more difficulty" brief. Drill 20''s retest list led Round 1 on fresh items:
the day+date split with a week-reference, the one legal `of`, the meridiem

## The Rule

at + clock, on + day/date, in + bare part of day. Small to large, zero glue.
Day+date is one unit under a single `on`. last/this/next/yesterday/
tomorrow/the day before yesterday/the day after tomorrow strip ALL
prepositions - no `on`, no `of`. midnight/noon always bare, and SAY them
instead of 12 am/12 pm. The one legal `of`: on the morning/evening of + a
date. `scheduled for` and `until` swallow the clock preposition
(scheduled for 3 pm, not for at 3 pm). `by` marks a completion deadline;
an ongoing activity takes `until`.

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Schedule a release: full date, weekday, and a time window (between...and), using scheduled for.

**中文：**安排一次上线：完整日期、星期几、一个时间窗（between...and），用 scheduled for。

**You wrote:**
> The coupon feature is scheduled for between 10 pm and 11 pm on Friday, October 30th.

**Answer:**
> The coupon feature is scheduled for between 10 and 11 pm on Friday, October 30th.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** Three former misses repaired in one sentence: `scheduled for` with
no stray `at`, `between...and`, day+date under one `on`.

---

**2.** Describe a late night: what you were doing until a deadline (by/until), and what time you finally slept (not...until).

**中文：**描述一次熬夜：到几点为止在干什么（by/until），几点才睡（not...until）。

**You wrote:**
> I was reading a book called how to invest by 12 am, and I didn''t sleep until 2 am.

**Answer:**
> I was up reading a book called How to Invest until midnight, and I didn''t get to sleep until 2 am.

**Verdict:** wrong form

**Why:** The `not...until` half is perfect. The first half: `12 am` should
be said as `midnight`, and `by` marks a completion deadline - an ongoing
activity takes `until` (reading until midnight). As written it reads as
"by midnight I had STARTED reading." Not counted: book titles take
capitals.

---

**3.** Tell a story that happened on the morning of a specific date.

**中文：**讲一个发生在某月某号上午的故事。

**You wrote:**
> I was teaching how to drive a car last Saturday, October 25th.

**Answer:**
> I was teaching my wife to drive on the morning of October 25th.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** The stack (`last Saturday, October 25th`) is a legitimate
appositive. But the target slot (`on the morning of + date`) went unused -
unconfirmed in production. Not counted: `teach` takes a person (teaching
my wife to drive).
