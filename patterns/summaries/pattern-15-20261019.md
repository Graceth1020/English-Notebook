# Pattern 15 - abstract prepositions by spatial metaphor

Date: 2026-10-19 | Pattern: P014 | Rounds: 3 | Items: 13 | Correct: 10/13

## The Gap

Learner-raised (Day 33 QA): abstract on/in/of feel random. They are not -
they project three physical prototypes: on = surface/line, in = container,
of = part-of-whole. Decision procedure: ask what the thing physically
resembles. Covers ~90% of abstract uses; the rest are fixed collocations.

## The Rule

on for surfaces and lines (a comment on line 42, on the dashboard, on
Rednote, a book on grammar, on Monday); in for containers (in the save
function, in the third paragraph, in October, in progress); of for
part-whole (the root cause of the incident, the color of the button).
Platforms are SURFACES not containers: on Rednote, not "in Rednote" - and
the off-Round of on is off (I got this off Rednote). Fixed collocations do
not follow the metaphor: rely on, lie in, depend on - learned one by one.

**3.** 问题的根源在于配置。
**中文：** 问题的根源在于配置。
**You wrote:**
> The root cause of the problem is down to configuration.
**Answer:**
> The root of the problem lies in the configuration.
**Verdict:** correct
**Why:** Taught collocation was lies in; be down to is a correct idiomatic alternative (BrE informal, = 归咎于). Both kept.

**Verdict:** correct

**5.** 他依赖缓存来提速。

**Verdict:** wrong
**Why:** Two errors: by + bare noun is transport/means only (by bus, by hand); a tool takes with the cache or by caching. And the cued fixed collocation rely on was not reached for - the distractor did its job.

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** 描述你自己的工作系统或一次排查（3-4 句），集齐 on / in / of / 固定搭配四张卡。
**中文：** 描述你自己的工作系统或一次排查（3-4 句），集齐 on / in / of / 固定搭配四张卡。
**You wrote:**
> Our business data displays on the index page of the platform. All data is saved in the database. What data displaying depends on the role of current user.
**Answer:**
> Our business data is displayed on the index page of the platform. All data is saved in the database. What data is displayed depends on the role of the current user.
**Verdict:** pattern achieved, sentence broken
**Why:** All four preposition cards collected correctly, depends on self-chosen. Outside the pattern: "what data displaying" needs the passive (what data is displayed - bare -ing, E005); "the role of current user" drops the determiner (E031).

## What It Showed

The spatial-metaphor procedure works: metaphor-guided items went 9/10 across
the drill, with the single systematic miss being platform-as-container (in
Rednote) - the Chinese default reading, now flagged everywhere it matters
(on Rednote / on the dashboard / on the platform). The residual gap is
exactly the predicted 10%: fixed collocations (rely on not reached for when
cued). And the pattern drill surfaced two non-pattern hits for the error
log: E005 (bare -ing) and E031 (empty determiner slot), both in the Round 3
free-production sentence - the pattern was produced, the clause around it
broke. Verdict: drilled; owned awaits spontaneous chat use.
