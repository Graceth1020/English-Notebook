# Pattern 25 - abstract prepositions (eighth pass: bare pictures, no sentence supplied in round 3)

Date: 2026-11-13 | Pattern: P014 | Rounds: 3 | Items: 10 | Correct: 9/10

## The Gap

Eighth drill on P014, requested directly after Day 55 despite the pattern already
being owned, so this pass was consolidation rather than repair. The design change
was to stop supplying two clauses to fold and instead hand over the spatial
picture alone: on = surface or line, in = container or boundary, of = part taken
out of a whole. One item - `in the loop` - was built specifically to separate
`in` from `on`, and that is the one that failed.

## The Rule

Ask where the thing physically sits:

- on = a surface or a line something rests on (responsibility, blame, focus, a list)
- in = inside a container or boundary (a module, a loop, a role, a state)
- of = a part taken out of a whole (two of the five, half of the team)

Fixed frames ignore the metaphor and must be memorised separately: `full of`,
`lead to` (against `result in`), `interested in`, `rely on`, `lie in`,
`down to`, `based on`, `focus on`.

## What Went Wrong

- `in` and `on` collapsed exactly where the item was designed to separate them:
  membership was expressed with the surface preposition (`on the loop`). The
  container picture loses to the surface picture when the noun - loop, circle,
  group - is abstract. Item 9 then put membership back on `in` correctly, so the
  model is unstable rather than absent: it holds when the container is physical
  (a module, a project) and fails when the boundary is social.

## Carry Into Chat

- Someone asks who is answerable for a stalled ticket: "that's on the PM" is the
  whole answer.
- A teammate needs to be added to a thread or a mailing loop: "I'll put you in the
  loop" is the natural sentence.
- Any share of a total - returns, budget, time - wants `of`: "a third of the
  returns", "half of the team".
