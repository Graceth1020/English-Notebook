# Pattern 14 - stacked time adverbials

Date: 2026-10-17 | Pattern: P013 | Rounds: 3 | Items: 11 | Correct: 7.5/11

## The Gap

From Drill 16 R3: "At midnight of last Saturday" - an of inserted between
date and clock time, plus the follow-up question on date + part-of-day +
clock combinations in general. Chinese time phrases chain with 的-like
freedom; English stacks them like an address, small unit first, each part
keeping exactly its own preposition.

## The Rule

at + clock (at 6:30, at noon); on + day-bound part (on Friday morning, on
the evening of the 15th); in + bare part of day (in the morning); dates
chain with zero glue (last Saturday, October 15th). last/this/next/yesterday
strip every preposition. The only legal "of": a specific date hanging off a
part of day - on the morning of October 15th. midnight/noon as clock points
are always bare (by midnight, never "by the midnight"). 12 am is midnight,
12 pm is noon - when in doubt say noon / midnight.

## Round 2 - embed in a sentence

**1.** 警报在周一晚上 9:40 响了。
**中文：** 警报在周一晚上 9:40 响了。
**You wrote:**
> The alarm went off at 9:40 pm on Monday.
**Answer:**
> The alarm went off at 9:40 pm on Monday.
**Verdict:** correct
**Why:** went off is the right verb for an alarm; the stack is clean.

---

**2.** 我昨天下午 3 点左右把修复发到了生产环境。
**中文：** 我昨天下午 3 点左右把修复发到了生产环境。
**You wrote:**
> I shipped a fix to production around 3 pm yesterday afternoon.
**Answer:**
> I shipped the fix to production around 3 yesterday afternoon.
**Verdict:** partial
**Why:** 3 pm and yesterday afternoon say the same thing twice - pm already means afternoon. Pick one: around 3 yesterday afternoon / around 3 pm yesterday.

---

**3.** 复盘会定在 10 月 15 号早上 10 点。
**中文：** 复盘会定在 10 月 15 号早上 10 点。
**You wrote:**
> The review meeting will be holding at 10:00 am on October 15th.
**Answer:**
> The postmortem is scheduled for 10 am on October 15th. / The postmortem will be held at 10 am on October 15th.
**Verdict:** wrong
**Why:** will be holding mixes active and passive - hold active needs a human subject (we'll hold it at 10); the meeting is held. Vocabulary: 复盘会 = postmortem, review meeting = 评审会.

---

**4.** 到上周六半夜为止，我们已经回滚了版本。
**中文：** 到上周六半夜为止，我们已经回滚了版本。
**You wrote:**
> We'd rolled back the release by the midnight last Saturday.
**Answer:**
> We'd rolled back the release by midnight last Saturday.
**Verdict:** partial
**Why:** The past-perfect trigger (by + past deadline -> 'd rolled back) was the item's real target and it landed. The error is the: midnight as a clock point is always bare - by midnight, at midnight, never "the midnight".

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** 用三个堆叠时间状语讲你昨天的一天（3-4 句）。
**中文：** 用三个堆叠时间状语讲你昨天的一天（3-4 句）。
**You wrote:**
> I woke up around 8 am yesterday. After preparing all things, I went out with my wife at 10:30 am. We arrived at Zhongshan city at 12 am. We'd returned home by 9 in the evening.
**Answer:**
> I woke up around 8 yesterday morning. After getting ready, I went out with my wife at 10:30. We arrived in Zhongshan at noon. We were back home by 9 in the evening.
**Verdict:** partial
**Why:** All three stacks structurally correct (prepositions, order, zero glue). One semantic trap: at 12 am means midnight - midday is 12 pm, safest is noon. Not counted: preparing all things -> getting ready; 'd returned by 9 -> were back by 9 (past perfect wants a reference event).

## What It Showed

Assembly was recognition-clean from the first pass (6/6); the errors all
lived around the stacks, not in them - verb voice, redundancy, the bare-midnight
rule, and the 12 am/pm meridiem flip. The pattern itself is drilled. The two
residual watches for chat: (a) 12 am vs 12 pm - teach the avoidance pair
noon/midnight; (b) by midnight / at noon staying bare under production.
