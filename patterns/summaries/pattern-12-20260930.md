# Pattern 12 - noun pile -> embedded question

Date: 2026-09-30 | Pattern: P011 | Rounds: 3 | Items: 10 | Correct: 7/10

## The Gap

Day 24 produced "the unit of the amount saved in database" - a word-by-word
rendering of the de-chain 数据库里存的金额的单位. The frame that should have
fired already exists: the same day, "I checked the logs to find out what the
problem was" came out unprompted and perfect. So the structure is not missing;
the de-chain simply beats it under pressure whenever the head noun is an
information point. Earlier instances of the same mechanism: Day 17 "the
amount showing logic on our pages", Day 10 "the send SMS logic", Day 05 "I'll
figure out the reason".

## The Rule

Catching yourself building "the + NOUN + of ..."? Check the noun:

```
- names a piece of information (unit / name / reason / time / place / way)
    -> drop the pile, use wh-word + clause:
       what unit it was stored in / why it failed / when it ships
- is a property that belongs to the thing (the end of the quarter)
    -> keep "the X of Y" - do not unwind
```

The unfold has four steps: info-noun -> wh-question -> real question in
English -> statement order inside the bigger sentence. Two how-shapes:
the way to do X -> how to do X; the way X works -> how X works.

## Round 1 - unwind the pile

**1.** We would have known the unit of the amount saved in the database.

**中文：**我们当时就会知道数据库里存的金额是什么单位。

**You wrote:**
> We would have known what unit of the amount stored in the database.

**Answer:**
> We would have known what unit the amount was stored in.

**Verdict:** wrong form

**Why:** The wh-word arrived but the of-pile survived inside the frame - "what unit of the amount". The of-phrase must unfold into a clause: subject + verb. Took three passes: first retry dropped "was" (passive participle with no be - third occurrence in two days, logged as E070), second gave the stranded "in" to "the database" instead of the unit. Final "stored in in the database" showed both locatives understood.

**2.** I don't know the way to refresh our API.

**中文：**我不知道怎么刷新我们的 API。

**You wrote:**
> I don't know the way to refresh our API.

**Answer:**
> I don't know how to refresh our API.

**Verdict:** not the pattern

**Why:** Grammatical - natives do say it - but the drill trains the how-frame: the way to do X -> how to do X. Landed clean on retry.

**3.** We need to figure out the reason for the failed alerts.

**中文：**我们得弄清楚告警为什么没发出去。

**You wrote:**
> We need to figure out the reason of the failed alerts.

**Answer:**
> We need to figure out why the alerts failed.

**Verdict:** not the pattern

**Why:** Both layers untouched - "reason" never became "why", and "the failed alerts" (a short de-pile of its own) never unfolded into a clause. Side note: reason takes "for", not "of". Landed clean on retry.

## Round 2 - unwind or keep?

**1.** I'll get through it by the end of the quarter.

**中文：**我会在季度结束前读完它。

**You wrote:**
> I'll get through it by the end of the quarter.

**Answer:**
> I'll get through it by the end of the quarter.

**Verdict:** ok

**Why:** Correctly refused - the discriminating item. "The end of the quarter" is a property, not an information point ("什么结束?" does not exist), so no unwind. No over-application.

## What Went Wrong

Round 1's three misses shared one mechanism, and it is the real finding of the
drill: **the wh-word is the easy half; unfolding the of-phrase into a clause
is the hard half.** Item 1 grabbed "what" on the first attempt, but the pile
survived inside the frame ("what unit of the amount stored"). Items 2 and 3
kept the pile entirely. After the four-step explanation, both landed clean on
the first retry - so the unfold is teachable in one pass, which matches the
track's founding bet: the bottleneck is exposure, not learning.

The side finding became an error row: the passive participle losing its "be"
("the amount stored" for "the amount was stored") appeared for the third time
in two days - Day 24 exchange 1, Round 1 first pass, Round 1 second pass. It
is now `E070`, and Round 2 item 3's "is sent" suggests the naming is already
working.
