## The Rule

on = surface/line/topic/contact, in = container, of = part-whole.
As verb particles: on = contact (put on the lid, put on shoes), off =
separation (shut off, take off, pry off). shut off / shut down both work
for 关机; only `off` for a tap or kettle. Fixed collocations: rely on,
depend on, lie in, thanks to, down to, based on, count on, focus on,
result in, full of. `result` takes IN (the outcome as container); `lead`
takes TO.
