## The Rule

on = surface/line/topic, in = container, of = part-whole. Platforms are
surfaces; the off-pair follows the verb (get OFF, see ON). A tool takes
`with`; a method takes `by + -ing`. Fixed frames ignore the metaphor:
`full of`, `lead to` (contrast `result IN`), `interested in`, `thanks to`,
`rely on`, `lie in`, `down to`, `based on`, `count on`, `focus on`.

A topic and a title cannot stack in one noun phrase: `a book on investing`
(topic) versus `a book called "How to Invest"` (title) - one label per noun
phrase, never both.
