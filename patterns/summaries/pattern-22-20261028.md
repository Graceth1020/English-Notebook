# Pattern 22 - stacked time adverbials (fourth pass, harder)

Date: 2026-10-28 | Pattern: P013 | Rounds: 3 | Items: 14 | Correct: 13/14

## The Gap

Fourth drill on P013, requested the day after Drill 21 (8/14), same
"more difficulty" brief. Drill 21''s retest list led: scheduled for + clock
with no `at`, by vs until for ongoing activities, and the pinned

New machinery: `a quarter to`, `this time + anchor`, `from...to`, and

## The Rule

at + clock, on + day/date, in + bare part of day. Small to large, zero glue.
Day+date is one unit under one `on`. Relative-day anchors (last/this/next/
yesterday/tomorrow/the day before or after/tonight/this time) strip all
prepositions. midnight/noon always bare and always said as words. The one
legal `of`: on the morning/evening of + a date - and the `on` is mandatory.
`scheduled for` swallows the clock `at`. Ongoing activities take `until`
or a from...to range, never `by`. Ranges: between X and Y / from X to Y.

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Announce a release to the team: scheduled for + clock + weekday + full date.

**You wrote:**

**Answer:**
> A release is scheduled for 10 pm on Saturday, October 31st.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** Third clean production of this frame. Not counted: 31st, not 31th.

---

**2.** Describe a crunch: from...to, until, not...until.

**中文：**描述一次赶工：从几点到几点（from...to），一直持续到几点（until），几点才结束（not...until）。

**You wrote:**

**Answer:**

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** All three target frames in one sentence; the ongoing activity
correctly paired with a range, not `by`. Not counted: bugs live `in` a
project (P014 container), not `of` it.

**3.** Tell a story and PIN the frame: it must contain `on the evening of` + a specific date.

**Answer:**
> The plane took off from Guangzhou to Xinjiang on the evening of October 28th.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** The pinned frame landed with `on` intact - Round 2 item 3''s miss
repaired in production. The route''s from...to is a correct bonus.
