## The Rule

on = surface/line/topic, in = container, of = part-whole. Texts are
containers (a typo IN the contract / the email subject). Topics are surfaces
(a talk ON time management). Platforms are surfaces; the off-pair follows
the verb (get OFF, see ON). Tool -> with. Method -> by + -ing. Deadline ->
by/before + time. Collocations: rely on, depend on, lie in, thanks to,
down to, based on.

## Round 2 - embed in a sentence

**1.** 邮件主题里有个错别字。

**中文：**邮件主题里有个错别字。

**You wrote:**
> There is a typo in the mail title.

**Answer:**
> There's a typo in the email subject line.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** `in` holds in a sentence. Not counted: `mail` is the postal system;
one message is `an email`, its 主题 the `subject line`.

---

**2.** 他做了一个关于时间管理的分享。

**中文：**他做了一个关于时间管理的分享。

**You wrote:**
> He made a share on time management.

**Answer:**
> He gave a talk on time management.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** `on` holds in a sentence. Not counted: 做分享 is `give a talk`,
not `make a share`.

---

**3.** 这次能赶上截止时间，多亏了他提前写了测试。

**中文：**这次能赶上截止时间，多亏了他提前写了测试。

**You wrote:**
> Thanks to the tests he wrote before, we can make it by the deadline.

**Answer:**
> Thanks to the tests he'd written ahead of time, we can make it by the deadline.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** `thanks to` + `by the deadline`, both targets hit. Not counted:
past perfect sits better for the earlier act (he''d written), and
sentence-final `before` -> `ahead of time`.

---

**4.** 问题出在数据库连接上。

**中文：**问题出在数据库连接上。

**You wrote:**
> The problem was down to the database connection.

**Answer:**
> The problem lay in the database connection.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** `down to` is an accepted collocation on record since Drill 15. The
cued target was `lie in`; his choice is idiomatic and passes.

---

**5.** 他用一把勺子把罐头撬开了。

**中文：**他用一把勺子把罐头撬开了。

**You wrote:**
> He pried the lid on with a spoon.

**Answer:**
> He pried the lid off with a spoon. / He pried the lid open with a spoon.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** Tool slot hit (`with a spoon`). Flagged, not counted: the particle
flips the meaning - 撬开 is `pry off / pry open` (off = separation);
`pried the lid on` means attached it. This is P014''s own on/off contrast
working as verb particles: on = contact, off = separation.

## Round 3 - free production

**1.** Describe a bug you found inside a piece of text - a log, an email, a message.

**中文：**说说你在某段文字（日志、邮件、消息）里发现过的一个错误。

**You wrote:**
> I found an exception which might occur in the task syncing transfer records.

**Answer:**
> I found an exception in the logs that might occur when the task syncs transfer records.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** Container use present (in the task...). Not counted: tense clash
(found + might occur) needs anchoring; an exception lives `in the logs`.

---

**2.** Recommend a talk or video about a topic - name the topic.

**中文：**推荐一个关于某个主题的演讲或视频，说出主题。

**You wrote:**
> I'd recommend you a TED video on how to learn multiple languages efficiently.

**Answer:**
> I'd recommend a TED video on how to learn multiple languages efficiently.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** `on` + topic in free production, one day after it was a miss. Not
counted: `recommend` takes no double object - recommend a video to you, or
drop `you`.

---

**3.** Describe a time you nearly missed a deadline and how you made it.

**中文：**说说一次你差点错过截止日期、最后赶上了的经历。

**You wrote:**
> We had to ship the feature of wealthy management before March 30th, but there were still a few bugs, so we all worked overtime until they were solved.

**Answer:**
> We had to ship the wealth-management feature by March 30th, but a few bugs were still open, so we all worked overtime until they were fixed.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** Deadline preposition correct - `before`, his own Round 1
alternative, in production. Not counted: 财富管理 is `wealth management`
(`wealthy` = 有钱的); `bugs are fixed`, problems are solved.

---

**4.** Describe a tool you use and the method behind it - what you use, how it works.

**中文：**说说你用的一个工具和它的方法：用什么，怎么起作用。

**You wrote:**
> I've developed a chrome plugin which helps me locate where the application deploys quickly. 

**Answer:**
> I've developed a Chrome plugin that helps me locate where the application is deployed - it works by reading the deploy config.

**Verdict:** correct

**Why:** No preposition error; the `with`/`by -ing` slots went unused behind
a relative clause - not an error. Not counted: `where the application
deploys` says the app deploys itself - the passive is meant (is deployed);
Chrome takes a capital.
