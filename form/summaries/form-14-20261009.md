## Round 1 - a general workout (scan in -> head out)

**Prompt:** Walk me through a workout at the gym - from scanning in at the entrance to heading out. What do you do, in what order? 6-8 sentences.

**中文：** 把去健身房锻炼的完整流程讲一遍，从扫码进门到离开。按顺序说做了什么。6-8 句。

**He wrote:**
> I scan my membership QR code to get into the gym. Then I step into the locker room to change into my workout clothes. After that, I warm up on the treadmill for ten minutes. Once it stops, I'm ready to do sets of actions. Before beginning, I wipe down the bench. Even though the exercise makes me tired, I'm really enjoy it. After doing all sets, I grab a shower in the bathroom and head out.

**tense:** ok
**article:** "Even though the exercise makes me tired" -> "the workout makes me tired / exercise makes me tired" (generic activity, no the; chat:E056 logged, hit 7); "all sets" -> "all my sets" (his own planned sets are specific)
**form:** "do sets of actions" -> "do sets of exercises" (calque: gym 动作 = exercises, not actions); "I'm really enjoy it" -> "I really enjoy it" (spurious be before a base verb)
**object:** ok
**prep:** ok

**Note:** Not counted. Word-list chunks produced correctly on first use: scan my
membership QR code, step into the locker room, change into my workout clothes,
warm up on the treadmill, wipe down the bench. Concession volunteered without
being asked (Even though). In the gym you grab a shower in the locker room or
hit the showers, not the bathroom. Connector quota met: one then, Once /

**prep:** ok

## What It Showed

**1. chat:E057 (comma splice) has moved into drills, with a visible shape.**

Two splices in two rounds, and they are the same sentence: an opening motion
verb and the next action joined by a comma (walk up ..., adjust ... / hop on
..., set ...). In chat the row sat at 2 hits since mid-September; now 3 and 4.
The pattern is not "he splices anywhere" - it fires specifically when two short
actions share one subject at the start of a sequence. The fix to teach is the
cheap one: and, not a period.

**2. Tense held across the whole drill for the first time in the action-chain format.**

Three rounds, three present frames, zero slips - after Drill 13's one
Once-it-reached. Drill 13's opener-determines-frame hypothesis gets weak
support: all three rounds here opened present and stayed present. The planned
past-vs-present same-scene experiment is still worth running.

**3. all sets -> all my sets is the current teaching frontier.**

Missed in Round 1, corrected, missed again in Round 2, absent in Round 3. Two
misses with the same mechanism: the narrator's own planned items feel definite
to him but he writes them bare. Adjacent to chat:E072 (first-mention the) but
not the same row - this is a possessive slot, not a first-mention. No new row
yet; one more instance opens one.

**4. The action-chunk bank (verb + equipment) is now the default word-list format.**

The learner's own complaint - "only squat rack, I don't know which verb goes
with it" - redesigned Round 2's bank, and Round 2's production immediately
justified it: five complex chunks produced correctly on first use. The bank
format is: chunk table with verb phrases, not noun lists.

**5. Self-check instructions work when the target is concrete.**

Round 3's spelling pass (assigned after Round 2's thighs/parallel) produced a
zero-spelling round. Same shape as Drill 10's article self-check: a concrete,
scannable target works; an abstract "check your grammar" does not.
