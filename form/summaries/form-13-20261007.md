## Why This Was Run

First new scene since bathroom and kitchen: the commute (leave home -> sit down
at desk), carried over from Drill 11's TODO list. Three variants of the same
route to stress the same chunk family under different weather and time
conditions: subway morning, ride-hailing after overtime, shared bike on a sunny

## Round 1 - subway to work (leave home -> badge in)

**Prompt:** Walk me through your morning commute - from the moment you step out of your apartment to the moment you sit down at your desk. 6-8 sentences.

**中文：** 把早上通勤的完整流程讲一遍，从出家门到坐到工位。6-8 句。

**He wrote:**
> After everything is up, I put my bag on and leave home. Before I step into the elevator, I double-check I have my keys and lock the door behind me. I take a subway to work so I head to the station and wait on the platform. When the train arrives, we squeeze onto it. I get off three stops later and take the lift upstairs to the office. Once the lift gets to the right floor, I get out it and badge in to my office.

**tense:** ok
**article:** "take a subway" -> "take the subway" (the subway as a city system is institutional, like the internet / the radio; a-vs-the choice, not an empty slot, so not chat:E031)
**form:** "we squeeze onto it" -> "I squeeze on (with everyone else)" (pronoun drift: the narrative was first person throughout; the crowd appeared without warning)
**object:** ok
**prep:** "After everything is up" -> "After I pack everything up" (particle: pack up, and "everything is up" means something else entirely); "I get out it" -> "I get out of it" (get out + thing takes of); "badge in to my office" -> "badge into my office" (badge in takes no place; with a place, into)

**Note:** Not counted. The connector quota was met in full: zero then, three
subordinators (Before / When / Once), one causal (so). Three bank chunks came
out correct on first use: lock the door behind me, double-check I have my keys,
wait on the platform. Elevator/lift mixed within one passage - not wrong,
flagged as a consistency habit.

## Round 2 - ride-hailing home after overtime (rain, 9:30 pm)

**Prompt:** It is 9:30 pm, you are getting off work, it is raining lightly, and you request a ride home on a ride-hailing app. Walk me through it, 6-8 sentences.

**中文：** 晚上九点半下班，下着小雨，你用打车软件叫车回家。把过程讲一遍，6-8 句。

**He wrote:**
> It's 9:30 pm now and time to get off. It's raining outside so I request a ride on Didi platform and an AION car driver accepts. After it comes to the pick-up spot in ten minutes, I hop in. As soon as I sit down, I fasten my seatbelt. Once it reached the entrance of my residential area, I get dropped off. I walk the last stretch to my home.

**tense:** "Once it reached" -> "Once it reaches" (the frame is present: request / hop / fasten / walk; one verb fell back into past, the reverse of Drill 12's clean run)
**article:** "on Didi platform" -> "on the Didi platform" (one specific platform; chat:E031 logged, hit 31)
**form:** "After it comes to the pick-up spot in ten minutes" -> "Ten minutes later it comes to the pick-up spot" ("in ten minutes" counts from now and cannot sit inside the after-clause; the time adverbial needs its own slot)
**object:** ok
**prep:** ok

**Note:** Not counted. "time to get off" accepted as the spoken ellipsis of get
off work - asked about live, ruled idiomatic. Three word-list chunks produced
correctly: hop in, fasten my seatbelt, get dropped off. "walk the last stretch
to my home" -> home works as an adverb: walk the last stretch home. Connector
quota met again: zero then, After / As soon as / Once, one causal (so).

## Round 3 - shared bike to work (sunny Friday)

**Prompt:** It is a sunny Friday morning and you decide to skip the subway and ride a shared bike to work. Walk me through it, 6-8 sentences. Try one concession (even though / although).

**中文：** 周五早上天气很好，你决定不坐地铁，骑共享单车上班。把过程讲一遍，6-8 句，试一处让步。

**He wrote:**
> The weather on Friday was awesome so I decided to ride to work. I found a shared bike outside my residential area, scanned the QR code and unlocked it. Then I hopped on to ride. I pedaled on the bike lane but suddenly a red light struck me. Even though it was hot, I had a good mood. After riding for ten minutes, I arrived at the company building and parked the bike in a designated spot. I ended the ride in the app and went to take the elevator.

**tense:** ok
**article:** ok
**form:** "I had a good mood" -> "I was in a good mood" (the collocation is be in a ... mood, not have); "a red light struck me" -> "I got stuck at a red light" (strike + person = hit physically, or an idea occurring; the word list's stuck at was replaced by a calque)
**object:** ok
**prep:** "pedaled on the bike lane" -> "in the bike lane" (a lane is a channel you move inside: in the fast lane)

**Note:** Not counted. A past frame chosen deliberately (one specific Friday)
and held for eleven straight verbs - the Round 2 slip did not repeat. All four
word-list chunks produced correctly: scanned the QR code, unlocked it, parked
the bike in a designated spot, ended the ride in the app. Concession challenge
completed (Even though it was hot). Connector quota met: exactly one then,
Even though / After as subordinators, one causal (so).

## What It Showed

**1. The tense frame is a choice, and he can hold it - when he chooses it.**

Round 2 broke the present frame once (Once it reached) inside a narrative that
was otherwise all present. Round 3 opened with a deliberate past frame (one
specific Friday: was / decided / found ...) and never left it. The mechanism
looks like frame-setting at the first sentence rather than verb-by-verb control:
when the opener is past, everything follows; when the opener is present, a
single completed-event verb can still drag one clause into past. Watch: whether
chat narratives show the same opener-determines-frame pattern.

**2. The prep axis is now two distinct problems.**

Particles (Round 1: pack up, get out of, badge into) still drop when he builds
a verb on the fly - the Drill 12 finding holds. But Round 3 added a plain
preposition miss (on the bike lane -> in), the first non-particle prep miss of
the action-chain format. The "particles only" narrowing from Drill 12 was
premature; both families stay on watch.

**3. Article misses split by subtype, and only one has a row.**

Round 1's a subway -> the subway is an a-vs-the choice (institutional system),
not an empty slot - kept out of chat:E031. Round 2's on Didi platform is the
empty-slot pattern and was logged to chat:E031 (hit 31). First-mention-the
(chat:E072) did not fire at all this drill: every object introduced with a or
the correctly - the residential area, a shared bike, the QR code, a red light,
a designated spot. One clean drill is not a trend, but the perspective check
may be transferring from dishes and towels to streets and bikes.

**4. Connector quota: three for three, and it now costs nothing.**

Zero/one/zero then across rounds, subordinators varied (Before/When/Once,
After/As soon as/Once, Even though/After), a causal so in every round, and the
Round 3 concession landed on the first ask. The quota has moved from constraint
to habit inside drills; the chat transfer is the open question.
