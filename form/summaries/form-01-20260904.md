# Form Drill 01 - 内容压力下的形态准确度

Date: 2026-09-04 | Axes: tense, article, form | Rounds: 3 | Clean: 1/3

## Why This Was Run

Form Drill 00 (articles, 12/12) proved that chat:E031 is not a knowledge gap - the
learner's article knowledge is perfect when attention is on the rule. The same
pattern was suspected for chat:E037 (tense in hypotheticals, 2 hits) and chat:E053
(verb form selection, 1 hit). All three needed to be tested under content load:
the learner answering a hard question while his attention is on meaning, with only
form being marked.

## Round 1 - Simple

**Prompt:** You just migrated your SMS provider. Explain why you switched - what was
wrong with the old one and what the new one solves. Three or four sentences.

**中文：**解释你为什么要换 SMS 供应商。旧的哪里不好，新的解决了什么。

**He wrote:**
> We talked it through last week. Since the new one is cheaper, we've got to swap the SMS provider out. The new one can reduce our cost.

**tense:** 1 miss — "Since the new one is cheaper" should be "was cheaper". The whole scene happened last week, so the second sentence drifts from past to present. This is the same second-sentence drift as chat:E037, in a simpler setting.

**article:** ok

**form:** ok

---

## Round 2 - Medium

**Prompt:** Your boss heard about your learning system idea and says: "I don't approve the budget unless you can prove only YOU can build it. If any average developer could do it, it's not worth funding. You have a minute."

**中文：**老板说除非你能证明只有你一个人能做出来，否则不给预算。

**He wrote:**
> My learning system is amazing because I have a special learning method. I know how to learn things effectively. The hard part is how to combine the efficiency with the system rather than coding.

**tense:** ok

**article:** 1 miss — "combine the efficiency" should be "combine that efficiency". `efficiency` is uncountable; `the` here is the coach:E008 direction (article added to an uncountable). The round-1 article drill had this exact type and he got it right there.

**form:** 1 miss — "how to combine ... rather than coding" should be "combining that efficiency ... rather than coding". `rather than` needs parallel forms on both sides. The left is an infinitive clause, the right is a gerund. Parallel means both infinitive or both gerund.

---

## Round 3 - Hard

**Prompt:** Boss: "`A special learning method` — everyone thinks their method is special. And you just said yourself the hard part isn't the code. That proves my point: if the hard part is the method, not the code, why do I need a product from you? I can just write your method up as a document and hand it to the team."

**中文：**老板说方法可以写成文档，不需要你来做产品。

**He wrote:**
> That's exactly why I have to do it on my own. The hard part is combining the experience with the system, and this probably makes misunderstanding.

**tense:** ok

**article:** ok

**form:** ok

**Note:** `makes misunderstanding` is a collocation error, not a form error — English says `causes misunderstanding` or `is where people get it wrong`. Not counted against the three axes per the rules, but worth recording because it is the same noun-for-verb habit as `chances` (Day 08) and `the change of that ticket` (Day 05). Mentioned once, not actionable here.

## What It Showed

**The degradation curve is inverted.** Conventional wisdom says hard content → more errors. The learner's actual curve is the opposite: tense slipped in the easy round, articles and form slipped in the medium round, and the hardest round was clean across all three axes.

This is the most useful finding of the session. It means the problem is not that
"content pressure causes errors". It is something more specific: **a drop in
perceived stakes causes errors.** The easy round was a technical decision he knew
cold, so he answered without checking — the same mechanism as the first sentence of
a hypothetical being correct and the second drifting. The medium round engaged his
attention but not enough to lock all three systems. The hard round had his full
attention, and everything held.

This aligns with the Day 10 finding that `C007` landed the first time it was not
being hunted. The common thread: **he performs at his best when he believes the
situation matters.**

## Carry Forward

1. The conventional drill (Form Drill 00, 12/12) will not be repeated. It was
   measuring recognition, which is already his strength.
2. The scheme-A format — hard content, only form marked, never announced — is the
   active instrument. Run it when he has the energy for a session that demands
   actual thinking, not when he wants a relaxed chat.
3. Axes are confirmed. The actual errors distributed across all three and none
   appeared in all three rounds, which means each axis is carrying weight.
4. The `makes misunderstanding` observation (collocation, not form) is worth
   feeding into the daily chat error log if it appears again, but the form-drill
   track should not expand its axes until the corpus demands it.
