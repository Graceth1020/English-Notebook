## Why This Was Run

Chat data, not theory: six preposition fixes across Day 35-36 (get TO the
office, integrate INTO, black box TO, spend morning ON, set aside FOR,
focus ON) - the strongest single-mechanism error signal since the article
streak. The learner himself asked about on/in/to on 10-12. All six real
errors were planted into Round 1 and Round 2 to test whether chat
corrections transfer to drill conditions.

## Round 1 - fill the gap (8 items: six real errors + two new)

**Prompt:** Fill in the preposition - 8 items, six built from your real chat errors of this week, two new.

**中文：** 填介词：8 道题，其中 6 道来自你本周 chat 里的真实错误，2 道新题。

**He wrote:**
> I got to the office at nine. We integrated a debug tool in to the H5 pages. The plugin is a black box to the frontend team. I spent the whole morning on the utility methods. I have to set aside more time for my coding. As an engineer, I focus on how to build a feature well. Whether I push back depends on my coding progress. He apologized to the delay and insisted on finishing it himself.

**tense:** n/a (prep-targeted round)
**article:** n/a (prep-targeted round)
**form:** "in to" as two words -> "into" (the preposition is one word; split only in phrasal-verb + to constructions, log in to)
**object:** n/a (prep-targeted round)
**prep:** "apologized to the delay" -> "apologized for the delay" (apologize TO a person, FOR a thing: apologize to the client for the delay)

**Note:** Score 7/8 plus the spelling note. The six planted real errors all
came back repaired - recognition and recall both online for this set.

## Round 3 - production (a request's journey to the SMS provider and back)

**Prompt:** Walk me through a request's journey to the SMS provider and back - filters, checks, and the response.

**中文：** 讲一遍一个请求到短信服务商再返回的完整旅程：过滤器、校验、响应。

**He wrote:**
> A request sent by frontend hits the proxy layer at first. Then it goes through all filters, from checking the legitimacy to its idempotency. After that, a real service handles it, parsing the parameter and call the third-party SMS provider. Once it receives the result, the handler checks if the request successes and records the request. Finally, the response is sent back to the frontend.

**tense:** ok
**article:** ok
**form:** "parsing the parameter and call" -> "and calls" (parallel verbs, one subject); "checks if the request successes" -> "succeeded" (success noun, succeed verb; past frame); "at first" -> "first" (at first = initially-with-a-twist; a sequence step takes plain first); "from checking the legitimacy to its idempotency" -> "from legitimacy checks to idempotency checks" (from-A-to-B parallelism)
**object:** ok
**prep:** ok

**Note:** The target axis held under free production: through all filters, from legitimacy checks to idempotency checks, sent back to the frontend. The non-prep errors are
the next theme: word-family pairs (success/succeed, legitimacy/legitimate)
and parallel structure.
