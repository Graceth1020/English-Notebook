# Form Drill 17 - prepositions: the chat-error special

Date: 2026-10-20 | Axes: prep (target), form (incidental) | Rounds: 3 | Clean: R1 7/8, R2 6/6, R3 prep-axis clean

## Why This Was Run

Chat data, not theory: six preposition fixes across Day 35-36 (get TO the
office, integrate INTO, black box TO, spend morning ON, set aside FOR,
focus ON) - the strongest single-mechanism error signal since the article
streak. The learner himself asked about on/in/to on 10-12. All six real
errors were planted into Round 1 and Round 2 to test whether chat
corrections transfer to drill conditions.

## Round 1 - fill the gap (8 items, his six real errors + two new)

Score: 7/8 + one spelling note.
- Items 1-7 all correct: to / into / to / on / for / on / on - every real
  chat error repaired under drill conditions.
- Item 2 spelling: "in to" as two words - into is one word (the preposition);
  split only in phrasal-verb + to constructions (log in to).
- Item 8 miss: "apologized TO the delay" -> FOR the delay. Rule taught:
  apologize TO a person, FOR a thing (apologize to the client for the delay).

## Round 2 - spot and fix (his own broken sentences replayed, two planted clean)

Score: 6/6, no overcorrection.
- All four broken sentences fixed (got to / into / focus on / set aside for).
- Both planted clean sentences identified as clean (depends on how big a deal;
  that's down to).
- Bonus 1: he upgraded "It's essential to integrate..." to "Integrating...
  is a must" - C113 produced one day after banking. Drill production, so NOT
  credited per the chat-only credit rule; flagged for chat watch.
- Bonus 2: item 4 he also fixed reasonable -> reasonably unprompted - two
  Day-36 errors closed in one line.

## Round 3 - production (a request's journey to the SMS provider and back)

Prep axis: CLEAN. through all filters, from legitimacy checks to
idempotency checks (structure correct, parallelism off), sent back to the
frontend. The target axis held under free production.

Non-prep errors (the next theme):

## Conclusions
