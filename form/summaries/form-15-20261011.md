## Round 1 - scrambled eggs and toast

**Prompt:** Walk me through making a quick breakfast - scrambled eggs and toast. From taking out the eggs to sitting down to eat. 6-8 sentences.

**中文：** 讲一遍做快手早餐（炒蛋+吐司）的流程，从拿鸡蛋到坐下开吃。6-8 句。

**He wrote:**
> In mornings, I always eat eggs with bread. First, I crack two eggs into a bowl and beat them with chopsticks. Then I heat up a pan over medium heat for two minutes before I pour eggs and scramble them with a spatula. When the eggs are about to be cooked, I season them with a pinch of salt. After I plate the eggs, I pop two slices of bread into the toaster. Once the toaster pops up, I sandwich eggs between the two slices.

**tense:** ok
**article:** "In mornings" -> "In the morning" (fixed time phrase, the cannot drop); "pour eggs" / "sandwich eggs" -> "pour the eggs in" / "sandwich the eggs" (second and third mentions still bare: first mention two eggs was correct, the perspective check failed on re-mention; chat:E031 logged, hits now 34 - the same empty-slot mechanism, plural instance)
**form:** "When the eggs are about to be cooked" -> "When the eggs are almost done" (eggs are not about to undergo cooking; be about to + passive is stilted); "Once the toaster pops up" -> "Once the toast pops up" (the appliance does not pop; the toast does - noun landing on the wrong entity)
**object:** ok
**prep:** "before I pour eggs" -> "pour the eggs in / into the pan" (pour needs a destination; bare pour + object dangles)

**Note:** Not counted. The comma-splice watch paid off immediately: crack ...
and beat ... used and correctly, zero splices in the round. season them with a
pinch of salt and sandwich as a verb were both asked about mid-round and then
produced correctly - live teaching converting to production within the same
passage. Connector quota partially missed: one then, four subordinators, but
no purpose/causal link anywhere.

## Round 2 - a bowl of noodles

**Prompt:** Walk me through cooking a bowl of noodles, 6-8 sentences. The purpose/causal link is mandatory this round.

**中文：** 讲一遍煮一碗面的流程，6-8 句。这轮必须带一个目的或因果衔接。

**He wrote:**
> This is how I cook noodles. First, I fill a pot with water and bring it to a boil. When it comes to a boil, I add a handful of noodles and stir so they don't stick. Then I toss in greens, and keep stirring. After cooking for a while, I turn the heat down to a simmer. I taste the broth and drain the noodles in a colander when it's well cooked. I ladle them in a bowl and drizzle some sesame oil over the top.

**tense:** ok
**article:** ok
**form:** "when it's well cooked" -> "when they're done" (pronoun number: it points at plural noodles; and done is the native word for food being ready, well cooked belongs on a steak)
**object:** ok
**prep:** "I ladle them in a bowl" -> "ladle them into a bowl" (the Drill 12 movement-into rule; the bank said into, remembered one notch loose - same loosening as Drill 14's start at a brisk walk)

**Note:** Not counted. Article fully clean and the Round 1 problem fixed: a pot
on first mention, the noodles on re-mention, the broth / the heat / the top
all correct. Connector quota fully met after last round's gap: so they don't
stick as the purpose link. Self-correction observed between drafts: the
aborted first version had cook noodle and a handful noodles, both fixed to
plurals before submitting. toss in greens, and keep stirring has and (no
splice) but the comma is superfluous with a shared subject.

## Round 3 - garlic broccoli (full prep, spelling self-check assigned)

**Prompt:** Walk me through stir-frying garlic broccoli - from rinsing to plating, 6-8 sentences. Do your own spelling and into/in pass before submitting.

**中文：** 讲一遍炒蒜蓉西兰花的流程，从冲洗到装盘，6-8 句。交稿前自己过一遍拼写和 into/in。

**He wrote:**
> I rinse the broccoli under running water and cut it into florets. Then I mince two cloves of garlic. After I heat the wok until it starts to smoke, I add oil and swirl it around. I toss in the garlic and stir-fry the florets over high heat. I splash in a litter water so the florets can be soft and cover the wok for a minute. Finally, I season the dish with salt and plate it.

**tense:** ok
**article:** ok
**form:** "a litter water" -> "a little water" (real-word spelling slip, third instance of the family: tide/tidy, things/thighs, litter/little; the assigned self-check missed it); "so the florets can be soft" -> "so they soften" (calque: 能变软; a purpose clause wants a verb, not can + be + adjective)
**object:** ok
**prep:** ok

**Note:** Not counted. Six determiners in a row all correct (the broccoli /
the wok / the garlic / the florets / the dish), the cleanest article round in
the action-chain format. The complex bank chunk heat the wok until it starts
to smoke produced verbatim-correct. into/in: zero errors on the assigned
re-check - the self-check works on the targeted axis and still misses
untargeted ones (spelling). Comma splice: second consecutive clean round.

## What It Showed

**1. tense has now been clean for five consecutive rounds** (Drill 14 x3,
Drill 15 x2 -> x3). The opener-determines-frame experiment is losing its
justification for present frames; if run at all, run it for past frames only.

**2. The article problem has migrated along the mention timeline.**

Drill 12-13: first-mention the (chat:E072). This drill, Round 1: first mention
correct, re-mention bare (pour eggs, sandwich eggs). Round 2-3: the whole
timeline clean. The perspective check is extending from "introduce with a" to
"re-mention with the" - one logged chat:E031 hit for the empty-slot mechanism,
and then self-repair within the same drill. This is the fastest a drill-born
article problem has moved toward resolved.

**3. Bank chunks are being remembered one notch loose.**

start a brisk walk (Drill 14 R3), ladle them in a bowl (this drill R2) - both
were given in the bank with the right preposition/particle and came back
slightly altered. The bank prevents word-finding failures but does not
guarantee verbatim uptake; the fixes stick one drill later. Expect a one-drill
lag on bank items and do not re-teach inside the same drill.

**4. Self-checks hit exactly what they target and nothing else.**

Round 3 was assigned spelling + into/in. into/in: clean. Spelling: one
real-word slip survived (litter). Same profile as Drill 10 (article check
caught a/an, missed bare nouns) and Drill 14 R3 (spelling check, clean). The
self-check tool works but is single-axis; multi-target checklists may exceed
what one pass can carry.

**5. Comma splice: two consecutive clean rounds** after Drill 14's pair. The
and-over-comma fix appears to have taken. chat:E057 stays open for chat
production, but drill-side watch can drop to passive.

**6. Vocabulary questions mid-drill are a feature, not noise.** Five this
drill (over/by, season, sandwich, fill up, wok/pot/pan), each answered in two
minutes, and both teachable items asked about (season with, sandwich) appeared
correctly in the very next paragraph. The action-chain format's real density
may be these micro-lessons, with the passage as the retrieval practice.
