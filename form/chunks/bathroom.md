# 场景词块：浴室（bathroom）

动作链 drill 的场景专用固定搭配。与 chat/chunks.md 分工：那边只收**通用**
表达，这里收**具体场景**的动作词块。每轮 drill 后由助手系统梳理补全，
不限于纠错项——产出正确的、词库里该会没会的，一并记录。

## 刷牙（Drill 12 R1, 2026-10-03）

**原句：**
> First, I switch on the faucet to wash my cup and toothbrush. Then I fill up the cup, squeeze some toothpaste onto the toothbrush. I drink some water and spit it to wash my mouth first, and begin to brush my tooth up and down. After brushing, I put all things back and tide up my appearance looking in the mirror.

**优化版（背诵用）：**
> First, I turn on the tap and rinse my cup and toothbrush. Then I fill up the cup and squeeze some toothpaste onto the toothbrush. I take a sip of water and spit it out to rinse my mouth first, then begin to brush my teeth up and down. After brushing, I put everything back and tidy myself up - a quick once-over in the mirror.

| Chunk | 中文 / 用法 | 状态 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| turn on the tap | 开水龙头（faucet 是美式；动词都是 turn on，switch on 是电器） | 纠错：switch on the faucet |
| rinse the cup | 涮杯子（杯/碗/嘴都用 rinse，不用 wash） | 纠错：wash my cup |
| wet the toothbrush | 把牙刷沾湿 | 词库补充 |
| fill up the cup | 接满一杯水 | ✅ 产出正确 |
| squeeze some toothpaste onto the toothbrush | 挤牙膏 | ✅ 产出正确 |
| brush my teeth up and down | 上下刷（teeth 永远复数） | 纠错：brush my tooth |
| take a sip of water and spit it out | 喝一口水再吐掉（out 不能省，spit it 是另一个意思） | 纠错：spit it |
| rinse my mouth (out) | 漱口 | 纠错：wash my mouth |
| rinse the toothbrush (off) | 冲洗牙刷 | 词库补充 |
| put everything back | 把东西放回去 | 纠错：put all things back |
| tidy myself up | 收拾一下自己 | 纠错：tide up（tide 是潮汐） |
| give myself a once-over in the mirror | 照镜子快速检查一遍仪容 | 词库补充（本轮笔记里的自然说法） |

## 洗澡（Drill 12 R2, 2026-10-03）

**原句：**
> I step in the bathroom to take a shower. I turn on the shower head to test the water. After setting the water temperature to the right temperature, I wet my hair and put some conditioner onto it. Then I grab the shower head to rinse off my hair. Then I use body wash to lather up. At last, I dry myself with a towel.

**优化版（背诵用）：**
> I step into the bathroom to take a shower. I turn on the shower and test the water until it feels right. Then I wet my hair and shampoo it, and rinse the shampoo out. After that I put some conditioner onto my hair, let it sit for a minute, and rinse it out too. Then I lather up with body wash. At last, I rinse off and dry myself with a towel.

| Chunk | 中文 / 用法 | 状态 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| step into the bathroom | 走进浴室（进入某个空间用 into；step in = 插手/顺路进来） | 纠错：step in |
| turn on the shower | 开花洒（说 the shower，不说 shower head） | 纠错：turn on the shower head |
| test the water | 试水温 | ✅ 产出正确 |
| until it feels right | 调到合适水温（避免 temperature 重复两遍） | 纠错：to the right temperature |
| wet my hair | 把头发打湿 | ✅ 产出正确 |
| shampoo my hair | 用洗发水洗头（shampoo 直接作动词） | 词库补充（本轮漏了这步） |
| put some conditioner onto it | 抹上护发素（it 指头发） | ✅ 产出正确（但顺序应在 shampoo 后） |
| rinse off my hair | 冲掉头发上的泡沫（off 不能省） | ✅ 产出正确 |
| lather up with body wash | 用沐浴露打泡 | ✅ 产出正确（他用 use body wash to lather up） |
| scrub with a loofah | 用浴花搓洗 | 词库补充 |
| towel off / dry myself with a towel | 用毛巾擦干（dry + 反身代词是对的） | ✅ 产出正确 |

## 背诵方式

按动作链整条背，不按单条背：从 turn on the tap 到 give myself a once-over，
能在脑子里过一遍完整流程并把每个词块放对位置，才算拿下这个场景。
