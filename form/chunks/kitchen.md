# 场景词块：厨房（kitchen）

动作链 drill 的场景专用固定搭配，梳理自 Drill 11（2026-10-01）三个轮次。

## 烧水装瓶（D11 R1）

**原句：**
> When I wake up in the morning, I've got to drink a bottle of water. I plug in the kettle and switch it on to boil water. When it comes to boil, I fill up my water bottle and drink it. Then I continue to pour water into my bottle. After filling it up again, I screw the lid on tight. Finally, I grab it on my way out the door.

**优化版（背诵用）：**
> When I wake up in the morning, I've got to drink a bottle of water. I plug in the kettle and switch it on to boil water. When it comes to a boil, I fill up my water bottle and drink some. Then I pour more water into the bottle. After filling it up again, I screw the lid on tight. Finally, I grab it on my way out the door.

| Chunk | 中文 / 用法 |
| --- | --- |
| plug in the kettle | 插上电热水壶 |
| switch it on | 按下开关（电器用 switch on，对照水龙头的 turn on） |
| when it comes to a boil | 水开时（固定搭配，a 不能丢） |
| fill up my water bottle | 灌满水杯 |
| pour water into the bottle | 往瓶里倒水 |
| screw the lid on (tight) | 拧紧盖子 |
| grab it on my way out the door | 出门顺手拿上 |

## 加热剩饭（D11 R2）

**原句：**
> I take the leftover fried rice out of the fridge and put it in the bowl. Then I cover it with a lid and microwave it for three minutes. I take it out halfway and give it a stir. After heating it up, I should be careful while the bowl is hot. I put the bowl on the table and let it sit for a minute. Then I dig in. What a delicious dish!

**优化版（背诵用）：**
> I take the leftover fried rice out of the fridge and put it in a bowl. Then I cover it with a lid and microwave it for three minutes. I take it out halfway and give it a stir. The bowl is hot, so I'm careful taking it out. I put it on the table and let it sit for a minute. Then I dig in.

| Chunk | 中文 / 用法 |
| --- | --- |
| take ... out of the fridge | 从冰箱拿出 |
| cover it with a lid | 盖上盖子 |
| microwave it for three minutes | 微波加热三分钟 |
| take it out halfway and give it a stir | 中途取出搅一搅 |
| let it sit for a minute | 放一会儿（静置） |
| dig in | 开吃 |

## 泡茶（D11 R3）

**原句：**
> Whenever I want to have a cup of tea, I've got to put the kettle on. After heating it up, I warm the cup with hot water. Then I put a tea bag in the cup, pour the water over it and let it steep for three minutes. Later I add a splash of milk and give it a stir. A delicious cup of tea with milk is done.

**优化版（背诵用）：**
> Whenever I want a cup of tea, I put the kettle on. While the water heats up, I warm a cup with hot water. Then I put a tea bag in the cup, pour the water over it, and let it steep for three minutes. I take the bag out, add a splash of milk, and give it a stir. A nice cup of tea with milk, ready to drink.

| Chunk | 中文 / 用法 |
| --- | --- |
| put the kettle on | 烧上水 |
| warm the cup with hot water | 温杯 |
| put a tea bag in the cup | 放茶包 |
| pour the water over it | 把水浇在茶包上 |
| let it steep for three minutes | 泡三分钟 |
| add a splash of milk | 加一点点奶 |
| give it a stir | 搅一搅 |

## 洗碗（D12 R3, 2026-10-03）

**原句：**
> I fill up the sink and put plates, bowls and chopsticks in it with dish soap. Then I wet the towel to clean the table. After that, I begin to wash things in the sink with a sponge. Once I clean them all up, I put them on the drying rack. At last, it's the greasy pan's turn. I squirt some dish soap to it, scrub it and rinse off the soap.

**优化版（背诵用）：**
> I fill up the sink with hot soapy water and put the plates, bowls, and chopsticks in to soak. While they soak, I wet a cloth and wipe down the table. After that, I wash everything in the sink with a sponge. Once they're all clean, I put them on the drying rack. Last comes the greasy pan - I squirt some dish soap onto it, scrub it, and rinse off the soap.

| while they soak | 趁泡着的工夫（衔接词） | 词库补充 |
