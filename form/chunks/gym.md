# 场景词块：健身房（gym）
动作链 drill 的场景专用固定搭配。与 chat/chunks.md 分工：那边只放**通用**表达，这里收**具体场景**的动作词块。每轮 drill 后由助手系统梳理补全，不限于纠错项——产出正确的、词库里该会没会的，一并记录。

## 锻炼流程（Drill 14 R1, 2026-10-09）
**原句：**
> I scan my membership QR code to get into the gym. Then I step into the locker room to change into my workout clothes. After that, I warm up on the treadmill for ten minutes. Once it stops, I'm ready to do sets of actions. Before beginning, I wipe down the bench. Even though the exercise makes me tired, I'm really enjoy it. After doing all sets, I grab a shower in the bathroom and head out.

**优化版（背诵用）：**
> I scan my membership QR code to get into the gym. Then I step into the locker room to change into my workout clothes and stash my bag in a locker. After that, I warm up on the treadmill for ten minutes. Once it stops, I'm ready to do my sets. Before beginning, I wipe down the bench. Even though the workout makes me tired, I really enjoy it. After doing all my sets, I grab a shower in the locker room and head out.

| Chunk | 中文/用法 | 状态 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| scan my membership QR code | 扫会员码进场 | ✅产出正确 |
| step into the locker room | 走进更衣室 | ✅产出正确 |
| change into my workout clothes | 换运动服 | ✅产出正确 |
| stash my bag in a locker | 把包存进柜子 | 词库补充 |
| warm up on the treadmill | 在跑步机上热身 | ✅产出正确 |
| do sets of exercises / do my sets | 做组（健身"动作"是 exercise，不是 action） | 纠错：do sets of actions |
| wipe down the bench | 擦卧推凳 | ✅产出正确 |
| the workout makes me tired | 锻炼让我累（泛指活动不加 the） | 纠错：the exercise |
| I really enjoy it | 我很享受（be 和实义动词只留一个） | 纠错：I'm really enjoy it |
| all my sets | 我那几组（自己计划的组要加 my/the） | 纠错：all sets |
| grab a shower in the locker room | 在更衣室冲澡（健身房不说 bathroom） | 纠错：in the bathroom |
| head out | 离开 | ✅产出正确 |

## 练腿日（Drill 14 R2, 2026-10-09）
**原句：**
> I walk up to the squat rack, adjust the safety arms to the right height. After I load two plates onto each side of the bar, I duck under the bar and rest it on my shoulders. As soon as I brace my core, I squat down until my things are parrel to the floor. Then I drive back up through my heels and rack the bar. When I catch my breath between sets, I drink water. After I finish all sets, I refill my water bottle and foam-roll my legs to relax.

**优化版（背诵用）：**
> I walk up to the squat rack and adjust the safety arms to the right height. After I load two plates onto each side of the bar, I duck under the bar and rest it on my shoulders. As soon as I brace my core, I squat down until my thighs are parallel to the floor. Then I drive back up through my heels and rack the bar. When I catch my breath between sets, I drink water. After I finish all my sets, I refill my water bottle and foam-roll my legs to relax.

| adjust the safety arms to the right height | 把保护杠调到合适高度 | ✅产出正确 |
| load two plates onto each side of the bar | 给杠铃两边各装两片 | ✅产出正确 |

## 有氧日（Drill 14 R3, 2026-10-09）
**原句：**
> It's a cardio day, so I decide to exercise on the treadmill. I hop on the treadmill, set the speed to 6 to start a brisk walk to warm up. After warming up for five minutes, I bump the speed up to a jog. My heart rate climbs slowly during the jog. Once I've run for 3.6 km, I hit the stop button to bring the speed back down. I cool down at a slow walk for five minutes and step off. It's essential to stretch my calves after exercising. While I am stretching, I check my workout stats on my watch.

**优化版（背诵用）：**
> It's a cardio day, so I decide to exercise on the treadmill. I hop on the treadmill and set the speed to 6 to start at a brisk walk to warm up. After warming up for five minutes, I bump the speed up to a jog. My heart rate climbs slowly during the jog. Once I've run for 3.6 km, I hit the stop button to bring the speed back down. I cool down at a slow walk for five minutes and step off. It's essential to stretch my calves after exercising. While I am stretching, I check my workout stats on my watch.

| bump the speed up to a jog | 把速度加到慢跑 | ✅产出正确 |
