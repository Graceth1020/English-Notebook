# 场景词块：通勤（commute）
动作链 drill 的场景专用固定搭配。与 chat/chunks.md 分工：那边只放**通用**表达，这里收**具体场景**的动作词块。每轮 drill 后由助手系统梳理补全，不限于纠错项——产出正确的、词库里该会没会的，一并记录。

## 地铁上班（Drill 13 R1, 2026-10-07）
**原句：**
> After everything is up, I put my bag on and leave home. Before I step into the elevator, I double-check I have my keys and lock the door behind me. I take a subway to work so I head to the station and wait on the platform. When the train arrives, we squeeze onto it. I get off three stops later and take the lift upstairs to the office. Once the lift gets to the right floor, I get out it and badge in to my office.

**优化版（背诵用）：**
> After I pack everything up, I put my bag on and leave home. Before I step into the elevator, I double-check I have my keys and lock the door behind me. I take the subway to work, so I head to the station and wait on the platform. When the train arrives, I squeeze on with everyone else. I get off three stops later and take the lift upstairs to the office. Once the lift gets to the right floor, I get out of it and badge into my office.

| Chunk | 中文/用法 | 状态 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pack everything up | 收拾好所有东西（up 不能丢；everything is up 是另一个意思） | 纠错：After everything is up |
| put my bag on | 背上包 | ✅产出正确 |
| lock the door behind me | 随手锁门 | ✅产出正确 |
| double-check I have my keys | 确认钥匙带了 | ✅产出正确 |
| step into the elevator | 走进电梯 | ✅产出正确 |
| take the subway | 坐地铁（交通系统用 the，同 the internet） | 纠错：take a subway |
| wait on the platform | 在站台等车 | ✅产出正确 |
| squeeze on (with everyone else) | 挤上车（主语别从 I 漂成 we） | 纠错：we squeeze onto it |
| get off three stops later | 三站后下车 | ✅产出正确 |
| get out of the lift | 下电梯（get out + 物 必须带 of） | 纠错：get out it |
| badge into my office | 刷工牌进办公室（badge in 不带地点；带地点用 into） | 纠错：badge in to my office |

## 打车回家（Drill 13 R2, 2026-10-07）
**原句：**
> It's 9:30 pm now and time to get off. It's raining outside so I request a ride on Didi platform and an AION car driver accepts. After it comes to the pick-up spot in ten minutes, I hop in. As soon as I sit down, I fasten my seatbelt. Once it reached the entrance of my residential area, I get dropped off. I walk the last stretch to my home.

**优化版（背诵用）：**
> It's 9:30 pm now and time to get off. It's raining outside, so I request a ride on the Didi platform and an AION driver accepts. Ten minutes later it comes to the pick-up spot, and I hop in. As soon as I sit down, I fasten my seatbelt. Once it reaches the entrance of my residential area, I get dropped off. I walk the last stretch home.

| Chunk | 中文/用法 | 状态 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| time to get off | 到点下班（get off work 的口语省略） | ✅产出正确 |
| request a ride on the Didi platform | 在滴滴上叫车（特指平台用 the） | 纠错：on Didi platform |
| the driver accepts | 司机接单 | ✅产出正确 |
| ten minutes later | 十分钟后（时间状语单独放，别塞进从句） | 纠错：comes ... in ten minutes |
| pull up to the pick-up spot | 开到上车点 | 词库补充 |
| hop in | 上车 | ✅产出正确 |
| fasten my seatbelt | 系安全带 | ✅产出正确 |
| once it reaches ... | 一到……（现在时框架别掉回 reached） | 纠错：Once it reached |
| get dropped off | 被送到、下车 | ✅产出正确 |
| walk the last stretch home | 走完最后一段路回家（home 前不加 to） | ✅产出正确 |

## 骑车上班（Drill 13 R3, 2026-10-07）
**原句：**
> The weather on Friday was awesome so I decided to ride to work. I found a shared bike outside my residential area, scanned the QR code and unlocked it. Then I hopped on to ride. I pedaled on the bike lane but suddenly a red light struck me. Even though it was hot, I had a good mood. After riding for ten minutes, I arrived at the company building and parked the bike in a designated spot. I ended the ride in the app and went to take the elevator.

**优化版（背诵用）：**
> The weather on Friday was awesome, so I decided to ride to work. I found a shared bike outside my residential area, scanned the QR code and unlocked it. Then I hopped on to ride. I pedaled in the bike lane, but suddenly I got stuck at a red light. Even though it was hot, I was in a good mood. After riding for ten minutes, I arrived at the company building and parked the bike at a designated spot. I ended the ride in the app and went to take the elevator.

| Chunk | 中文/用法 | 状态 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| scan the QR code | 扫二维码 | ✅产出正确 |
| unlock a shared bike | 解锁共享单车 | ✅产出正确 |
| hop on | 骑上去 | ✅产出正确 |
| pedal in the bike lane | 在自行车道骑（lane 是通道，用 in） | 纠错：on the bike lane |
| get stuck at a red light | 被红灯拦住（strike + 人 = 砸中/想法浮现） | 纠错：a red light struck me |
| be in a good mood | 心情好（mood 用 be in，不用 have） | 纠错：had a good mood |
| arrive at the company building | 到公司楼 | ✅产出正确 |
| park it at a designated spot | 停到指定停车点 | ✅产出正确 |
| end the ride in the app | 在 app 里结束行程 | ✅产出正确 |
