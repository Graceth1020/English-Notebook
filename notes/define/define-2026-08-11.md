## 2026-08-11 17:09
- **Word**: confession
- **Definition**: A statement admitting something you did wrong, kept secret, or feel strongly about; an admission of guilt, a hidden fact, or a personal feeling. Also the act of telling sins to a priest in the religious sense.
- **Usage**: Noun, countable in most uses (a confession). Common patterns: make a confession, confession of (confession of guilt, confession of love), full confession. Related verb: confess (confess to doing something). In the day's context, the old-timer's confession is his admission that he built the robot to win back his son. Casual phrase: confession time.
- **Examples**:
  - The old-timer made a confession about the robot he built to win back his son.
  - He signed a full confession at the police station.
  - She made a confession of love to her best friend.
  - Confession is good for the soul.
- **Synonyms**: admission, acknowledgment, declaration, disclosure, revelation
- **Antonyms**: denial, concealment, cover-up, secrecy
