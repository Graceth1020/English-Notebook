- **Word**: ditch
- **Sentence**: We ditched him over nothing.
- **Definition**: To leave someone or something behind on purpose; to abandon or get rid of them. In the day's sentence, ditching someone means dumping them or leaving them out of a plan or group.
- **Usage**: Transitive verb: ditch someone/something. Common collocations: ditch a plan, ditch a car, ditch work/school, ditch someone (leave them behind or end a relationship). Casual and slangy, so it fits spoken English. In 'ditched him over nothing,' over nothing means for no real reason.
- **Examples**:
  - We ditched him over nothing.
  - They ditched the old plan and started over.
  - She ditched me at the mall.
  - I ditched class to go to the lake.
- **Synonyms**: abandon, dump, leave behind, desert, drop, get rid of
- **Antonyms**: keep, retain, hold on to, stick with, take along
