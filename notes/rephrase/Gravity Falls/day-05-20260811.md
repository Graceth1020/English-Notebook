- **Word**: contrived
- **Sentence**: In retrospect, it seems a bit contrived.
- **Definition**: Artificial, forced, or unnatural; deliberately planned or arranged rather than happening naturally. Used of stories, excuses, or situations that feel fake or too obviously constructed.
- **Usage**: Adjective, usually before a noun (a contrived plot, a contrived excuse) or after a linking verb (it seems/looks/feels contrived). Often softened with a bit, somewhat, or rather. Common in criticism of writing and films. Related words: contrive (verb), contrivance (noun).
- **Examples**:
  - In retrospect, it seems a bit contrived.
  - The movie's ending felt contrived.
  - His excuse sounded contrived to the teacher.
  - The plot was too contrived to be believable.
- **Synonyms**: artificial, forced, unnatural, labored, staged, manufactured, fabricated
- **Antonyms**: natural, spontaneous, genuine, organic, effortless, unforced
