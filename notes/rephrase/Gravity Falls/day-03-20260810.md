- **Word**: plan
- **Sentence**: So what's the plan? Throw more cameras overboard or what?
- **Definition**: A set of actions you intend to take to achieve something; as a verb, to think about and arrange what you will do.
- **Usage**: Noun: make a plan, what's the plan?, a plan for/to do something, go according to plan, plan B (a backup plan). Verb: plan to do something, plan on doing something, plan for something. 'What's the plan?' is a casual question asking what someone intends to do next.
- **Examples**:
  - So what's the plan? Throw more cameras overboard or what?
  - We need a solid plan before we start.
  - They plan to leave early tomorrow.
- **Synonyms**: scheme, strategy, arrangement, intention; verb: intend, arrange, organize
- **Antonyms**: improvisation, spontaneity, guesswork
