- **Definition**: To form a picture or idea in your mind of something that is not real, present, or certain; also, to suppose or guess that something might be the case.
- **Usage**: Often used as an imperative to invite the listener to picture a scenario, usually followed by a clause (imagine what would happen / imagine (that) ...) or by a gerund (imagine doing something). It also appears as 'I can't imagine ...' to say something seems impossible, and as '(just) imagine!' for a casual exclamation. In the day's sentence it sets up a conditional: what would happen if we got that picture.
- **Examples**:
  - Not worth it? Guys, imagine what would happen if we got that picture!
  - Imagine you're lying on a quiet beach with nobody around.
  - I can't imagine living without music.
  - Just imagine how surprised she'll be!
- **Synonyms**: picture, envision, visualize, conceive, suppose, fancy
- **Antonyms**: know, doubt, disregard; as a noun: reality, fact
