- **Definition**: As an adjective, powerful, strong, or great in size or force. In informal American English, also an intensifier meaning 'very' (a mighty big deal). The day's sentence uses the fixed phrase 'high and mighty,' meaning arrogant or self-important.

- **Antonyms**: weak, powerless, feeble, insignificant; high and mighty: humble, modest, down-to-earth

- **Usage**: Adjective, usually before a noun: a legendary hero, a legendary band, legendary status. Can describe both real fame (a legendary coach) and storybook fame (a legendary monster). - **Examples**:
  - Well, we spent all day trying to find a legendary dinosaur.
  - The band's concert was a legendary night.
  - He is a legendary figure in the history of the town.
