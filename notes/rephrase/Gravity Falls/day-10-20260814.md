- **Definition**: Consent or authorization to do something; the official or informal approval that makes an action allowed.

Note the British spelling: permission; common phrase: ask for permission.
- **Examples**:

  - You need permission from your parents to go on the trip.
  - The guard asked to see my permission.
  - She gave me permission to use her laptop.
- **Synonyms**: consent, approval, authorization, leave, clearance, go-ahead
- **Antonyms**: denial, refusal, prohibition, ban, veto
