- **Word**: terrible
- **Sentence**: Ya see, it's funny because marriage is terrible.
- **Definition**: Very bad, extremely unpleasant, or of poor quality; also, in a stronger sense, causing great fear or alarm (inspiring terror).
- **Usage**: Adjective: a terrible mistake, terrible weather, terrible news. After a linking verb: it's terrible, I feel terrible. With at: terrible at something (very unskilled). Adverb: terribly (it went terribly wrong). The word is informal but neutral in tone, though 'feel terrible' usually means guilty or very unwell rather than bad quality.
- **Examples**:
  - Ya see, it's funny because marriage is terrible.
  - The weather was terrible all weekend.
  - I made a terrible mistake.
  - I feel terrible about what happened.
  - He's terrible at keeping secrets.
- **Synonyms**: awful, horrible, dreadful, appalling, atrocious, very bad
- **Antonyms**: wonderful, great, fantastic, excellent, superb
