## Why Segment 7 First

It is the densest source of the exact move the learner most needs: Lisa Su is
asked a question built on a premise she does not fully accept ("AMD has doubled
down on inference rather than training"), and instead of saying "no, that's
wrong," she reframes - "maybe let me take a step back," "I think that is a
little bit more in terms of the tactics of a given year," "I would step back
from that and say." She also handles a direct competitor comparison
("AMD is the Android to Nvidia's Apple") by conceding first ("Nvidia is a great
company") then differentiating ("I think our view is a little bit different").

That is precisely the register a developer needs in design reviews and
interviews, and it maps onto the learner's top logged weakness: function drift
when disagreeing.
