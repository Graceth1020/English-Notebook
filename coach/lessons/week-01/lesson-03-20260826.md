# Lesson 03 - Reporting bad news without apologizing for it

**Goal:** when something has gone wrong, open with the impact, state facts without hedging,
close on a next update.

<details><summary>中文说明</summary>

今天只练一件事：出了问题时怎么汇报。中文习惯是先道歉、先解释原因、最后才说影响；
英语职场相反：**影响在前，原因在后，道歉可以不说**。过度道歉不会让你显得负责，
只会把对话的重心从“怎么办”拉到“谁的错”。

</details>

```text
state the hard fact  ->  scope it  ->  say what you're doing  ->  no apology anywhere
```

Absent throughout: *sorry*, *unfortunately*, *my fault*. Difficulty is a property of the
problem, not the speaker.

## Dialogue


### Turn 1 - The fix did not ship  [channel: spoken]

**Coach (manager, standup):** "Did the batching fix go out yesterday like we said?"

**You:** "The code change had been completed. But during the testing, I found that some
users got double notified. As the core logic was complicated, I spent a few hours to make
it clear. In order not to impact on the production environment, I have to test carefully
so it really takes some time."

**Fix:**
- No verdict, no date  -> open "No - I held it", close "QA sign-off by Friday"  [wrong: he asked *did it ship*]
- "some users got double notified"  ->  "it would have double-notified some orders"  [wrong: caught in testing]
- "had been completed"  ->  "is done"  [wrong: past perfect needs an earlier past point; this state is current]

**Model (minimal repair):** "No - I held it. The code change is done, but during testing I
found that some orders would have been double-notified. The core logic is complicated, so I
spent a few hours making it clear. I didn't want to break prod, so I need to test carefully -
another day or two. QA sign-off by Friday."

**Reorder:** verdict first, date last - what he asked for.

**Native:** bookish - "As the core logic was complicated", "In order not to impact on the
production environment" read written. In speech: "the core logic is messy", "I didn't want to
break prod". `impact on` as a verb is wrong.

### Turn 2 - Your mitigation hit a peer team  [channel: Slack]

**Coach (backend lead, shared channel):** "@you our dashboard's shown zero expiry
notifications since Monday. Did something change on your side? Support tickets are piling
up."

**You:** "Right. I found some expiry orders double-notified users. After analyzing the
log, I found the batching fix has affected the prod. Since I has turned off the send
expiry notification cron job, all expiry notification will not be sent to user. I'll make
this fix by Friday. When this version releases, all notifications that has been backlogged
for two days will send by a task."

**Fix:**
- "I has turned off" / "all expiry notification" / "will send by a task"  ->  "I turned off" / "no expiry notifications" / "will be sent"  [wrong: agreement, plural, passive missing `be`]
- Impact buried under two sentences of diagnosis  ->  open "Yes - that's us, and it's my doing."  [wrong: Slack needs the cause first]
- "has affected the prod"  ->  "would have double-sent, so I killed the cron"  [wrong: vague, and `prod` takes no article]

**Model (minimal repair):** "Right. I found some expiry orders would have double-notified
users. After analyzing the log, I found the batching fix would have affected prod. Since I
turned off the expiry-notification cron, no expiry notifications have been sent. Fix ships
Friday, and the notifications backlogged for two days will be sent by a task."

**Reorder:** in Slack the cause goes first - "Yes, that's us, and it's my doing."

**Native:** slightly off - "I'll make this fix by Friday", "when this version releases" ->
"fix ships Friday", "when it goes out".

### Turn 3 - Owning the communication gap  [channel: spoken]

**Coach (manager, 1:1):** "Support escalated this - two days of no expiry notifications is
a customer-facing outage, and I first heard the cron was off in the backend channel
yesterday. Why didn't you flag it Monday?"

**You:** "That's on me. It's my fault not to flag it. I got to check what the real problem
was and forgot to notify the business department. I'll never make such a mistake whenever
the same situation happens."

**Fix:**
- "It's my fault not to flag it"  ->  "I should have flagged it Monday"  [wrong: `it's my fault to do` is not an English pattern]
- "I'll never make such a mistake"  ->  "From now on, if I turn anything off in prod, I'll post it the same day"  [awkward: a promise cannot be verified; a rule can]
- "I got to check"  ->  "I went straight to checking"  [wrong: `got to` = *had the chance to*]

**Model (rebuilt: the closing promise had to become a mechanism):** "That's on me - I should have flagged it Monday. I went straight
to checking what the real problem was and forgot to notify the business department. From now
on, if I turn anything off in prod, I'll post it the same day."

**Native:** clean opening - "That's on me" is exactly right, and unprompted. `the business
department` is correct but formal for a 1:1; say `support`.

## Consolidation

No `I'm sorry` in three turns, and Turn 3 opened with `That's on me` unprompted - the apology
habit is gone. What replaced it is **over-explaining**: Turn 1 gave four sentences of process
without answering "did it ship"; Turn 2 buried the cause under the investigation order.

The thread: **fact first, story second, commitment closes.** The ordering slips, not the
vocabulary.

## Recap

### 1. Expressions You Learned

- **`That's on me.`** - ownership without apology
- **`I should have flagged it Monday.`** - own a past omission
- **`I held it.`** / **`fix ships Friday`** - release status
- **`it would have double-notified orders`** - caught in testing
- **`From now on, if X, I'll Y.`** - a verifiable mechanism, not a promise
- **`I didn't want to break prod`** (not *impact on the production environment*)

### 2. Your Key Errors

| You said | Say instead |
| --- | --- |
| "It's my fault not to flag it" | "I should have flagged it Monday" |
| "I'll never make such a mistake" | "From now on, if I turn anything off in prod, I'll post it the same day" |
| "all expiry notification will not be sent" | "no expiry notifications have been sent" |

<details><summary>中文说明</summary>

**三轮下来最大的进步**：一次 `I'm sorry` 都没有，而且主动用出了 `That's on me`。
但道歉被**过度解释**取代了：第一轮四句话讲过程却没回答“上线了没”，第二轮把原因埋在排查过程后面。
你想证明自己尽责，但对方得自己掘事实。

**两个关键句式**：
- `should have + 过去分词` = 过去该做而没做，是承认失误的标准说法。
  注意：`It's my fault to do sth` 不是英语句型。
- 改进要给**机制**，不给**保证**。`I'll never make such a mistake` 无法验证；
  `From now on, if I turn anything off in prod, I'll post it the same day` 可以验证。
  英语职场重规则，不重态度。

</details>
