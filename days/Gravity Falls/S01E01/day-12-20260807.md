# Day 12 - Tourist Trapped: humor and emphasis in longer sentences

Source: Gravity Falls - S01xE01 - Tourist Trapped | Difficulty: Tier 3 (hard block, [H])

Context: Dipper's narration opens the episode with comedy, irony, and self-aware humor.

1. The girl about to puke is my sister Mabel. `humor: about to + verb` (line 2)
2. You may be wondering what we're doing in a golf cart fleeing from a creature of unimaginable horror. `ironic narration: you may be wondering` (line 3)
3. Rest assured, there's a perfectly logical explanation. `reassurance + irony: rest assured` (line 4)
4. The real mystery was why anyone came. `irony: the real mystery was` (line 12)
5. Ladies and gentlemen, behold, the sascrotch! `formal address + comedy` (line 13)
6. I know you're going through your whole "boy crazy" phase, but I think you're kind of overdoing it with the "crazy" part. `concession + pun: boy crazy / crazy part` (line 20)
7. I wouldn't be surprised if the man of my dreams walked through that door right now. `hypothetical: I wouldn't be surprised if` (line 31)
8. Just today my mosquito bites spelled out "beware." `deadpan humor: spelled out` (line 43)
9. That says "bewarb." `comedy: correction` (line 44)
10. It's hard to believe it's been six years since I began studying the strange and wondrous secrets of Gravity Falls, Oregon. `emphasis: it's hard to believe + since` (line 48)

Today's focus: the hard block opens with humor and emphasis in longer
sentences. Keep the irony and the joke when you rephrase; these lines need
more than a word swap. Pass any sentence that feels too easy, and leave a
blank if you don't know.
