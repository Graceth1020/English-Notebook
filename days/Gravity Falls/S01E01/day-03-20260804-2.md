# Day 3 - Tourist Trapped: reactions, praise, and clarification

Source: Gravity Falls - S01xE01 - Tourist Trapped | Difficulty: Tier 1 (everyday patterns) | Round 2 of 2026-08-04

Context: The Mystery Shack crew reacts to the attic, Mabel flirts, and Dipper sets the record straight.

1. I like turtles too. What is happening here? `reaction + confusion question` (line 28)
2. It's getting closer! `warning / situation update` (line 1)
3. This attic is amazing! `praise: noun + is + adjective` (line 8)
4. It's amazing! `praise` (line 57)
5. That would be so awesome! `imagined excitement` (line 101)
6. I'm joking! `clarification` (line 26)
7. I'm not joking! `clarification + seriousness` (line 105)
8. That is not funny, Dipper. `criticism / reaction` (line 104)
9. You scallywag! `playful insult` (line 79)
10. Say hello to my new boyfriend! `command + introduction` (line 68)

Today's focus: keep the exact meaning when you rephrase. Notice the
difference between praising ("amazing"), warning ("getting closer"), and
clarifying ("I'm joking" vs "I'm not joking"); each has its own natural
alternatives. Pass any sentence that feels too easy.
