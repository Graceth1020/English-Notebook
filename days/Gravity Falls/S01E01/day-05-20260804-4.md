# Day 5 - Tourist Trapped: two patterns in one sentence

Source: Gravity Falls - S01xE01 - Tourist Trapped | Difficulty: Tier 1+ (harder end of easy) | Round 4 of 2026-08-04

Context: The Mystery Shack crew hedges, promises, threatens, and explains in slightly longer sentences.

1. I guess I can be kind of paranoid sometimes. `hedging: kind of + sometimes` (line 120)
2. There's something I should tell you. `there's something I should...` (line 131)
3. We understand. We'll never forget you, Mabel. `promise: we'll never...` (line 146)
4. Give her back right now, or else! `threat: or else` (line 161)
5. That's for lying to me! `that's for + gerund (payback)` (line 193)
6. I'm trying to tell you that Norman is not what he seems! `appearance vs reality` (line 99)
7. What about me, huh? Why can't you trust me? `what about me? + why can't you...` (line 110)
8. Try not to hit any pedestrians. `try not to + verb` (line 126)
9. Check out this giant smooch mark he gave me! `check out + relative clause` (line 98)
10. I can't even remember what I was talkin' about. `can't even remember + what clause` (line 180)

Today's focus: slightly longer sentences that combine two patterns. Keep the
exact meaning and the casual tone, and rephrase the whole sentence, not just
half of it. Pass any sentence that feels too easy.
