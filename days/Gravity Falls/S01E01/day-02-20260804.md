# Day 2 - Tourist Trapped: short reactions and quick responses

Source: Gravity Falls - S01xE01 - Tourist Trapped | Difficulty: Tier 1 (everyday patterns)

Context: The Mystery Shack crew warns, reacts, and responds in quick everyday bursts.

7. It worked! `result reaction` (line 216)
8. What is all this? `confusion question` (line 49)
9. Look at this! `attention-getting` (line 74)
10. Oh! I love jam! `enthusiasm + love + noun` (line 73)

Today's focus: short everyday reactions, warnings, and quick responses.
These lines each have a couple of common alternatives; rephrase them the way
you would actually say them, and keep them short. Pass any sentence that
feels too easy.
