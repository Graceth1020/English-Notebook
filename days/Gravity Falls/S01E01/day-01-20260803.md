# Day 1 - Tourist Trapped: everyday phrases that carry attitude

Source: Gravity Falls - S01xE01 - Tourist Trapped | Difficulty: Tier 1 (everyday patterns)

Context: The Mystery Shack crew reacts, urges, and reassures each other through the episode's opening scenes.

1. Oh, my gosh! `reaction phrase` (line 14)
2. Come on, Dipper! `urging someone` (line 21)
3. Let's go somewhere private. `let's + suggestion` (line 56)
4. Let me get this straight. `discourse opener` (line 63)
5. I know, and I'm comfortable with that. `concession + stance` (line 37)
6. Something weird is going on in this town. `something ... is going on` (line 42)
7. Nobody ever believes anything I say. `complaint pattern` (line 47)
8. What's the catch? `everyday idiom question` (line 208)
9. I'm sorry for ignoring your advice. `apology + gerund` (line 198)
10. There's gotta be a way out of this! `there's gotta be` (line 183)

Today's focus: short everyday phrases that carry attitude, especially
reactions, urgings, hedged opinions, and reassurances. Keep the meaning and
the casual tone when you rephrase; write each version the way you would
actually say it.
