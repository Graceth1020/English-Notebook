# Day 7 - Tourist Trapped: grammar around the phrase

Source: Gravity Falls - S01xE01 - Tourist Trapped | Difficulty: Tier 2 (medium block, [M]) | Round 2 of 2026-08-05

Context: Dipper watches, worries, and listens; Soos and Stan hand out wisdom and deals.

1. I'm being watched. `passive continuous: being + past participle` (line 41)
2. I'm always noticing weird stuff in this town. `always + present continuous (habitual complaint)` (line 86)
3. Sheesh! You two get hit by a bus or somethin'? `passive: get + past participle` (line 205)
4. Our parents decided we could use some fresh air. `could use + reported idea` (line 6)
5. Yeah, but do you need to flirt with every guy you meet? `do you need to + relative clause` (line 24)
6. I'd fire all of you if I could. `conditional: I'd ... if I could` (line 39)
7. My wisdom is both a blessing and a curse. `idiom: a blessing and a curse` (line 91)
8. Just keep an open mind. Be cool. `idiom: keep an open mind` (line 134)
9. It's the end of the line, kids. `idiom: end of the line` (line 181)
10. Wouldn't you rather have a doll or something? `wouldn't you rather + have` (line 211)

Today's focus: the grammar around the phrase. Watch the passive forms
("being watched", "get hit"), the always + -ing habit, conditionals, and
keep the idioms intact. Pass any sentence that feels too easy.
