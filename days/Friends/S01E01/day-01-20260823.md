Today's focus: keep the fixed phrases intact. This round is built on frames
that break if you swap a single word: "there has to be", "all of a sudden",
"it turns out", "go through", "freak out", "it hit me", "kind of hoping",
"for the best". Change the wording around the frame, not the frame itself,
and keep the spoken register (contractions, tags like "okay?" and "you
know?"). Pass any sentence that feels too easy, and leave a blank if you
don't know how to answer.
