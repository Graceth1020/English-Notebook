## "Reached but broken" - recognition transfers, assembly does not (2026-09-20)

The learner reaches for chunk/pattern language under production pressure and the
fixed form comes out damaged, even though the chunk was clearly recognised and
aimed for. This is the forward signal - recognition is transferring - and it is
scored `missed` (never credited), because the damaged form is not ownership.

Instances so far:

- C001 `couldn't put it down` -> "I'll not to put it down" (Day 22, 6th try; modal + to garbling)
- C017 `all sorts` -> "post sorts" (Day 20; came out whole Day 22)
- C039 `be up to the job` -> "be up to my job" (Day 20)
- C040 `out of my depth` -> "people out of our depth" (Day 20; idiom applied to others)
- C056 `that's a symptom, not the reason` - missed 4x before landing whole (Day 21)
- C091 `high-spec` -> "has a high-sec" (Day 44, 1st try; spec->sec + adjective used as a noun)
- C045 `get in the way of` -> "gets ON the way" (Day 44; preposition broke, pending row so no off-scene confirm either)
- C124 hint contamination (Day 44): named in Day 43's Two To Use, produced correctly next day - credit REVERTED. Two To Use naming = one-day hint; a chunk named there can only be credited from a later session.
- C126 `get involved in (something)` -> "which different teams are get involved in" (Day 46; auxiliary stack broke: are + get cannot coexist; reached-but-broken, logged missed).
- C089 `matter to (someone)` -> "it really matters for the developers" (Day 46; preposition slot broke, to -> for; reached-but-broken, logged missed).
- C127 `write things down` -> "write any plan down" (Day 47; fixed part broke: things -> any plan, plus my own question had hinted the slot; reached-but-broken, logged missed).
- C086 `a one-day (X) pass` / C116 `so far` (Day 47) - both were named in Codex's own opening/question, then produced back next turn; same-session echo, no credit (same rule as the C124 case below).

Pattern in the damage: the fixed parts break - the particle, the article, the
modal+to glue - never the content words. The learner knows the chunk; the
assembly slot is what gives way under load.

What to do: never penalise the reach. Log `missed` when the form breaks, credit
when it comes out whole (C017 and C056 both landed whole the next time, so the
reach is a real step). When one mechanism recurs, promote it to a pattern drill.
Current candidate: **P009 modal + bare infinitive** - "I won't put it down",
never "I won't to put it down" (one instance, Day 22). Not worth a drill until
it recurs.


## "Form intact, wrong slot" - the chunk reaches the wrong concept (2026-10-31)

Distinct from fixed-part damage above. The fixed phrase comes out whole, but
its meaning slot does not match what the sentence talks about:

- C125 `high-priority / low-priority` -> "a high-priority drink" (Day 45). The
  adjective ranks tasks and projects, not beverages; he meant the default/go-to
  drink. Form was perfect, concept wrong - logged `missed`, same as a broken
  form, because the learner has not owned the collocation's boundary yet.

Watch for the next occurrence: if a chunk keeps landing on a nearby but
wrong concept, the gap is semantic scope, not assembly - a contrast card
(high-priority task vs default drink) may be more useful than another seed.

## Prompt comprehension: abstract options stall, concrete A/B does not (2026-10-31)

Day 45 exchange 4 opened with "settle everything in one go / sort it out piece
by piece" - he could not parse it after one plain-language unpacking, and asked
for a second round. A concrete two-option example (one message with the whole
plan vs several messages, one decision each) unblocked it immediately. When a
planning or process question stalls twice, skip the abstract vocabulary and
write the two situations as labeled mini-examples before asking again. Those
clarification rounds are `[meta]`, never counted as exchanges.


## "I + verb" every time - the fronted subject never comes out (2026-09-20)

Every sentence expressing a thing or a wish opens with "I + verb"; the object or
idea is never put in subject position. Not grammar - nothing ever errors - it is
the fronted-subject frame (passive, gerund subject, "X is the plan") that cannot
be recalled. Same mechanism as C031, one level up: C031 is a noun slot, this is
a whole-sentence frame.

- "I follow the rule strictly." -> "The rule gets followed strictly."
- "I want to read books before bed." -> "Books before bed are still the plan."

Handling (in chat, C031-style mechanism test): when a non-I subject appears
naturally ("The rule holds" / "Doing X helps" / "A day of work wears me out"),
mark `[good]` - any correct subject word counts, not a fixed phrase. When a run
of sentences all opens with I, say one gentle line - never a formal correction.

Logged as **P010** (P009 stays reserved for modal + bare infinitive). Status
`owned` as of Day 47: "Whether I skip a day depends on..." (exchange 3) was the
unprompted non-I-subject production; `pattern-used` logged it (used=9/2). Keep
praising the frame on sight, but no further logging needed.

## The chunk that would not land - it was the opening, not the chunk (2026-11-06)

C091 "high-spec" arrived twice unprompted on Day 49 after three seeded sessions
produced nothing, and the difference was the kind of question. Every failed
attempt asked him to evaluate equipment ("is your setup good enough"). Today it
came out of ownership and contrast ("I refuse to give up my laptop - it's still
high-spec"), then again for a tablet he had bought. Once a chunk has failed three
seeded sessions, stop re-asking the same shape: change the frame from evaluation
to possession or comparison before writing it off.

- C119 "try out" was echoed, not recalled: the question itself said "did you try
  anything else out first" and the answer reused the phrase. Naming the chunk in
  the Codex turn turns it into a prompt, so it can never be credited. Ask about
  the decision ("how did you end up on it") and leave the phrase out.
- Two questions fused into one turn failed again (the site-sections question
  needed "all-rounders" and "built for one specific job" unpacked together). One
  idea per question is the ceiling, and the flag came as "your question is hard to
  understand" rather than a request for meaning.
- Conditional drift is now a repeating habit, not a one-off: "I'd try out
  something else ... I will get something". Same family as Day 48's "if the
  company pushed ... I have to switch up". Watch for it as a drift across a whole
  answer, not a single clause.
- chat:E046 (a purpose-built tool called "professional") was reachable all session
  and never fired - he answered "all kinds of laptops" both times. It needs a
  question he can only answer by naming a category of machine, not by listing
  variety.

you can switch off totally", and C020 "talk it through" (home `work`) from

- C086 "a one-day (X) pass" does not belong in a trip chat. Ten days of seeding
