## Today's Practice

The hard block got harder: deadpan jokes, long causal sentences, and two
structured patterns ("couldn't help but", "the more... the more..."). This
round had the most major fixes in a while, six, and two blanks that were
taught below. The reasons cluster into two habits: leaving the hard
sentences blank (2 and 3) and small mechanical slips that derail an
otherwise good idea ("profund", "will gonna", "gonme queue"). The one
brilliant line was 8, where the clever logic came through naturally.

## Corrections Worth Remembering

### 4. "Profund" is not a word

Original: "He's really deep."
My version: "He's truly profund."
Model: "We met at the cemetery. He's really deep."

"Profund" does not exist in English; the adjective is "profound". And the
original's joke is a pun on "deep" (a grave is deep, and he is supposedly
intellectually deep), which a synonym kills. Keep "deep".

### 9. No "will gonna"

Original: "The more you struggle, the more awkward this is gonna be for everybody."
My version: "The more everybody will gonna be awkward."
Model: "The more you struggle, the more awkward this will get for everyone."

"Will gonna" doubles the future; you say either "will be" or "is gonna be",
never both. The "the more... the more..." structure also needs both halves
to mirror each other.

### 5. "Couldn't help + -ing"

Original: "I couldn't help but overhear you..."
My version: "I can't stop myself overhear you..."
Model: "I couldn't help overhearing you..."

After "stop myself" you need "from + -ing" ("stop myself from overhearing").
The fixed pattern in the original is "couldn't help + -ing", which needs no
"from".

## Don't Know - Taught Today

### 2. The long causal sentence

Meaning: 那句"森林里有怪物"的说法是像我们这样的人编出来，好把东西卖给那种游客的。
Model: "Look, kid, the whole 'monsters in the forest' thing is just a local
story made up by guys like me to sell junk to guys like that."
Note: the chain is "X is Y made up by A to sell to B"; keep the cause and
effect in one sentence.

### 3. "After a certain point" + "like + clause"

Meaning: 写到某个地方之后，书页突然就断了，好像写书的人凭空消失了。
Model: "At some point the pages just stop, as if the person writing it
vanished mysteriously."
Note: "like + clause" here means "as if"; use "as if" in writing for the
same effect.

## Patterns I Can Use Today

- "Everyone, come on in to the kingdom of savings!"
- "At some point the pages just stop, as if..."
- "He's really deep."
- "I couldn't help overhearing you..."
- "The more you struggle, the more awkward this will get."

## How This Builds Speaking

Hard-block rounds put structure and humor under pressure, and the pressure
showed: two sentences were left blank and two were garbled at the word
level. The useful lesson is that hard sentences need you to hold the whole
shape, not just the idea. Follow up by saying the taught models for 2 and 3
aloud, and drill "the more... the more..." with real pairs, "the more I
practice, the better I get".
