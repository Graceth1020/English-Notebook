## Corrections Worth Remembering

### 3. "Gonna be" carries the future in the past

Original: "It looked like it was gonna be the same boring routine all summer..."
My version: "It seemed that it was the same routine during this summer..."
Model: "It looked like the same boring routine all summer, until one fateful day..."

"Was gonna be" is a past view of the future: at that time, it looked as if
the summer would be boring. Changing it to "was" makes it past and finished,
and "seemed that it was" adds words without adding meaning.

### 4. Dating is a verb, romance is the noun

Original: "It's my big chance to have an epic summer romance."
My version: "It's my excellent opportunity to have an epic summer dating."
Model: "It's my big chance to have an epic summer romance."

"Dating" is an activity ("we are dating"), not a thing you have. The noun
you "have" here is a "romance" (or a "love affair"). Also "excellent
opportunity" is more formal than the playful "big chance".

### 8. "Nerd thing" is teasing, not criticism

Original: "Whatcha readin'? Some nerd thing?"
My version: "Are you reading something boring?"
Model: "What are you reading? Some nerd thing?"

The question is affectionate teasing about the book being nerdy, not a claim
that it is boring. Keep the playful tone.

## Don't Know - Taught Today

### 9. "Catch up on"

Meaning: 补看/补做之前没看没做的事。In the scene, Dipper is pretending he is
reading magazines to cover up the journal.
Model: "I was just getting caught up on, uh..."
Note: the passive form "getting caught up on" is also common: "I need to
catch up on my emails."

## Patterns I Can Use Today

- "We could use some fresh air." (could use = would benefit from)
- "It's a blessing and a curse."
- "Until one fateful day..."
- "Help me put up this sign!"
- "I feel like something is watching me."
- "I need to catch up on my emails."

## How This Builds Speaking

The revisit strategy works: both Day 7 trouble spots are now natural and
automatic. That is how vocabulary becomes speech, meeting a pattern twice
with review in between. Follow up by saying aloud "we could use..." and "a
blessing and a curse", then use "catch up on" in a real sentence today.
