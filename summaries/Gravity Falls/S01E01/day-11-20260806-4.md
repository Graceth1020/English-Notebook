# Day 11 - Tourist Trapped: last of the medium block

Date: 2026-08-06 (round 4) | Source: Gravity Falls S01E01 Tourist Trapped | Level: Tier 2 ([M])

## Today's Practice

The medium block ends with phrasal verbs, casual offers, and reported
reactions. This round was the strongest of the block: six of ten sentences
were fully natural or nearly so, and the two major fixes both came from
losing a function of the phrase, "in case" became "prevents you from" and
"and it's like" became a literal question. That is the fixed-expression
pattern one more time, but overall accuracy is clearly trending up.

## Corrections Worth Remembering

### 4. "In case" is preparation, not prevention

Original: "And this is in case you see a pinata."
My version: "And this prevents you from seeing a pinata."
Model: "And this is in case you see a pinata."

"In case" means the thing is there to be ready if something happens. It
does not stop the event, it prepares for it. "Prevents" reverses the whole
meaning of Soos handing Dipper a weapon.

### 6. "And it's like" reports a reaction

Original: "And it's like, 'what?!'"
My version: "And what is it like?"
Model: "And I was like, 'what?!'"

"And it's like" (or "and I was like") is how speakers quote a reaction in
storytelling. Reading it as a literal question changes the grammar and the
function. The casual "be like" pattern is worth owning whole.

### 8. Watch means be careful with

Original: "There we go, watch those fingers, Mike."
My version: "There we go, see those fingers, Mike."
Model: "There we go, watch those fingers, Mike."

"Watch" in this instruction means "be careful with your fingers"; "see"
means look at them. Context decides the verb, and here the warning needs
"watch".

## Patterns I Can Use Today

- "Don't you wanna show me?"
- "Don't wait for me!"
- "I'm going to be... and he's going to be..."
- "This is in case..."
- "And I was like, 'what?!'"
- "I'll get you back for this!"

## How This Builds Speaking

This round closes the medium block on a high note: six of ten natural. The
two misses were both function loss, turning a fixed phrase into its literal
meaning. That is the final lesson of the medium block, fixed phrases carry
their own meaning and can't be read word by word. Follow up by saying "and I
was like, 'what?!'" and "this is in case..." aloud.
