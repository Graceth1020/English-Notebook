## Corrections Worth Remembering

### 9. The lesson is a general truth, not a future plan

Original: "But when you battle a hundred gnomes side-by-side with someone, you realize that they've probably always got your back."
My version: "But you'll realize that they've probably supported you when you fight..."
Model: "But when you battle a hundred gnomes side-by-side with someone, you realize they've probably always had your back."

The original states a general truth: whenever this happens, you realize it.
"You'll realize" turns it into something that will happen later, which
changes the lesson. Also "got your back" is the fixed phrase; "supported"
flattens it.

### 10. Who knows is fixed

Original: "But who knows what other secrets are waiting to be unlocked?"
My version: "But who know what other mysteries are waiting to be opened?"
Model: "But who knows what other secrets are waiting to be unlocked?"

"Who knows" is a fixed phrase: "who" takes the third-person singular verb
even though it asks about a group. "Opened" also loses the "unlocked"
metaphor of the journal's secrets.

### 8. Awkward is not embarrassing

Original: "Awkward sibling hug?"
My version: "Embarrassing sibling embrace?"
Model: "Awkward sibling hug?"

"Awkward" describes the clumsy, uncomfortable feeling of the moment;
"embarrassing" means it actually shames someone. And "embrace" is too
formal for two kids. Keep "awkward" and "hug".

## Patterns I Can Use Today

- "Did somebody say...? What is that, ...?"
- "Gnomes of the forest... Gather together!"
- "Who's giving orders? I need orders!"
- "You saved our butts back there."
- "When you battle side-by-side with someone, you realize they've got your back."
- "Who knows what other secrets are waiting to be unlocked?"

## How This Builds Speaking

This round closes the hard block well: seven of ten natural, with the long
contrastive lesson nearly intact. The two errors are the classic finale
pattern, a small agreement slip and a tense shift under length. Follow up by
reading the taught models aloud, then try making one general-truth sentence
of your own with "when you..., you realize...".
