## Corrections Worth Remembering

### 6. Phase, not phrase

Original: "going through your whole 'boy crazy' phase"
My version: "you are in the phrase that you are really into boys"
Model: "I get that you're going through a 'boy crazy' phase"

"Phase" is a period or stage; "phrase" is a group of words. They are easy to
confuse, but here the meaning needs "phase". Also "going through a phase"
is the fixed pattern, so "in the phrase that" is not English.

### 8. Rephrase, do not repeat

Original: "Just today my mosquito bites spelled out 'beware.'"
My version: (identical)
Model: "My mosquito bites literally spelled out 'beware' today."

The original was copied back. A rephrase needs a real change, like moving
the time word or using "literally".

## Don't Know - Taught Today

### 10. "It's hard to believe + since"

Meaning: 难以置信（这件事）已经持续了这么久。The pattern "it's hard to
believe it's been X since..." emphasizes how long something has lasted.
Model: "It's amazing that I've been studying the strange and wondrous
secrets of Gravity Falls for six years."
Note: you can also say "I can't believe it's been six years since I started
studying..."

## Patterns I Can Use Today

- "The girl about to throw up is my sister."
- "You might wanna know what we're doing..."
- "Trust me, there's a completely reasonable explanation."
- "I get that you're going through a phase, but..."
- "My mosquito bites literally spelled out 'beware' today."

## How This Builds Speaking

Hard-block lines are where meaning, tone, and structure all have to survive
the rephrase, which is exactly what speaking demands in real time. The irony
survived in most lines, which means you are rephrasing the situation, not
just swapping words. Follow up by saying the taught model for sentence 10
aloud, then trying "I can't believe it's been..." about something real.
