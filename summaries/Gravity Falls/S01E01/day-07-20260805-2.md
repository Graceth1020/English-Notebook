## Corrections Worth Remembering

### 1. Overseen is not watched

Original: "I'm being watched."
My version: "I'm being overseen."
Model: "Someone's watching me."

"Oversee" means to supervise work ("oversee a project"). Being "watched"
here means observed or spied on. When the passive feels awkward, switch to
the active form: "Someone's watching me."

### 3. Hit stays hit

Original: "Sheesh! You two get hit by a bus or somethin'?"
My version: "Sheesh! Are you hitted by a bus or something?"
Model: "Sheesh! Did you get hit by a bus or something?"

"Hitted" does not exist. "Hit" is irregular: hit, hit, hit. Use the plain
past participle with "get" or "did".

### 8. Take it easy is not the same as keeping an open mind

Original: "Just keep an open mind. Be cool."
My version: "Just take it easy. Be cool."
Model: "Just stay open-minded. Don't get worked up."

"Take it easy" means relax; "keep an open mind" means be willing to accept
new or strange ideas. The meaning drifted because the idiom was replaced
with a similar-sounding one.

## Don't Know - Taught Today

### 4. "Could use some fresh air"

Meaning: 父母觉得我们需要呼吸点新鲜空气。The pattern is "could use" = "would
benefit from". It is a soft suggestion, not a command, so "made us use" was
too strong.
Model: "Our parents thought we could use some fresh air."

### 7. "A blessing and a curse"

Meaning: 有利也有弊。The idiom describes something with both advantages and
disadvantages.
Model: "My wisdom helps me and hurts me at the same time."

## Patterns I Can Use Today

- "Someone's watching me."
- "Did you get hit by a bus or something?"
- "Do you have to flirt with everyone you meet?"
- "My wisdom helps me and hurts me at the same time."
- "Wouldn't you prefer a doll?"

The grammar focus moved the needle: the always + -ing habit, the conditional,

Follow up by saying the taught sentences aloud, especially "Did you get hit
