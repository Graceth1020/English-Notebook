## Corrections Worth Remembering

### 3. "Back to work" needs a verb

Original: "Well, time to get back to work on my death ray!"
My version: "Well, it's time to back to work on my death ray!"
Model: "Well, it's time to get back to work on my death ray!"

"Back" is an adverb, not a verb, so "to back to work" is incomplete. The
phrase is "get back to work", and "it's time to ..." was a good frame to keep.

### 2. Nobody takes a third-person verb

Original: "Well, when you get to be an old fella like me, nobody pays any attention to you anymore."
My version: "Well, when you become an old fella like me, nobody focus on you anymore."
Model: "Well, when you become an old fella like me, nobody focuses on you anymore."

"Nobody" acts like a singular subject, so the verb needs "-s" ("focuses").
"Become" was a good substitute for "get to be".

### 7. "Forbidden" is too formal here

Original: "You can look... But you can't touch."
My version: "You can look... But touch is forbidden."
Model: "You can look... But no touching."

The original is a casual rule; "forbidden" sounds like a museum sign. In
everyday speech you would say "no touching" or "you can't touch".

## Common Phrases and Fixed Structures

- "So what's the plan?" / "or what?" question frame: "So what's the plan? Throw more cameras overboard or what?"
- "when you get to be ... nobody ... anymore": "When you get to be an old fella like me, nobody pays any attention to you anymore."
- "time to get back to ...": "Well, time to get back to work on my death ray!"
- "So... did you ever ...?": "So... did you ever talk to your son about how you felt?"
- "Not worth it?" / "worth it": "Not worth it? Guys, imagine what would happen if we got that picture!"
- "There's no way we're gonna ...": "There's no way we're gonna miss this."
- "You can look... but you can't touch." contrast frame
- "There has to be a way out of this." / "There must be a way out."
- "I'm not sure that's a good idea." / "I don't think it's a good idea."
- "If you don't ..., I'm ..." conditional threat: "If you don't leave right now, I'm calling the police!"

## How This Builds Speaking

register choices like "forbidden". Read the model versions aloud three
times, then plant "There's no way ..." and "If you don't ..., I'm ..." into
two sentences of your own before the next round.
