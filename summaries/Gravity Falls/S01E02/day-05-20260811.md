## Corrections Worth Remembering

### 3. "I'm agree" breaks the verb

Original: "Dipper, I am one million percent onboard with this!"
My version: "Dipper, I'm totally agree with this."
Model: "Dipper, I'm one million percent on board with this!"

"Agree" is a verb, so it cannot follow "I'm" (am). Say "I agree" or use the
adjective form "I'm on board with this". And keep the hyperbole: "one
million percent" is the joke, "totally" is not.

### 6. The praise was for the decision, not the performance

Original: "How right you were to do so. He looked like a real piece of work."
My version: "You've done it correctly. He looked like a real piece of work."
Model: "You were so right to do it. He looked like a real piece of work."

"How right you were to do so" celebrates a good judgment call. "You've done
it correctly" praises how something was carried out, which moves the meaning.
The exclamation frame "how + adjective + subject + verb" is the structure to
keep.

### 7. Rebuild the echo question instead of copying it

Original: "I think there might be a cave behind there! 'Might' be?!"
My version: "I think there might be a cave behind there! 'Might' be?!"
Model: "I think there could be a cave back there! 'Could' be?!"

The joke is the echo: repeating one word back in disbelief. Swapping the
word ("might" -> "could") is enough to make the pattern yours, and it is the
same move for lines 1, 4, and 5.

## Common Phrases and Fixed Structures

- "What did I tell you about + -ing?" rhetorical scolding: "What did I tell you about scaring my customers?"
- "chop/cut ... up to smithereens" / "tear ... to shreds": "It chopped my boat up to smitheroons!"
- Hyperbole "one million percent + on board with": "I am one million percent onboard with this!"
- "This is exactly why ...": "You see? This is exactly why you need backup cameras."
- Pun frame "still misses me, but her aim is gettin' better": both meanings of "miss" in one joke
- "How right you were to ...": "How right you were to do so."
- "a real piece of work": "He looked like a real piece of work."
- Echo question: "I think there might be a cave behind there! 'Might' be?!"
- "In retrospect / in hindsight, it seems a bit ...": "In retrospect, it seems a bit contrived."
- "You just don't know the lengths ... go through": "You just don't know the lengths us old-timers go through..."
- Bet frame "Five bucks says ..." / "I bet ... that ...": "Five more bucks says you can't do it..."
- "At the top of my lungs": "...plus me singing at the top of my lungs!"

## How This Builds Speaking

Jokes and fixed chunks only help your speech when you can rebuild them, not
just recognize them; copying the original reads them off the page instead of
recalling them from memory. The two natural rephrases (lines 2 and 9) show
the target: keep the meaning and the tone, change the words. Read the model
versions aloud three times, then plant "one million percent" and "a real
piece of work" into two sentences of your own before the next round.
