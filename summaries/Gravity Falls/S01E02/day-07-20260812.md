## Corrections Worth Remembering

### 7. "You two" is plural

Original: "I guess the real lake monster is you two."
My version: "I guess you two is the real lake monster."
Model: "I guess you two are the real lake monster."

"You two" behaves like "you": it takes "are", not "is". The agreement slip
is easy to miss because the sentence starts with "the real lake monster is".

### 1. "High and mighty" is the taunt phrase

Original: "Not so high and mighty anymore."
Model: "Not so full of yourself anymore, huh?"

"High and mighty" means acting superior and self-important. The whole line
is a comeback to someone whose pride has just been deflated, and "full of
yourself" carries the same jab in everyday speech.

### 10. "Conflict" sounds like a war

Original: "I had a run-in with the lake police!"
My version: "I had a conflict with the lake police!"
Model: "I had some trouble with the lake police!"

"Run-in" means a brief, annoying clash, not a serious dispute. "Conflict" is
formal and heavy for this line, so keep it casual with "run-in" or "trouble
with".

## Common Phrases and Fixed Structures

- "Not so high and mighty anymore." / "Not so full of yourself anymore, huh?"
- Deadpan prediction frame: "You're a side character and you die in the first five minutes of the movie."
- "As + title, I authorize/grant ...": "As co-captain, I grant that request."
- Deadpan claim frame "normally + like + noun": "No, my stomach normally sounds like whale noises."
- "I don't often do this, but ...": "I don't often do this, but I feel the need to give you an award."
- "We should be + -ing" (as it happens): "Dude, we should be writing this down."
- "I guess the real + noun + is ...": "I guess the real lake monster is you two."
- "Spend + time + -ing" / "track down": "We spent all day trying to track down a legendary dinosaur."
- "Save your sympathy!" / "Don't waste your pity on me!"
- "A run-in with ...": "I had a run-in with the lake police!"

## How This Builds Speaking

Short punchlines are the easiest to copy and the most worth rebuilding,
because one swapped chunk ("find" -> "track down", "sympathy" -> "pity")
makes the line yours. The single grammar slip ("you two is") shows how
agreement bends under pressure, and the taught idioms ("high and mighty",
whale-noise deadpan) are comedy chunks you can reuse. Read the model
versions aloud three times, then plant "I guess the real + noun + is" and
"I don't often do this, but ..." into two sentences of your own before the
next round.
