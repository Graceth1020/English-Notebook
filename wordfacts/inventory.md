# Wordfacts Inventory

Word-level grammar facts the learner violates - countability, transitivity,
and later other word-level patterns. This is the layer the other tracks do not
own: chunks are expressions, patterns are sentence structures, error rows are
epidemiology, form axes are instruments. This bank is the medicine.

**Corpus-only.** Entries come from real errors or explicit asks - never from
generic "common uncountable nouns" lists. Those become a wordlist to memorize,
which is the recognition-level habit the whole practice exists to break.

**Status:** `open` -> `drilled` (quiz-correct) -> `owned` (produced correctly,
unprompted, in daily chat). A violation in chat after `drilled` sends the entry
back to `open`. Quiz proves recognition; only chat production proves recall.

Pilot phase: hand-maintained. If the track earns its keep, it gets a logging
script and a site importer, same as the other tracks.
