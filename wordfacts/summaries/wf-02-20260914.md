# Wordfacts Session 02 - transitivity transfer test

Date: 2026-09-14 | Category: transitivity | Items: 10 | Score: 10/10

## Why This Was Run

Session 01 scored 10/10, but the learner challenged its validity: five items
were his own corrected errors (one only two days old) and two were built from
stories he had just told, so the score might reflect memory of the corrections
rather than transitivity knowledge. The criticism is methodologically correct -
the same confound applies to the original 12/12 article diagnostic.

Session 02 is the control group: six target verbs he has never been corrected
on (discuss, mention, complete, reach, contact, demonstrate), in new contexts,
plus four traps - two plain intransitives (arrive, fail) and two subtle ones
(answer, understand) that are transitive in other frames, testing whether he
judges the slot rather than memorising the verb.

## What It Showed

10/10 on unseen material, including both subtle traps. The strongest evidence
is items 9-10: `answer` and `understand` are transitive in other frames, and he
correctly judged that THESE frames need nothing. He is not recalling which verb
is transitive - he is evaluating whether the slot needs filling, per frame.
The memory-of-corrections objection is answered: the knowledge transfers.

Combined with session 01 and the article diagnostic, three clean instruments
now say the same thing: **for slot-filling knowledge (determiners, objects,
verb transitivity), recognition and judgement are complete, and the failures
are production-time only.** The corpus-only rule holds: none of these six verbs
enters the bank, because entries come from real errors, and he has none here.

The remaining instrument for `chat:E055` is the self-check variant - whether he
can catch dropped objects in his own composed text on a second pass. Session
02's result removes the last alternative explanation and is logged as the
supporting evidence for extending that variant beyond `chat:E031`.
