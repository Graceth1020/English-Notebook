## Why This Was Run

`chat:E055` (object dropped after a transitive verb) has 8 hits, and Drill 06
round 2 added `to assign` with a self-correction one sentence later - the
signature of knowledge that exists but does not fire under production. The
article gap had its own diagnostic (12 items, 12/12, proving no knowledge
gap). Objects did not have one. This session is that diagnostic.

All 10 items from the learner's own corpus: 5 verbatim error sentences, 2
derived from his Day 14 stories, 3 clean intransitive traps (end / wait /
happen). Instruction: fill the slot, or say nothing is needed.

## What It Showed

10/10, traps included. The result pattern-matches the article diagnostic
exactly: 12/12 there, 10/10 here. **There is no knowledge gap for object
drop.** He knows `test` needs an object, knows `end` does not, and can fill or
clear every slot when the task is the slot itself.

The two diagnostics now say the same thing about two different slots
(determiner, object): the knowledge is complete; what fails is retrieval
during composition, when attention is on content. Chinese deletes recoverable
slot-fillers and English does not, and no amount of quizzing changes that -
recognition is already perfect.

Consequence: conventional quizzes on this material are finished as an
instrument. What remains untested is the second pass - whether he can catch
his own dropped objects after composing, when the content load is gone. That
is the self-check variant, currently reserved for `chat:E031` in the form-drill
rules. This result is the argument for extending it to `chat:E055`.
