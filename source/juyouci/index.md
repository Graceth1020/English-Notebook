Standalone flashcard decks extracted from real English transcripts, matched to an
exam level. Each chunk opens as its own full-screen study page with IPA, a real
source sentence and a Chinese translation; progress is saved per chunk in this
browser. These pages carry their own app shell, so the site header and footer are
not shown on the study screen — use the browser back button or the cards below to
return here.
